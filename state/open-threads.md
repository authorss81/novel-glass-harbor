# Open Threads

No chapter thread has been activated yet. These are the controlled promises inherited from the approved planning stack.

## Opening batch promises

- **Ordinary work and cost:** Calder's first chapter must show a paid repair job, debt pressure, and why safe equipment matters before the glass mystery takes over.
- **Legitimate claim:** the shard must arise from physical salvage labor; Calder cannot win the claim by stealing Crown property or relying on Oren's private connection.
- **First echo cost:** Calder's initial tide-ear entry must use an anchor, tender, air limit, and physical cutoff; aftertaste and hearing confusion must affect Chapters 6–10.
- **Mara's independence:** she can test glass provenance but cannot enter the echo, certify truth, or become Calder's assistant.
- **Batch loss:** Kest confiscates the original shard. The crew preserves measurements and a public provenance duplicate rather than keeping the object through a convenient loophole.
- **Earned forward pull:** the *Vigil* arrives with Ilya Serr's senior examiner seal, placing Calder's evidence under naval seizure pressure while the shard's old route remains unexplained.

## Central mystery, staged by volume

- **Volumes 1–3:** Why does the Gannet evidence contradict the physical cargo handling, and why do the transparent wrecks form a route rather than a scatter?
- **Volumes 4–6:** Who changed civic custody into Crown ownership, and what evidence proves the Quietening was a forced-consensus cascade followed by a deliberate civic scuttling?
- **Volumes 7–10:** How did the Dominion edit charts and rebuild the Crown Seal, and how many communities must Ilya's network connect before activation?
- **Volumes 11–14:** Can independently governed routes resist synchronization without filling every gap, surrendering restricted testimony, or recreating centralized control?
- **Volume 15:** The historical answer is already planned, but Oren's context changes at the midpoint: the evacuation bell had sounded and a second rescue line was active when he cut. His visible hand movement cannot prove what he understood, so the uncertainty defeats any attempt to make one witness a clean symbol; the final authority choice remains for the climax.

## Character promises

- Alden's original repair log and partial confession remain unrevealed until the middle volumes.
- Oren's stored choices must remain incomplete and occasionally resistant to Calder's preferred interpretation; no resurrection or obedient final message.
- Calder's full Second Bell license is not a Volume 1 payoff. Volume 1 settles the first recovery claim; Volume 4 removes his eligibility for the full examination.
- The Mara relationship progresses through professional equality and public accountability. The Volume 12 marriage is a chosen life, not a plot reward.

## Intentionally open at the ending

- The precise origin of tideglass remains uncertain.
- Some merged echoes may develop behavior beyond their witnesses; the ending does not prove the sea has a conscious will.
- The Dominion survives as a diminished political power, so the new public network can fail and be repaired in ordinary human ways.
