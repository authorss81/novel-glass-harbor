- Oren Marr is Calder's older brother. He was twenty when the *Aster Gannet* sank thirteen years ago, making him thirty-three now.
- The ferry sank during the *Vigil*'s seizure with sixty-three workers aboard. The public account blames an illegal crossing; the surviving pressure log contains a deliberate three-second gap.
- The public record is a set of separate documents that were never meant to be read together: the harbor **salvage report** Alden signed, which omitted the sealed case; the **Navy seizure report**, which denies the ferry's hold was ever occupied and denies the cutter's own case needed declaration; a **Registry custody log** recording the evidence as uncontested; and the **public pressure log and ferry survey** copied into the inquest record, which show a three-second gap and record the compartment's dimensions. Calder sees only the public version at the opening, and he has read all of it.
- **Clue availability for the compartment fact, one canonical answer:** the *Gannet* survey and its compartment figures are public from the opening, so nothing is being withheld from the reader or from Calder. What Calder cannot do at the opening is *use* them, because proving the compartment was too large to have been empty requires the sealed case's own dimensions, and those sit in Navy-held custody papers he cannot read. Chapter 9's joinery match supplies the case's type and approximate size and makes the contradiction testable without proving it. Only the Volume 1 midpoint supplies the physical measurement that proves the compartment could not have been empty. No document, character, or forum may treat the compartment as proven before that.
- **The unaccounted plate note:** the repairer's note about a plate cut from the *Gannet*'s iron plating appears in the public copy of Alden's signed harbor salvage report, which Calder has read. It concerns the ferry's hull iron, not transparent glass, and it must never be fused with the weathered transparent hull plate lifted in Chapter 4. Alden will not discuss the report, and Calder does not yet know what Alden left out of it. The note authenticates no object, does not identify the shard's origin, and cannot be used to authenticate the Chapter 4 plate as a *Gannet* artifact. Its pay-off belongs to Volume 4, where the page removed from the Gannet file accounts for the plate that was never accounted for. Alden's own partial confession is a separate, later thread: it begins in Volume 9 and is completed in Volume 15 with the original repair log, so the two are never collapsed into one scene.
- Alden is not yet ready to confess, and Calder does not yet know what Alden omitted.
- Ilya Serr was a junior Registry official during the Gannet Cut. At the opening he is a fifty-six-year-old senior Lantern Registry examiner. He becomes the active central antagonist in Volume 4, is promoted to Minister of Returning Memory at the close of Volume 8, and holds the office from Volume 9 onward. Do not call him minister in Volumes 1–8, and do not describe him as the central antagonist before Volume 4.

## Institutions and jurisdiction

- The **Morrow Quay harbor authority**, working from the reeve's office on the west basin, issues claim windows, work permits, and one-season provisional working charters, and can order a wreck held for inspection. It cannot certify a glass witness or overrule a Registry seizure.
- **Three boundaries, never blurred:** the **harbor line** runs along the mole and the inner shoals and lets harbor crews work any wreck inside it for a share; the **imperial line** is the Crown's outer-shelf boundary, beyond the harbor line, and working past it needs the full Second Bell license; the **one-season provisional working charter** is the harbor authority's wider seasonal permission and is the first instrument that lets a crew work beyond a single claim. In Volume 1, every dive stays inside the harbor line under a claim window or a **work permit** that names a site and dates. No chapter in Volume 1 may put an unlicensed Calder past the imperial line, because the one-season charter is not granted until Movement 5 and the full license is a Volume 4 question. The *Vigil*'s naval salvage claim in Movement 2 bites precisely because it sits over the same shoal and past the line the crew cannot cross.
- The **Lantern Registry** certifies provenance and custody, not truth. Mara works from the **Morrow circuit office**, whose copying room holds the public pressure log and the copies.
- The **Crown Navy** asserts salvage authority at sea. Keep the Registry, the harbor authority, and the Navy distinct in every scene, with distinct documents.
- The **Free Keel** shares tools, rescue plans, and legal risk among Morrow Quay's independent crews. It has no leader and is frequently broke.
- The **Office of Returning Memory** is the ministry; Ilya's seal represents it in Volume 1 through a seizure order, not through his presence.

## License and Volume 1 resolution

- Calder wins a disputed claim window, not ownership.

Both are ordinary iron, neither is glass, and neither is touched by the seizure. The crew keeps the freed shoal iron; the drifted mole anchor goes back to the harbor authority as municipal tackle.
