## The three files, and what each one is for

- **`state/current.md` is a log, not a brief.** Newest entry first, one entry per phase, and the entries grow to hundreds of lines. **Read the head entry only, which is the repair pass against `logs/batch-0001.review.log`. If a figure you want is not in it, it belongs in a continuity section and not in that file.**
- **`state/continuity.md` is the state of the world.** Newest section first. **Read L1 and `outline/volume-14.md`, and that is the whole of what a writer of Chapters 671 to 700 needs from state. Read the canon blocks once and search for them by their headings: the line numbers for them have moved at every repair since this index was written, and the heading does not move.**
- **`state/open-threads.md` is what is owed.** Newest batch first, then every older batch's threads, and the long-standing threads further down. **Read the newest section at the head, which is this pass, and then the eight-item hand-over immediately below it, and then the long-standing cross-volume threads once. THE HEAD ALSO NAMES § 2, WHICH IS THE ONE DEBT THAT IS NOT A FIGURE, AND § *Flagged for the controller*, WHICH IS THE ONE PLACE THE LEDGER GAP IS STATED.**
- **`state/chapter-summaries.md` is for the voice and for the figures of a batch.** **The Volume 14 hand-over for Chapters 651 to 670 is at the head of that file and is the next; read those two, and not the three thousand other lines.** The Volume 13 sections are under them and are the record of what thirteen volumes did.

## What this index exists to stop

A phase that read all four files whole would spend most of a model context on Volumes 01 to 11, every one of which is closed, audited and ruled, and would arrive at the active volume with no budget left for the ten chapters it has to write. The repair pass over `logs/batch-0005.review.log` recorded that the previous phase wrote forty-three thousand and seven words for ten chapters and left roughly this much state behind, and that a large and growing share of a batch's output budget is being spent restating what earlier batches already said.

**THE LINE NUMBERS IN THE FOUR FILES HAVE MOVED AT EVERY REPAIR SINCE THIS INDEX WAS WRITTEN AND THEY WILL MOVE AGAIN AT THE NEXT ONE. THE ENTRY POINT IS WHAT IS FIXED HERE, NOT THE NUMBERS: A PHASE THAT READS THE HEAD OF `state/continuity.md` AND SEARCHES THE REST BY SECTION HEADING HAS A BUDGET FOR ITS CHAPTERS. THE FOUR FILES ARE NOT TRIMMED HERE, BECAUSE TRIMMING A STATE FILE IS AN AUTHOR-LEVEL DECISION AND NOT A REPAIR PASS'S. THE TABLE OF CLOSED VOLUMES FURTHER DOWN CARRIES OLDER NUMBERS AND IS INFORMATIONAL; A PHASE THAT NEEDS ONE OF THEM SHOULD SEARCH BY THE SECTION HEADING RATHER THAN BY LINE.**
