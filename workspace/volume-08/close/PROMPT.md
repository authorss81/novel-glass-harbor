A COMPANY OF BOATS IS NO USE AT ALL EXCEPT IN THE WATER SOMEBODY IS LOOKING AFTER BETWEEN IT AND THE NEXT TIDE, PAID AS A BEGINNING AND NOT AS A STAGE, WITH FOUR THINGS ON A LEAF AND A FIFTH THING THAT IS A PERSON AND IS NOT ON IT, AND A WAGE OF A MARK AND EIGHTPENCE AND A DAY AND A MAN WHO IS THERE ON A TUESDAY, AND SIX MASTERS TAKEN IN ON A PAPERS QUESTION AND ANSWERED ON A LINE THAT ASKED THEM NOTHING. AND THEN ABOUT ELEVEN MASTERS ASKED ONE AT A TIME IN THE SAME WORDS, FOUR OF THEM SAYING NO WITH A REASON AND A MAN'S DAY AS THE COST; SEVEN BOATS OUT ON A FLOOD ON A BREACH OF THE FIRST CLAUSE AND BACK ON THE SIXTH; THE ELEVEN WEEKS READ ALOUD IN A PUBLIC ROOM IN THE ORDER SIGNED WITH THE FORM'S SHAPE READ AFTER THEM AND NOT BEFORE; A TRANSPORT OF ABOUT A HUNDRED AND FORTY TONS CUT, STOVED AND HOLED ON THE REACH, WITH NOBODY HURT, NOBODY NAMED AND NOBODY COUNTED; THE WORK ON THAT REACH STOPPING AT ELEVEN BECAUSE THIRTY MEN HEARD A WORD AT THE SAME MINUTE FROM ONE MOUTH; THE MAN WITH THE BOARD UNDER HIS ARM WALKING AWAY FROM IT; A SQUADRON THAT LOST A SHIP OUT OF THAT REACH; THE ADMISSION SAID ONCE ON A MOLE IN ABOUT SIXTY PEOPLE'S HEARING AND NOT IMPROVED ON AND NOT COMFORTED; A PAPER ON A CUSTODY ROOM DOOR NAMING TWO AS PERMITTED AND ONE AS PRESENT, WITH A FIGURE THAT IS A TIME AND A PLACE AND AN ARRIVAL AND IS THE ONLY FIGURE IN FIFTY CHAPTERS THAT IS NOT A PRICE ANYBODY PAID; A NEW SEAL LINE NAMING A NEW OFFICE, IN NO MOUTH, WITH NOBODY IN A ROOM; A NINE-LINE SHEET IN A FRONT STREET SINCE THE FOURTEENTH OF AUGUST THAT IS THE BEST PAPER THE CROWN PRODUCES IN THIS VOLUME AND IS ANSWERED BY NOBODY; A QUESTION ASKED ONCE IN A ROOM WITH A CLERK IN IT AND ANSWERED NOWHERE; AND A COMMITTED RELATIONSHIP BEGUN ONCE ON A MOLE AND NOT SEALED AND NOT A MARRIAGE.**

**AND THE LAST LINE OF THE VOLUME IS THE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-FIFTH LINE OF A FOURTH COLUMN THAT IS NOT CLOSED, AND THE LAST THING THE VOLUME SAYS IS WHAT A HAND AT THE FOOT OF A SHEET IS NOT, AND A HARBOR POORER ON THE FIFTIETH CHAPTER THAN ON THE FIRST WITH NO FLOUR IN ITS MARKET AND A LIGHT OUT AND A BAG OF ONE MARK AND FORTY-THREE WEEKS OF A WAGE FALLEN DUE WITH NO ENVELOPE AGAINST ANY OF THEM, AND THE CHEAPEST THING IN IT WENT UP ON THE FIRST OF JUNE AND DID NOT COME BACK DOWN.**
