## Shape of the ten chapters

Spine, not cage; move a beat a chapter if prose improves, provided all land and the batch closes the volume: **charter granted, share kept, bell-buoy core recovered, Oren's first minutes surfaced publicly at the cost of a childhood memory of his voice, the *Vigil*'s attention earned, the seizure's reason still undisclosed, and the Oren mark still hidden.**

A workable spine, with the dates the calendar gives you:

1. **The purpose, and the twenty-second.** Monday the 21st and Tuesday the 22nd. Calder writes the purpose for the 28th. Kest reads it into the record with the door shut on the 22nd — the same day the conservator's second note is dated, and the two documents arrive in the same week and do not know about each other. 2. **The Navy's paper.** The *Vigil* answers the charter question inside its month, and it asks for something the yard must refuse.
3. **How a man gets a steel on that ground.** The batch's first problem, and the compartment's midpoint. Not a heist, not a night dive, not a ground violation. The lawful ways are few and one of them is a document the yard does not have.
4. **The tenth of December.** Bevin Rook back in the water under a tender who is not him, with forty marks owed and a crew reorganized around a hand that no longer shapes the work.
5. **The recovery.** The bell-buoy core, worked as a job with air, rope, money, weather and a crew, under a named instrument, at a real cost in money and body and time.
6. **The fragment.** Oren's first minutes, in a room, under a stated reason, with a named tender, a fixed limit and a cutoff — **and the compartment's measurement, which is a reversal and not a climax.**
7. **The cost of the public route.** Calder surfaces the fragment through a public route rather than keeping it privately, and loses a specific childhood memory of Oren's voice. This is the volume's climax and it must be felt in the body, not summarized.
8. **The 28th.** The charter is granted, the share is kept, the accounts are read out, and the *Vigil*'s attention lands.
9. **The record.** The public record showing the cutter's claim and the shard on the same shoal and the manifest unreliable — as records, with their limits, not as a ruling — and the reason for the seizure still undisclosed.
10. **Slack water.** The volume's last chapter. The core answers with the delayed note; the fragment is in the custody room under a claim Calder cannot withdraw; the public duplicate is on the market wall with the crew's names on it; and Calder hears an adult version of a childhood call he can no longer remember, and chooses the next licensed run.

**Do not let the ten chapters be ten speeches.** Batch 0004's own review finding was that seven of ten were rooms, ledgers and noticeboards, and its two over-length chapters were two hearing rooms. **Alternate the pressure deliberately: paperwork, water, a body, a market, an office, a family, a boy, an argument in a room.** Do not open two hearings in one day again.
