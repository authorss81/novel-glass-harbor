## WHAT YOUR FOURTEEN DAYS ARE, AND THE LOCKS THAT RULE THEM

1. **A ROUTE FAILS BECAUSE A SUPPOSEDLY WILLING WITNESS WAS COERCED, AND THE SEAM SHOWS DIFFERENCE AND NOT TRUTH.** The seam cannot prove the route good or the rest of the coast good, so the route closes and the closing is entered in that community's own hand. No chapter calls it a traitor's route.
2. **A SECOND ROUTE SUCCEEDS ONLY AFTER A NEGOTIATED COMPROMISE WITH AN OUT-OF-DATE CURRENT.** The pilot remembers truly and the sea has moved; the solution is compromise and not perfect memory.
3. **THE FOUR HEADS ARE EXERCISED FOR THE FIRST TIME ON 719 AND 722, AT THE SAME SIZE AS THE REST.** Claims and public correction on 719; refusals and restricted records on 722. No fifth head is added on account of anything.
4. **THE SEIZURE TRUTH COMES OUT ON A PUBLIC BELL AND THE ROUTE CARRYING IT STARTS TO COME APART WHILE STILL TRUE.** No chapter fixes it by arguing with the document; the opposition is nine times given by nine communities.
5. **THE COUNT OF THOSE WHO WENT HOME IS TAKEN ONCE ON 726 AT ELEVEN, OFF A PAGE OF DAYS, NOT SAID IN A ROOM AND NOT IN THE SLIP.** It is departures and never refusals, never disagreements, never people who were wrong.
6. **THE PAGE OF HANDS THAT RANG IS NOT RE-TAKEN AND THE PAGE OF ROUTES IS NOT COUNTED IN YOUR FOURTEEN DAYS.** The hands sheet stands with thirty; the routes stand at two held, three restricted, four dormant.
