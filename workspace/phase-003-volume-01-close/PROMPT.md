## Part 3 — The locks a Volume 02 writer will inherit, verified before you plan against them

Each of these is on the page or in a state file. **Open the chapter and confirm it yourself.** If any is wrong, repair it in the chapter and say so in the audit; do not plan Volume 02 on top of a wrong lock.

1. **The calendar.** 14 November was a Monday, 1 December a Thursday, 26 December a Monday. Therefore: **6 December Tue, 8 Thu, 10 Sat, 13 Tue, 14 Wed, 17 Sat, 20 Tue, 21 Wed, 25 Sun, 26 Mon; 1 January a Sunday; 4 January a Wednesday; 19 January a Thursday; 1 March a Wednesday.** Twenty-six December to the first of March is **nine weeks and two days**. **The harbor authority's clerk wipes the hearing room's slate wall every Monday at eight**; the wall was last wiped on Monday the nineteenth of December and is wiped again on the twenty-sixth, so the ledger is the record and the wall is a convenience.
2. **The money chain, which is one chain and is checkable in the prose.** Five marks in the bag on the 13th of November. The mole job of **the 16th to the 18th** pays thirty-four and spends **twenty** — the harbor's eighth 3, the harbor's share 3, rope and the day's food 3, five marks of air, six on the chandler's note — and closes the bag at **nineteen**. The certified copy of the hearing record costs **three**, entered against the nineteen on the 23rd and **paid at the copying room on the first**. The first of December spends **eleven** on air. **Nineteen less three less eleven is five, and the bag holds five from the first of December to the end of the volume, and nothing has gone into it since the 18th of November. The tin has been empty all batch.** The chandler's note took **six marks in the three days of that job and none since**, is still written up in the chandler's own book as *eleven weeks into six*, is thirteen weeks by the yard's own count, and **the chandler has never been asked and neither has Calder.**
3. **The delayed note: heard many times across Volume 01, logged eight** — the 26th of October, the 2nd and 4th of November, the 8th (shared with a naval rating's slate), the 14th, the 23rd of November, the 6th and the 26th of December. 4. **The concealment at the volume boundary.** The Oren mark is **in no record, in no office, in no book, and not in Kest's chart notation.** What exists on the record is the claim and the refusal to name: a boy's fifteen seconds and no name in the ledger; a slate in the crew's book; four *not stated* entries and a hand that declined to fill in the fifth on a conservator's certificate.
