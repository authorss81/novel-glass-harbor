## L6. THE APPARATUS, AND THE FIGURES THE CHAPTERS PRINT

Each chapter carries, in this order: the chapter number; an italic subtitle made of that day's own figures and hours; the dateline paragraph; **a bold block of the day's figures and of what is standing**; **the nine day-counters, the hearing, and two or three standing items and nothing else**; the scene, in paragraphs of two to six sentences, separated by rules; **a bold closing block**; a rule and a rule; and then the leaf's *first column* and *second column* entries in the yard's own mouth, in italics, with an hour against each. **The first column takes one entry a day and the day's own entry goes in after that day's reading. The second column takes five lines a day, three before the hour of that day's reading and two after it, and is not checkable from a mole, and a reason entered in the second column of one chapter is not restated in the second column of another. No two chapters of this batch may carry the same standing block. The standing paragraph carries the nine day-counters and the hearing and two or three standing items and nothing else. The second person is at ZERO in the narration and in the apparatus INCLUDING EVERY QUOTED PASSAGE, except inside a mouth, where a person may of course say you.** **A QUESTION THAT WAS SAID OUT LOUD AND THAT THE LEDGER HAS TO CARRY IS RENDERED IN THE THIRD PERSON IN A COLUMN, BECAUSE THE APPARATUS HAS NO MOUTHS IN IT.**

**THE COUNTS THAT MUST COME BACK ZERO ON EVERY ONE OF THE TEN CHAPTERS: a four-digit year; the second person in the narration and in the apparatus; the question of Chapter 550 in any form; the question of Chapter 600; *singular*; *chorus*; the words listed at L4; the forbidden panel constructions *not a lie, not a silence, not a refusal, anybody's shame*; a named body; a casualty figure; a fourth head; a fifth head; a roster of anybody who put nothing in; the date of the fourth column's reason; the words *a day in the summer*; a read-back of that leaf; the words for a gift. QUOTE PARITY IS EVEN ON EVERY FILE. EVERY CLOCK EVALUATES. EVERY COUNTED THING A STRANGER WITH A PENCIL CAN CHECK PASSES.**

**AND A PRINTED WORD-LENGTH EQUALS THE PASSAGE IT COUNTS, MEASURED AND NOT ESTIMATED: COUNT EACH PASSAGE OFF THE PAGE BEFORE YOU PRINT ITS LENGTH, AND IF A LENGTH IS A PERSON'S OWN ESTIMATE INSIDE THE WORLD, SAY SO IN THE WORDS THAT MAKE IT ONE. **A WORD-LENGTH IS CHECKED ONLY AGAINST A PASSAGE THAT IS PRINTED ON THE PAGE. WHERE THE COUNTED SPEECH IS REPORTED AND NOT PRINTED, THE COUNT IS NOT CHECKED AND IS NOT A FAULT.** A COUNT is one figure with one name: the nine words under the copied rule are nine words, the same nine words, and not a second count of something else. AND A PAGE CARRIES ONE SET OF NAMES: there are two pages of hours in that shed, the first carries two names, and the second carries three, and no chapter may put a third name on the first.**

**THE DUPLICATE SWEEP MUST BE RUN OVER THE SCENE PROSE OF EVERY CHAPTER IN THIS BATCH AND NOT OVER THE SECOND COLUMNS ALONE, BECAUSE A SWEEP SCOPED TO THE APPARATUS CANNOT SEE A DUPLICATED SCENE PARAGRAPH.
