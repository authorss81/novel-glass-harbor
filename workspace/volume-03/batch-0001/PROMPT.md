## The shape rule, corrected

**The rule "no chapter is two rooms" is WITHDRAWN. Do not carry it forward as a rule and do not enforce it against yourself.** Batch 0005 had three — Chapter 92 the shed and the market, Chapter 98 the net loft, the mole's steps and the shed, and Chapter 100 the boards and the dock.

**The rule that replaces it:**

- **The pressure types alternate deliberately** — local work, relationship pressure, training, investigation, political or institutional pressure, the larger mystery, recovery, and the volume's climax and resolution.
- **No cost is paid twice and every date in a chapter belongs to one day.**
- **A chapter that wants to be a hearing room and a market is split across two days, with a body or a market or a boy in one of them.**
- **A chapter that is a rescue and a hearing is split across two days.**
- **The hearing room and the market wall are never the same day.** Chapter 108 is a market and it is a Thursday.
- **Ten speeches are not ten chapters.**
- **Length: 2,200–3,200 words a chapter.** Where a scene will not compress, **let it run rather than drop a cost the crew pays.** A chapter long because it is one complete room is a different fault from a chapter long because it is two.

## The ten chapters

Follow `outline/batches/volume-03-batch-0001.md` card by card. In summary:

- **101 — Four Days.** Thursday the fourth and Friday the fifth of May. The clock runs from the day of the finding, the return was due on the second, and the yard's first return is **two days late** before a word of it is written — and it is a return about the yard's own bucket.
