## The shape rule

- **The pressure types alternate deliberately** — action, character conflict, training, investigation, political or institutional pressure, the larger mystery, recovery, and the civic record — **and the movement's own shape is a choice between a crew and an archive, a cutter and a public record, a boy with a hand and a person with a pencil, so the ten chapters should not be ten arguments in rooms.**
- **The rule "no chapter is two rooms" was WITHDRAWN and is not carried forward. Do not enforce it against yourself.** Chapters 92, 98 and 100 of Volume 02 are the precedent. **The rule that replaces it: no cost is paid twice and every date in a chapter belongs to one day. A man may be in three rooms in one day. A man may not be paid for two days in one chapter, and no day may be in a chapter twice.**
- **A chapter that wants to be a hearing room and a market is split across two days.**
- **The hearing room and the market wall are never the same day, this batch uses the hearing room at most once and the market wall at most once, and Chapter 150 is owed the market wall because the volume's final image is a page going up on it.** The market wall currently carries the naval paper of the eighteenth of March, the sheet of the seventh of June with a woman's name and a figure and a date on it, the yard's tenth thing, Perry Ollett's printed sheet, and the yard's own fourth return.
- **Length: 2,200–3,200 words a chapter.** Where a scene will not compress, **let it run rather than drop a cost the crew pays.** **A chapter long because it is one complete room is a different fault from a chapter long because it is two, and a chapter long because it is one person's bad wheel and a walk to a sluice is a third fault, and that is the one to watch for.** Batch 0004 ran long in four chapters and every one of them ran because a cost could not be dropped; say so in your report if yours do.
- **The read-out at the head of the dock is the yard's own practice and it is load-bearing, and the Batch 0003 and 0004 reviews both found that ten identical same-order inventories read as a habit rather than a practice. STAGE IT IN FULL IN TWO OR THREE CHAPTERES AND NOT IN ALL TEN, and never let two of them carry the same list twice.**
- **THE CLIMAX IS A WORKING DAY AND IT HAS TO BE ONE. IT IS NOT A SCENE IN A ROOM AND IT CANNOT BE A SCENE IN A ROOM.** The wrong, the testimony, the boy's failure, the posting and the final page are separate days and may not be stacked on the day of the choice.
