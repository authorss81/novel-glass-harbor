## The shape rule

**The rule "no chapter is two rooms" is WITHDRAWN. Do not carry it forward and do not enforce it against yourself.** Chapters 92, 98 and 100 of Volume 02 are the precedent: each is **one day** in two or three rooms, and a single-day multi-room chapter is allowed and is not a fault, and no reviewer may re-raise the withdrawn rule against this batch.

**The rule that replaces it:**

- **The pressure types alternate deliberately** — local work, relationship pressure, training, investigation, political or institutional pressure, the larger mystery, recovery — and the batch card's pressure table is the plan, not an accident.
- **No cost is paid twice and every date in a chapter belongs to one day.** A man may be in three rooms in one day. **A man may not be paid for two days in one chapter, and no day may be in a chapter twice.**
- **A chapter that wants to be a hearing room and a market is split across two days.**
- **The hearing room and the market wall are never the same day.** This batch uses the hearing room at most once and the market wall at most once, and never on the same day. **The card puts the market wall in Chapter 116 on Wednesday the seventh of June; that is a market in daylight, not a Monday and not a hearing-room day, and it is the batch's one use.**
- **Ten speeches are not ten chapters.**
- **Length: 2,200–3,200 words a chapter.** Where a scene will not compress, **let it run rather than drop a cost the crew pays.** A chapter long because it is one complete room is a different fault from a chapter long because it is two. **A chapter long because it is one person's bad wheel and a walk to a sluice is a third fault and is the one to watch for in this batch.**
