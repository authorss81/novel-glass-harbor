AND AT ABOUT FOUR, ON THE FLAT OF THAT BENCH STONE AT THE END OF THAT MOLE, WITH ABOUT FOUR PEOPLE WITHIN SIGHT OF IT AND NOBODY AT IT, A MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-THREE WHO IS NOT IN ANY PART OF THIS READS THE HONEST TOTAL OFF A PAGE LYING FACE UP ON THAT STONE AND READS IT OUT LOUD TO NOBODY AND NOBODY ASKS HIM WHY HE READ IT.** THAT IS THE LAST IMAGE OF THE VOLUME.

**AND ON NO DAY OF YOUR TEN:** nobody answers who says a correction is owed; nobody says why the wall takes a minute longer each way; the fortnight is not touched; nobody explains the drawer before it is used; the second line the woman wrote on 848 is not taken down and not corrected; the correction and the refusal stay on that end stall at the same size with the wrong figure still showing underneath; the fifteen-gap sheet does not move; the third column stays empty under that boy's hand; the fourth column stays at one hundred and twenty-five; no bellwright is asked anything; nobody dies; nobody is thanked; no stage is taken or named; the sealed record is never opened and the hill is never climbed; and **two married people who are still married walk up a lane together at the end of it and the four words said on 850 are not said again.**
