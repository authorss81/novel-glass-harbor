## The climax, stated once and at length, because a Movement 5 that does not know its climax will spend its volume for nothing

**THE CLIMAX IS FORTY SMALL DECISIONS IN ONE NIGHT BY ABOUT ELEVEN BOATS, EACH ONE REFUSABLE, EACH ONE ENTERED WITH A NAME AND A TIME.** Calder and independent crews open the remembered Bramble Strait route to bring medicine, flour, oil and people through the blockade. **The climax is not a battle and no officer is in it and no office is in a room.** The record of it is a page a man can check because every decision has a name and a time against it, which is the whole argument of this book and is paid here in the only form it can be paid in.

What that means on the page, and what it does not mean:

- **THE STORM-READER STAGE IS TAKEN HERE, IN SHARED WORK, WITH A CREW, AT A COST, AS A TRADE AND NOT AS A POWER, AND THE SEASON OF THE MARK OF THE SECOND SEAM IS STILL NINE MARKS AND STILL NOT PAID.** The cost is that the remembered pattern is of a coast that has moved and cannot be commanded. - **THE BLOCKADE IS BROKEN ENOUGH AND NOT LIFTED AND THE NOTICE STANDS.** The notice of the seventeenth of May is still on that market wall at the last page of this volume and has not been withdrawn.
