11. **The concealment closes hidden and is told once.** The Oren mark is in no record, in no office, in no book, and not in Kest's chart notation. **The one false line on the unverified note is left false.** A concealment may be told once, to one person, in a private scene, at a price — it was told once, in Chapter 38 — and may not be told a second time for nothing.
12. **The answer inside the delayed note is reserved to the Volume 15 midpoint.** **Eleven logged occasions since the twenty-sixth of October and two empty answers, and neither count may be advanced by arithmetic or by a measurement.** Nobody may name the call and nobody may treat two empty answers as an answer.
13. **The bell is not an instrument.** It reads nothing, settles nothing and says no word. Fouled waist, a frame cut through the casting, two parts of a chain in a lead plug, a year's rope, **a new tongue a day's work every four or five years for as long as that bell hangs, and the whole of the craft is the going out: a person, in a boat, in the weather.** **Eight marks and a day of a boat, paid once, and it recurs.** It may never become an instrument that answers a question.
14. **Corvin Sarn is the harbor's own bellwright of Morrow Quay, about fifty, and this volume paid his eight marks. He teaches nobody.** **No formal Bellwright stage is taken in Volume 2; that is Volume 5 and it requires a crew.**
15. **No formal power stage is taken in Volume 2.** Calder's growth in the volume is method: a schedule, a tender, a fixed limit, a stated reason, a ground named in the paper, two columns and now three, and other people in the room who know why. **The tinnitus was named for the sixth and last time in Chapter 88 and the name is not used anywhere in Chapters 91–100.**
16. **No custody object returns.** Three items, three numbers, **digits not quoted.** The municipal claim on the third cannot be withdrawn without a request and **the yard will not make one.** The custody-log re-entry of the twentieth of December gives no reason, **and no paper states what a municipal claim does to a presumed Crown material, and that sentence may not be answered by a clerk.**
17. **The ten yards.** The withdrawal is on a wall; **the difference is not stated as a measurement on any record or wall.** The reeve's ruling that no line may be drawn between any of the three points **stands and is not lifted**, and Volume 03 may not draw one.
18. **The first echo's witness stays where he is** — unnamed, unlocated, uninterviewed, and not to be resolved by a convenient anonymous letter or a surviving crew list.
19. **Labor stays visible in every chapter that moves a day.** The forty marks to the boy; the wage, with **sixteen marks paid out of four hands in a market on the twenty-ninth of April and four unpaid lines left in the column on the third of May, the fifth of March the oldest of them, and the boy to be off the stone on the fifteenth of May whatever his own arithmetic says**; the forty to Mara Venn unpaid and unreachable by the writ; **eight shillings and tenpence to Sella Brigg unpaid and about four hundred feet of her own warp on the bottom of the harbor**; the chandler's note at twenty-three and un-re-written; **no air, no compressor gland, no delivery hose, and a hand lead's line being made badly at night**; the *Kittiwake* on the hard with no cradle; the harbor's crane and barge and pump and hand crane on the authority's own account; the north berth out of service; the ninth seam's mooring out of service with no end of it entered; **the tide gang four men short from the first of May, one of them stood off to mind a cart, and the mole steps will not be cleared in June**; **fifty fathoms of winter warp on the north wall where ninety belonged and forty feet of the authority's marline in the sea**; the cistern charged on a rota that a man does not walk; **the harbor authority's fire party and its dead hose**; the mole-head light not on and will not be; **the harbor authority with no boat of its own after the evening of the twenty-eighth**; Elias Voke's statement of capacity; the hard at Ashtide, unanswered since the ninth of November; **the eleven copies of the public record, of which the yard holds seven and has named four it cannot reach and has not asked**; and the ninth thing on the market wall, of which two copies exist and one is with a coaster's master who paid a shilling for it.
20. **Sera Mott may not be asked again in this volume, and the volume is closed.** She was asked once, on Sunday the ninth of April, and refused, and her name is not in the yard's book at her asking. She is sixty-eight and has said she will say no one more time in about two years. **Volume 03 inherits the refusal and not the question.**
21. **The run under the town is a bearing and a bottom and nothing else.** Nothing may explain what is under the town, name it, or send anybody after it at night. **Nine and a quarter feet is the *Aster Gannet's* ferry plate and is a different object at a different part of the mole, and the two may never be fused.**
22. **The coast road may be described, published, doubted and half-believed, and is not resolved.** It may not be explained, may not be named as a network, and may not be connected to anything under the town. **The two may never be fused, and a character may say so on the page.** The figures on the page are the cairn at eleven feet by pole, the stem at nine fathoms at the middle cast of four, the bar at two and three-quarters at twenty past two on the flood off a spring and two and a quarter on the ebb, the fouling at four fathoms and a hand's width, and **the Kettle, which was not looked at.** **Five miles of that coast was not looked at and eleven miles of water was run to look at two stones and a bar.** Two men have spoken about it in a market and one of them has not been proved wrong either. **The word *network* was used once and refused.**
23. **The charter is gone and the yard is not ruined by a sentence, and the writ does not replace it.** The writ runs to the first of March next, is not the Second Bell license, is not a charter, renews nothing, and **its own last line says the second season is not the harbor authority's to give and will arrive out of a city from a man who has never been inside this harbor.** A Volume 03 chapter may name the absence and may not manufacture a renewal.
24. **Mara Venn is a trade and not an office and her break is Volume 4.** She is expensive and right and she does not break. **She was refused work, refused payment and refused a hearing, her forty marks were not paid, the market money did not touch them, the writ cannot reach her, and she sent a paper of her own up the coast with three questions about a chart.** She is not the yard's assistant and is not going to be one.
