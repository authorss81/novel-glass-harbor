## Escalation sequence

Five movements. Each resolves a local problem and creates a larger one. Chapter ranges are the writing schedule's boundaries and the movements are written to them.

### Movement 1, the notice and the seat (Chapters 151–160)

**A paper comes up the hill that is not about the second season of the writ and is not from a man about the second season, and it asks for a book.** The first of September arrives and the ninth seam's mooring's end of it arrives with it. A clerk of the circuit office comes up the hill with a form. **The yard is not charged with anything: it is asked for its book, and the asking is the danger, because a book is only ever as good as the worst day in it and this yard has entered against itself that it lost the count of its own basket on a Tuesday in July and that a boy went off a foredeck in August and that the case went down.**

**Turn:** a party that published itself on purpose discovers that publishing was not the same as being believed, and that the instrument it built to make itself checkable is the instrument that will be used to open it.

### Movement 2, the work that cannot be sold while a book is being read (Chapters 161–170)

### Movement 4, the faultreader in a room (Chapters 181–190)

**THE VOLUME'S MIDPOINT, and it is not a discovery. It is a hearing.** The party's own book is read out in a room, whole, unshortened, because that is what a book is for and because the yard entered on the sixth of June that the second column is the only place a man is allowed to say *I do not know* and mean it. **And in the middle of it, a man who reads seams puts one where nobody has put one, on a document, and it tells him that a document has been in two hands at two times and nothing else, and he is asked in a room what that means and he cannot answer, and the answer he gave in a market in July is read back to him in his own words.** **Nobody is convicted. **This is the reversal because it turns the volume's competence against the volume's virtue.** A volume that has spent a volume saying *a record can be a lie about provenance and a true account of a road* arrives at a room where a true record is being used and cannot be made to say anything else, **because a man who is the best reader of joins in this harbor is also the man whose join-reading was wrong in a market eleven people heard.**
