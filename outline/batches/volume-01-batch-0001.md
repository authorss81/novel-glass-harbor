# Volume 01 Batch 0001 Outline

Chapter range: 1–10

## Batch goal

Establish Calder's ordinary salvage life and the Gannet debt, uncover the first transparent hull through a legitimate physical job, force Calder and Mara into a bounded cooperation, show the tide-ear procedure and its first bodily cost, and end with the shard surrendered to a Registry seizure while its provenance record survives and the *Vigil* arrives.

## Starting condition

No chapter prose exists. Calder is an ordinary line diver in debt, one failed attempt short of the full Second Bell license, and distrustful of imperial custody. Mara has not yet inspected his work. The transparent fleet, Quietening, Crown Seal, and Ilya's historical role remain unknown to the characters.

## Starting relationships

Calder treats Alden's silence as protection, indulges Bevin's eagerness, and accepts Nessa's safety objections only when the equipment is already broken. Mara enters as a Registry tide conservator, an independent witness with a reporting duty, and a potential threat. No romance or automatic trust is established.

## Starting power level

**Line Diver.** Calder has practical rope, pump, pressure-bell, hull, and weather skill. He has no practiced echo ability and has never suffered formal aftertaste.

## Batch midpoint

By Chapter 5, the crew establishes that the shard comes from a transparent hull built by a civic network older than the Serein Dominion. The object is physically extraordinary and politically dangerous, but it does not yet break the Gannet record.

## Batch climax

Kest serves a current Registry seizure order bearing senior examiner Ilya Serr's seal, holds the transparent shard at the yard, and hands it to a Registry courier who takes it to the Morrow circuit office. Calder prevents the crew from destroying or concealing evidence, but cannot keep the physical object. The loss changes the investigation from possession to copies, measurements, memory, and contested provenance.

## Hard rules for this batch

1. **No System.** No panels, stat blocks, status messages, floating text, or software vocabulary. The only message-like elements are claim seals, Registry notations, bell marks, written limits, gauges, and physical warnings.
2. **Calder is an ordinary line diver who becomes an unstable tide-ear.** One supervised entry only. He surfaces with a limited practice trace, a rope-burn aftertaste, ringing ears, and emotional residue. He cannot rewind the scene, verify its truth, open the contaminated edge, or enter a second echo that day.
3. **Mara cannot enter an echo, certify truth, or become Calder's assistant.** She can test material, read provenance, and say no. Her institutional knowledge must change events on its own.
4. **The physical shard is surrendered to a Registry seizure.** Kest holds and witnesses; a Registry courier takes the object to the Morrow circuit custody room. The crew keeps measurements, names, and a public provenance duplicate. No convenient loophole keeps the object, and the duplicate is a record of the object rather than a copy of its memory.
5. **The Oren-specific mark stays private.** Calder tells Nessa that a sound is not an ordinary mechanical fault; he does not tell Mara what the mark is.
6. **No new canon rule, new villain, or new institution.** Ilya Serr's name appears only as a senior Registry examiner's seal on a Registry document.
7. **Reveal discipline.** Do not name the Quietening, the Crown Seal, or the Nine Harbors Compact; do not reveal what the sealed glass case contained or who ordered the Gannet lie; do not establish Oren's final choice.
8. **Labor stays visible.** Air, pump time, wages, repairs, permits, and the crew's remaining safe diving day are the real stakes alongside the glass.
9. **The bearing toward Orison is uncertain.** A broad physical direction is allowed; a complete route, a map, or a purpose is not.

## Required character changes

- Calder admits to Nessa that a sound in the wreck is not an ordinary mechanical fault, though he does not tell Mara it carries the Oren mark.
- Calder chooses a public duplicate record over a private emotional advantage.
- Mara begins treating Calder as a fallible but useful witness rather than a suspect to be managed.
- Nessa becomes the person who notices Calder is lying about his condition and refuses to let him dive again unrepaired.

## Required power changes

Calder completes one shallow, anchored echo entry under observation and surfaces a limited practice trace plus strong aftertaste. He has an unstable first tide-ear capacity, not a new profession or reliable skill. He cannot rewind the scene, verify its truth, or read another echo while carrying its residue.

## Required world reveals

- Echo-glass can preserve an embodied scene but only from one witness's position.
- Transparent vessels are older civic construction, not a recent Dominion invention.
- The first limited echo places a sealed glass case crossing the *Gannet*'s loading space while an official voice reads an empty public manifest. Later physical measurement must show that the compartment the manifest calls empty was too large to have been empty, and that the *Vigil*'s mooring line was cut from shore, before the manifest conflict is proven near the volume midpoint.
- Ilya Serr currently holds the senior Registry examiner title; he is not yet Minister of Returning Memory and is not called minister anywhere in this batch.
- A recovered echo can be unstable and still useful, but neither stability nor emotional force establishes truth.
- The harbor authority, the Lantern Registry, and the Navy are separate institutions with separate documents, and a claim window is a permission rather than ownership.
- The only interface in this world is physical: seals, notations, bells, gauges, and the diver's own body.

## Required continuity updates

Record Calder's first aftertaste, tinnitus, rope-burn reenactment, and temporary hearing confusion. Preserve the original Registry record, Calder's measurements, and the public provenance duplicate. Keep the Oren-specific mark private. Do not identify the Quietening, Crown Seal, Ilya's role in the Gannet lie, or the transparent route's full course and purpose. The opening batch may establish an uncertain physical bearing toward Orison.

## Open questions after the batch

- What did the sealed glass case contain, and why did every document omit it?
- How did the shard reach an ordinary Morrow Quay wreck?
- Why does Calder recognize an Oren-specific pressure pattern?
- What authority will the *Vigil* use beyond Kest's local seizure?
- The physical keel trace points broadly toward Orison, but the crew cannot yet distinguish a deliberate route from a shifted wreck chain.

## Chapter Cards

### Chapter 0001 — Salt in the Second Pump

**POV:** Calder Marr, close third person.

**Location/time:** Morrow Quay public dry dock, early morning, first day of the batch.

**Immediate goal:** Finish a routine pump repair, collect the small fee, and preserve enough air and crew time for another paid dive without risking the Free Keel's only working winch.

**Resistance:** The pump gasket keeps failing, the client wants a cosmetic patch rather than an honest rebuild, and Bevin is eager to skip the pressure test.

**Information revealed:** Establish Calder's practical competence, debt pressure, family silence, ordinary salvage economics, and the physical danger of worn equipment. A routine storm-anchor haul at the mole turns up a transparent fragment wedged under the shank of an anchor that is bedded too deep for the yard crane.

**Action or decision:** Calder rejects the unsafe shortcut and finishes the repair properly. He chips the fragment free instead of leaving it for another crew, records the anchor's position and lead-line tag, and leaves the anchor itself in place for a proper lift later.

**Emotional change:** A job that should be simple becomes personally dangerous when the fragment answers his hammer with a delayed bell note. He wants it because it may be Oren's, not because he has evidence that it is.

**Power or resource change:** No magic is used. Calder gains a transparent fragment and an anchor position mark, pays for a cracked pump housing, and loses most of the day's safe diving margin.

**Continuity fact:** The shard is unclaimed glass of Registry interest, not yet proven to come from the *Aster Gannet*. The storm anchor is ordinary iron and is still on the bottom. Calder is a line diver.

**Ending type:** Discovery.

**Next-chapter pull:** Kest appears before Calder can hide the fragment and announces that Crown glass may be seized on inspection.

### Chapter 0002 — The Claim Window

**POV:** Calder Marr, close third person.

**Location/time:** The reeve's floating office on the west basin, late morning.

**Immediate goal:** Turn an accidental recovery into a legitimate Free Keel salvage claim before Kest can refer the fragment to the Registry as unclaimed Crown glass.

**Resistance:** Kest has inspected the dock, Calder's record contains a disputed family connection to the Gannet, and the harbor cannot afford the bond required to appeal a seizure.

**Information revealed:** Kest is enforcing real permit and bond pressure rather than serving a private evil. He grants a short claim window because Calder's work generated the recovery, not because he trusts Calder.

**Action or decision:** Calder submits the salvage labor log, the anchor position mark, and the crew names. He gives Mara Venn access to the fragment's provenance because refusing inspection would validate Kest's referral.

**Emotional change:** Calder's private goal becomes an official record, and he realizes secrecy may cost him the claim he needs.

**Power or resource change:** Calder secures only a disputed-claim window, not ownership. Mara is introduced as a Registry tide conservator who cannot enter an echo but can test glass history and custody.

**Continuity fact:** Kest's authority comes through the harbor authority's permits, holds, and hearings, and the Registry's rules. Ilya's current order is not yet examined in person.

**Ending type:** Reversal.

**Next-chapter pull:** Mara finds construction marks that do not match any licensed modern wreck, and asks who taught Calder to recognize that pattern.

### Chapter 0003 — Work Before Proof

**POV:** Calder Marr, close third person.

**Location/time:** Free Keel yard and Gannet Shoal survey line, afternoon.

**Immediate goal:** Build a defensible salvage team and locate the ordinary wreck from which the shard came without spending the claim window on a speculative search.

**Resistance:** The crew has one usable boat and little air money; Alden refuses to discuss the Gannet; Nessa says Calder is treating the find as personal evidence before establishing its physical context.

**Information revealed:** The shard was wedged in a storm anchor that came adrift from a mundane coastal wreck tangled in the old shoal. Calder recognizes a Gannet-era repairer's pressure mark but hides that it also matches Oren's work.

**Action or decision:** Calder accepts Nessa's condition that the crew logs the dive as ordinary salvage, shares the anchor position, and places Bevin on the surface watch rather than another deep task.

**Emotional change:** His secrecy strains family and crew trust without yet producing a useful answer.

**Power or resource change:** The crew commits its remaining air, pump time, and a share of the ordinary wreck's value to establish provenance.

**Continuity fact:** The Gannet sank thirteen years ago. Oren was twenty and is Calder's older brother. Alden's signed report remains unexamined by Calder.

**Ending type:** Decision.

**Next-chapter pull:** At the wreck, the anchor is trapped beneath a much larger transparent keel rib.

### Chapter 0004 — What the Anchor Caught

**POV:** Calder Marr, close third person.

**Location/time:** Gannet Shoal, first controlled work dive, late afternoon.

**Immediate goal:** Free the anchor and recover enough of the surrounding hull to prove where the transparent shard originated.

**Resistance:** Silt, a narrow pressure window, a cracked bell hose, and a keel rib that shifts when the first clamp takes load.

**Information revealed:** The larger structure is a transparent hull, not a loose panel. Its scale and old joinery imply a vessel, but Calder cannot safely inspect it in this dive.

**Action or decision:** Calder abandons the attempt to raise the whole rib and uses a sacrificial line to bring up the freed anchor, the clamps, his measurements, and a weathered hull plate. He chooses recoverable proof over a dangerous trophy.

**Emotional change:** Excitement gives way to professional disappointment when the tide turns before he can answer the larger question.

**Power or resource change:** Calder gains physical evidence and loses one pressure-bell cycle plus a replacement clamp. No echo ability is used.

**Continuity fact:** The transparent vessel is old and civic in construction, but its route and purpose remain unknown. The storm anchor is ordinary iron, not glass, so it is not affected by any later claim over the fragment.

**Ending type:** Cost (small practical victory).

**Next-chapter pull:** Mara identifies a sealed witness chamber in the plate and warns that lifting it without a controlled anchor could contaminate the scene.

### Chapter 0005 — Older Than the Registry

**POV:** Calder Marr, close third person.

**Location/time:** Free Keel workbench, evening.

**Immediate goal:** Build a physical provenance record strong enough to keep the shard within the claim window while preparing a safe first echo test.

**Resistance:** The plate carries at least three incompatible repair histories; Mara will not certify a tidy story, and Calder wants only the Oren-linked seam examined.

**Information revealed:** The vessel predates the Serein Dominion. Its makers used a distributed keel pattern and an old custody mark of nine joined lines, not a standard imperial cargo seal. This suggests civic infrastructure, not a ruling family's private possession.

**Action or decision:** Calder records every repair seam, including evidence that damages his preferred Oren connection, and allows Nessa to build a physical anchor, a fixed air limit, and a cutoff line. Mara writes down the stated reason for any entry before Calder goes near the panel.

**Emotional change:** Calder loses the comforting certainty that one familiar mark will lead directly to Oren and begins to see Mara's refusal to certify as a safeguard rather than obstruction.

**Power or resource change:** The crew gains a valid anchor, a shallow pressure plan, and a documented claim; the first tide-ear attempt becomes possible but remains high risk.

**Continuity fact:** Do not name the Quietening or Crown Seal, and do not name the Nine Harbors. The old nine-line custody mark predates imperial custody and does not itself prove truthful consent.

**Ending type:** Discovery (batch midpoint).

**Next-chapter pull:** When the anchor settles, the shard produces a bodily pulse Calder recognizes from Oren's pressure-log notation.

### Chapter 0006 — One Witness, One Angle

**POV:** Calder Marr, close third person.

**Location/time:** Shallow protected dive over the Free Keel platform, night.

**Immediate goal:** Enter one anchored scene without losing the crew, the physical shard, or Calder's ability to surface.

**Resistance:** The echo is contaminated at one edge; Calder recognizes Oren's work rhythm; air, line angle, and aftertaste all threaten to pull his attention away from the tender.

**Information revealed:** From one witness's limited position during the Gannet seizure, Calder sees a sealed glass case cross a loading space while an official voice reads an empty public manifest. The angle does not show the case's Registry mark, the space's full dimensions, or whether another record corrects the spoken count. The scene also catches a marked keel-line, a shouted order, and a gloved hand with a young deck apprentice's callus closing around a line-marked fitting; the hand passes out of frame before anything is cut, and the scene does not show who acted later or what happened next.

**Action or decision:** Calder follows the procedure under the six stated conditions, a named tender, an independent line, a fixed air limit, a seated anchor, a cutoff Nessa can pull, and Mara's written reason for the entry. He surfaces when Nessa calls the cutoff, not when he feels satisfied. He does not attempt to remain for a better angle or force open the contaminated edge, and he accepts that the one scene he got is the only scene he gets tonight.

**Emotional change:** He receives useful corroboration and loses the comforting belief that finding Oren's choice will tell him what to do now.

**Power or resource change:** Calder surfaces with a limited practice trace, a rope-burn reenactment, tinnitus, and emotional aftertaste. He has unstable tide-ear capacity but cannot safely repeat the dive that day or carry a second echo.

**Continuity fact:** The echo makes a manifest contradiction plausible but does not prove it; later pressure records and hull measurements must corroborate the physical impossibility near the volume midpoint. The echo remains one witness's embodied frame.

**Ending type:** Cost.

**Next-chapter pull:** Back on deck, Calder answers Nessa in the rhythm of Oren's old call mark and realizes the residue has altered an ordinary exchange.

### Chapter 0007 — What the Bell Borrowed

**POV:** Calder Marr, close third person.

**Location/time:** Free Keel yard infirmary and workbench, night.

**Immediate goal:** Make the aftertaste medically and operationally manageable before the morning inspection at which the claim either holds or collapses.

**Resistance:** Calder minimizes the hearing change, Nessa refuses an unsupervised return to work, and Mara can document instability but cannot remove it.

**Information revealed:** Calder's ordinary bell hearing now overlaps the recorded loading bell. Mara identifies a contaminated edge that could spread through a connected route; Nessa ties the physical cutoff procedure to a stop Calder cannot overrule while attached.

**Action or decision:** Calder accepts no further echo entry, hands Nessa the shutdown decision, and gives Mara measurements and a complete contamination warning while withholding only the Oren-specific mark.

**Emotional change:** Being stopped by the crew both irritates and relieves him; competence no longer gives him sole authority over the risk.

**Power or resource change:** Calder loses diving access for at least a night. Nessa gains explicit authority over the physical cutoff, and Mara gains a stable technical basis for the provenance record.

**Continuity fact:** The original shard remains physically present with the Free Keel under temporary claim protection. No public copy exists yet. The named-tender cutoff established here is the same procedure Calder and Nessa use again in Volumes 14 and 15, so it must be written as a physical method with a spoken word, not as a rule explained in narration.

**Ending type:** Cost (recovery and relationship consequence).

**Next-chapter pull:** Word comes from the reeve's office that a Registry seizure order will be served before the morning inspection, and the shard is to be surrendered at the yard.

### Chapter 0008 — The Shard Leaves the Deck

**POV:** Calder Marr, close third person.

**Location/time:** Free Keel yard, predawn.

**Immediate goal:** Keep the shard on the yard long enough to complete a public provenance record before the Registry courier can take it.

**Resistance:** Kest's order is technically valid and he can hold the object on the harbor authority's authority, Calder's claim window is nearly expired, and destroying or hiding the shard would destroy the evidence needed to challenge the seizure.

**Information revealed:** The order is a Registry seizure order bearing senior Registry examiner Ilya Serr's name and seal, and Kest serves it as the harbor authority's officer. It does not explain the Gannet lie, identify the case, or prove that Ilya acted during the original disaster.

**Action or decision:** Calder stops Bevin from hiding the shard and stops Nessa from breaking it. He signs a witnessed surrender, has Mara create a public provenance duplicate, and preserves measurements, repair traces, and the anchor diagram before the object leaves the deck in a Registry courier's box. The duplicate is a record of the object, not a copy of its memory.

**Emotional change:** Calder feels the loss as another abandonment of Oren, but chooses accountable custody over an impulsive act that would protect the object only by destroying the proof around it.

**Power or resource change:** The crew loses the physical shard and gains a defensible duplicate record, witness names, and a documented chain of custody. No new power appears.

**Continuity fact:** Ilya is a senior Registry examiner at this point, not Minister of Returning Memory. Kest holds and witnesses; he does not seize. The object is now in the Morrow circuit custody room, and Calder no longer has any claim on its physical custody.

**Ending type:** Cost (batch climax).

**Next-chapter pull:** With the shard gone, Calder and Mara discover that its provenance diagram aligns with the *Vigil*'s recorded Gannet mooring position.

### Chapter 0009 — The Three-Second Gap

**POV:** Calder Marr, close third person.

**Location/time:** Morrow circuit office copying room and Free Keel yard, morning.

**Immediate goal:** Preserve the link between the shard and the *Vigil* without accusing Ilya or treating Calder's aftertaste as evidence.

**Resistance:** The public pressure log has been copied, the original Registry record belongs to Mara's custody, and Alden refuses to discuss the salvage report he signed.

**Information revealed:** The provenance diagram's joinery matches the hull pattern the *Vigil* used for its sealed cases, and the anchor bearing places the shard's origin on the same ground the cutter claimed at the seizure. The ferry's public pressure log, copied into the inquest record, contains a deliberate three-second gap. Separately and in his own hand, Calder notes what his echo showed, labeled unverified. This makes the contradiction testable but does not yet prove that the compartment was too large to be empty, reveal the case's contents, or identify who ordered the loss.

**Action or decision:** Calder records the physical findings and the limits of his memory in two separate documents so that neither can borrow the other's authority. Mara retains the original Registry record; Calder keeps only a marked public copy. Calder does not force Alden to confess.

**Emotional change:** Evidence brings Calder closer to an answer while making the cost of treating memory as proof more obvious.

**Power or resource change:** The crew gains a corroborated route fact and a preserved public copy, but no physical panel and no reliable second echo.

**Continuity fact:** Alden's signed report, Oren's final choice, and Ilya's Gannet culpability remain unrevealed.

**Ending type:** Decision (investigation and emotional refusal).

**Next-chapter pull:** Kest offers Calder a short disputed-claim protection in exchange for ceasing public use of the provenance record, while the *Vigil* enters Morrow harbor.

### Chapter 0010 — A Bell Out of Time

**POV:** Calder Marr, close third person.

**Location/time:** Morrow Quay outer mole, late day.

**Immediate goal:** Secure temporary legal protection for the crew's evidence and keep the ordinary harbor operations running as the *Vigil* arrives.

**Resistance:** Accepting Kest's terms would suppress the duplicate record; refusing them leaves the provenance vulnerable to seizure. Calder's hearing is still altered, and the *Vigil* can assert naval salvage authority over the anchored wreck.

**Information revealed:** A limited inspection accepts the crew's chain of labor and preserves the public duplicate for twelve days, but the broader seizure remains unresolved. The *Vigil* files a naval salvage claim, and the Registry's seizure order with Ilya's current seal is attached to it as the instrument that gives the claim a harbor berth. Comparing the anchor bearing, public provenance diagram, and the continuing keel line establishes only a broad physical direction toward Orison, not a complete route or purpose. With the shard gone, Calder hears the delayed bell note in the keel line still running beneath the shoal, and an Oren childhood call seems to answer inside it; he cannot tell whether the call is his memory, Oren's, or contamination carried by the aftertaste.

**Action or decision:** Calder rejects a permanent gag on the provenance record, accepts only the temporary protection, logs his hearing impairment, and chooses to keep investigating under increased Navy scrutiny rather than surrender Oren's clue or pretend he is unchanged.

**Emotional change:** He gains professional proof and loses certainty about his own memory. The choice to continue is deliberate and costly, not an impulsive declaration that truth will solve everything.

**Power or resource change:** The crew retains no physical shard and no open echo route, but it preserves a public record, a twelve-day claim defense, and Calder's first fragile tide-ear capacity. The next phase begins with a legal dispute under naval pressure.

**Continuity fact:** The characters now have an uncertain bearing toward Orison but do not know the full route, the Quietening, the Crown Seal, or Ilya's long-term plan. No new interface, canon rule, or major antagonist appears.

**Ending type:** Earned hook.

**Next-chapter pull:** The *Vigil* orders Calder off the wreck while its salvage line tightens around the place where the transparent keel continues beneath the shoal.
