## Central pressure

**THE ANTAGONIST OF THIS VOLUME IS NOT A MAN AND NOT A CUTTER AND NOT A MONSTER. It is a requirement.** The Crown's position, which is the only position anybody in Veyr has ever put in writing about glass, is that an unsettled testimony may be quarantined until a recognized claimant appears, and that a person who keeps unsettled material in a house is a keeper of it, and that a person who keeps it in a drawer with no office above it is a keeper of it in a place where nothing can be appealed to. **That claim is true enough to be dangerous and it is true in the way a fire door is true, and this volume must not once make it false.** The Crown's instrument is a form, served on a person, at an address, and it costs that person nothing to comply with and everything to refuse.

## Escalation sequence

Five movements. Each resolves a local problem and creates a larger one.

### Movement 1, an instrument that is not there, and a boat that is not the yard's (Chapters 251–260)

**THE VOLUME OPENS WITH A PARTY THAT CANNOT BE HIRED.** The charter is gone and the form that took it is careful, and the consequence is not a speech: from the sixteenth of March there is nothing to book on a working day, the face of the second seam stops, and the only money that moves is the price of a boat hired to look at a mark this yard cannot afford to keep. **A mark is looked at three times in twenty days and on the third time it has a line on it and a shackle of a make nobody in this harbor makes, and the only people on this coast who could have done it own five bells four days away, and the limit they wrote stands, and nobody asks them to take it off. The volume's question is asked at a mole head by a person of no office and is not answered, and a leaf is ruled in the yard's own book with four columns, the fourth of which says what a thing is not, and it does not go on any wall and nobody can check it. And on the third day a letter is posted to a man who cannot read, in a town that keeps one of the five pages, and a letter from a person of no office came up the hill the day before, and neither is answered.**

**Turn:** a yard that has spent five volumes refusing to be an instrument of anybody's, and then refusing to be an address, discovers that the only channel it has left to a town is the post, and the post is a channel with a counter in it, and a letter is a way of being found.

### Movement 2, the air, and the only man on this coast who will cut it (Chapters 261–270)

**THE DESCENT IS BOUGHT, NOT FOUND.** A fireman of a pressure house at Ternwatch, who has read a copy of that yard's printed sheet because a copy of it is in about sixty boats on that coast, will supply air and a housing and will not take a Crown wage doing it, and his condition is that nothing goes under that has not been cut, and there is nobody on this coast who cuts. **The first descent goes to about forty fathoms in a trench and costs a pot, a day, and a body, and it brings up a thing that is not a record and a person who is not a witness, and it puts a person in a hospital at Ashtide whose hard has been unanswered since the ninth of November.**

**Turn:** the volume's question is found to be answerable and the party is found to be the wrong party for it, because the evidence that answers it is four hundred miles west under seventy fathoms and the people who keep the answer are the people this yard will not ask.

### Movement 3, a form served on a person who keeps a page in a house (Chapters 271–280)

**THE QUARANTINE.** A form with a name on it and an address on it goes to a keeper in a town two days down that coast, and the keeper is not an officer and does not attend the service and the form is served by being left. **And a person of no office says a thing out loud, in a market, in about forty people's hearing, and the thing she says costs her the last of a certificate and the standing of a certificate in a market, and she says it because she was asked by a person and not by a yard.**

**Turn:** the party discovers that a page in a drawer in a house is custody that an office can reach, and that the four lines four people of that coast wrote and signed are about a *record* and are not about a *house*, and that the price of the volume's own solution is now somebody else's problem.

### Movement 4, a room under seventy fathoms, and a record with nine hands' worth of disagreement in it (Chapters 281–290)

**THE VOLUME'S MIDPOINT, AND IT IS A READING IN A DARK ROOM AND NOT A DISCOVERY IN A MARKET.** A surviving municipal record from the drowned civic chambers says the fleet was ordered down. **The order is collective, disputed, and not the work of a single ruler: it carries marks from more than one community and the marks do not agree, and one of the marks is a builder's mark of a pre-Accord construction and is not a claim seal and not a mark of consent and no office in Veyr can say what it means.** **THE WORD *QUIETENING* IS NAMED HERE AND NOWHERE EARLIER IN THIS VOLUME, IN A CHARACTER'S MOUTH, AND THE WORD *CROWN SEAL* IS NOT NAMED IN THIS MOVEMENT AT ALL.**

**Turn:** the answer the party came for is not a monster and not a lie and not a weapon, and the party discovers that the thing it has been calling an office's fear was a real thing that really happened, and that knowing it does not make anybody safe.

### Movement 5, a thing that closes, and a storm, and what a man loses (Chapters 291–300)

**THE VOLUME'S CLIMAX.** A fragment at the bottom of a chamber carries a seal that turns out to be the mechanism, and bringing it up makes a local glass storm, and the storm is not the point. **The point is that there is a sealed archive of nine communities' vote fragments in a chamber that will close, and it will close whether it is read or not, and Calder and Mara must let it close rather than use it to prove a politically useful lie — because a lie proved with a thing that closed is a lie nobody can check, and this yard has spent five volumes saying that a record a man cannot check is worth less than a record a man can look at.** **Tobias Wren is injured and does not die. Calder loses a portion of his own recall, permanently, and enters the second column of it with a date against it.**

**RESOLUTION:** the team proves the Quietening was a real cascade caused by forced consensus and not a monster and not a weapon, the Crown's history is exposed as incomplete rather than as false, **and a seal of a Registry examiner, dated in the chain of the old evidence and older than the office that made the chain, is found on it: *Magister Ilya Serr, senior Registry examiner*, on a line, and he is in no room.**
