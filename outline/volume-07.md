## Central pressure

**THE ANSWER TO THIS VOLUME'S QUESTION IS A LINE DRAWN FROM A MOLE-HEAD TO A CAN ON A STAKE, AND THE LINE IS NOT ON ANY CHART THIS HARBOR CARRIES.** On the seventeenth of May a notice goes up on the outside of whatever is on the market wall of Morrow Quay, under a sheet that was bare at eight that morning, and it is the third naval notice on that wall in two years and this yard did not take the first two down and is not going to take this one down. It does not close the port. It closes *the water*, by making a master's lawful passage depend on a declaration lodged with a municipal office **whose season ended on the thirty-first of March with nothing issued**, so that the instrument requires a thing from an office that does not exist, and nobody wrote it that way, and it does not matter, and this yard is not going to stand in a market and say that it does not matter. **The volume's question is whether a coast can be starved on paper in eleven days and no longer in ninety, and the volume's central pressure is that a restriction is not hunger and is worse than hunger, because a man can be angry at hunger and cannot be angry at a sentence.**

**THE ANTAGONIST OF THIS VOLUME IS A MAN WITH A NAME ON A LINE AND A SCHEDULE, NOT A MONSTER AND NOT A CUTTER.** **ADMIRAL SOREN VEY, FIFTY-TWO, COMMANDER OF THE CROWN NAVY'S CLOSED-SEA SQUADRON**, is named in this volume on the face of one instrument and on no other, and he is calm and logistical and willing to let a harbor go without oil rather than permit a route he does not control, and **his argument is the one this book has spent six volumes building toward and has never once made false**: a coast that cannot feed itself has no business choosing its own crossings. **He may not be made a caricature, and he may not be answered by a fact.** He is not a face in a room in Movement 1.

**AND THE VOLUME'S METHOD IS THE VOLUME 06 METHOD UNCHANGED, WHICH IS THE POINT: the same four-column leaf, the same first column and second column, the same third column that has not been filled since the first of August and is not to be filled, and the same fourth column, which is a column for what a thing is not.** What is new in this volume is not the method but the number of people it applies to, and the price of that is that a sheet a man can check about one yard is a sheet that can be read aloud in about forty people's hearing by a stranger, and the yard has no way to check that and never did.

## Escalation sequence

Five movements. Each resolves a local problem and creates a larger one.

### Movement 1, a notice on a wall that was bare at eight, and a copy nobody asked for (Chapters 301–310)

**THE VOLUME OPENS WITH A CRATE ON THE MOLE STEPS AND A TENDER LYING OFF THE SOUTH END ALL NIGHT, AND THE TENDER IS NOT THE FIRST THING AND THE CRATE IS NOT THE LAST.** The restriction arrives as a line put on the side of a boat at a slip, then as a word in a chandler's, then as a sheet nailed up at nine on a market wall. **And then it arrives a second time, in a figure nobody was looking for: a sheet is a penny and forty sheets are a shilling, and the yard bought one of them on the twenty-seventh of April and about two hundred copies of the same nine lines went through the same counter since, and the woman at the counter did not ask what it was for, and she is not going to stop, and the reason she gives is that a copyist who stops copying is a copyist who has admitted she copied something.** The batch's last image is a stranger standing on the mole steps with a copied line, and he is not an officer and has no paper, and he came because a page on a wall four days up this coast names a drawer in this shed.

**Turn:** a party that has spent six volumes refusing every instrument in this country finds out that it has been left outside the only instrument anybody in this country has ever been frightened of, **and that being outside it is not a door, because the sentence is about the price of things and not about whose boat it is.**

### Movement 2, a thing that cannot be declared, and a harbor that begins to arrange itself wrongly (Chapters 311–320)

**THE DECLARATION IS THE VOLUME'S FIRST WORKED MECHANISM AND IT IS CAREFUL AND IT IS UNUSABLE.** A master's declaration must state the vessel, her master, her cargo in general terms, and whether she carries material that is the subject of an unsettled claim, made before two witnesses and lodged with the harbor authority before noon. **The form is at a desk in Ternwatch. The harbor authority to lodge it with has no season and no clerk with authority and has issued nothing since the thirty-first of March. And a declaration that says *yes* to the fourth line is a declaration that hands a person to an office, and a declaration that says *no* is a lie, and this book does not permit a lie in a form.** A man of about fifty of the second lay is asked to choose and does not choose and says so out loud. **And the harbor, having nothing to be angry at, begins to be angry at each other, and a man of about sixty of the first lay says the sentence that governs the whole volume: a restriction that arrives in a sentence and not in a boat is a thing a man cannot be angry at, and a man who cannot be angry at it will be angry at something else by Friday.**

**AND IT IS ON THE FACE OF THE CHAPTERS THAT AS OF CHAPTER 310 THAT SENTENCE HAS NOT BEEN SAID AND NEITHER HAS THE LINE THAT GOES WITH IT.** Chapter 307 gives the man the three honest targets — a man with a book, a chandler, and the harbor authority — and his saying that all three are wrong, **and then stops him on purpose and gives the reason out loud, being that a sentence said in a chandler's on a Friday morning is in eleven markets by Sunday and comes back with a man's name on it that the man did not put there.** He took two candles at fourpence and went down to the water and said he would come back into that shop on Monday, being the twenty-fifth, which is the first day of Batch 0002. **A LATER WRITER TAKES THE REST OF IT FROM THE CHAPTERS AND NOT FROM THIS LINE, and this line is a status note and not a plan.**

**Turn:** the coalition this series has been describing is found to be a thing that exists in about sixty boats on this coast and in nobody's, and the volume's real antagonist turns out to be **time** and **a price list set four hundred miles away by people who will never come here.**

### Movement 3, what a chart does not have on it (Chapters 321–330)

**THE VOLUME'S MIDPOINT.** A sequence of old vessels in the yard's own keeping, and a man of about thirty-one of the third lay with a coaster out of Ashtide and a Crown chart in a bag, produce **a storm route across Bramble Strait that no current chart includes**, and the chart that omits it is a Crown chart of about nine years standing that this harbor holds two copies of. **The route is old. Its soundings are of a coast that has moved. Its usefulness is a labor fact and not a memory, and the person who says so is a man of about sixty-one at a far bench who is not asked and asks nothing, and who looks at the thing for four minutes and refuses to say either of the two things he could say about it.** The route can be used — but only if a crew gives up a safer supply crossing, and the price of that is named in the first column before anybody agrees to it.

**Turn:** the party that has spent six volumes proving that a record a man cannot check is worth less than one he can is handed the best record it has ever been offered, and the offer is only good if they stop checking it.

### Movement 4, a boat with a line on her and a man's name on a line in a book (Chapters 331–340)

**RESCUE WORK, AND A HARBOR THAT HAS TO CHOOSE A BOAT.** A vessel of that coast is taken out past the line by her own master under a declaration and something happens to her that the party cannot see and can only price. **A local defense crew of about six men is on a boat of about nineteen tons with no replacement, and the volume's question becomes a single choice: put the mark of the second seam to a use it has never been put to, or leave a boat with six men in her out on a falling tide.** And Calder hides the risk from Mara, and does it in the ordinary way, and is caught in the ordinary way, **by a person who is not jealous of him and is not rescuing him.**

**Turn:** the volume's cost is not a death and not a victory but **a boat and a crew and a public sentence**, and Calder accepts the sentence in public, in about forty people's hearing, on his own feet, and the acceptance is not the end of anything.

### Movement 5, the sea is open enough, and the harbor is poorer (Chapters 341–350)

**THE VOLUME'S CLIMAX.** Calder and independent crews open the remembered Bramble Strait route to bring medicine, flour, oil and people through the blockade. **The climax is not a battle. It is forty small decisions made by about eleven boats in one night, each one by a person who could have refused, and the record of it is a page a man can check because every decision has a name and a time against it, which is the whole argument of this book and is paid here in the only form it can be paid in.** A local defense crew is left without a replacement boat and Calder accepts public responsibility for the choice out loud, in about forty people's hearing, and does not improve on it afterwards.

**RESOLUTION:** the blockade is broken enough to keep Morrow Quay alive — **not lifted, not defeated, and continued on the face of the notice until somebody says otherwise** — and independent sailors now understand, in their own mouths and not in this yard's, that an open route is an obligation and not a freedom, and that a thing on the water has to be looked at by somebody who is not being paid by the person who wants it looked at.
