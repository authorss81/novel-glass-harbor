# Outline Guide for Every Novel

## Series Bible

Before prose, create these files:

```text
bible/premise.md
bible/world.md
bible/characters.md
bible/power-system.md
bible/themes.md
bible/terminology.md
```

Each file must have concrete rules and examples. Avoid vague descriptions such as “power grows” or “the world is mysterious.” State what changes, what it costs, who benefits, and how failure works.

## Series Outline

`outline/series.md` must define:

- The one-sentence premise.
- The protagonist’s external want.
- The protagonist’s internal need.
- The protagonist’s central flaw and fear.
- The primary mystery.
- The final external conflict.
- The final internal choice.
- The final emotional answer.
- The planned ending state of the world.
- The major antagonist ladder.
- The relationship milestones.
- The power stages and their costs.
- The main volume progression.

`outline/ending.md` must describe the final volume in detail even if the prose does not reveal it yet. The ending cannot be improvised after 500 chapters.

## Volume Outline

A normal volume is 40–80 chapters.

Each volume outline should contain:

```text
Volume number
Central pressure
Starting state
Starting relationships
Starting power level
Major locations
Major factions
Midpoint reversal
Escalation sequence
Volume climax
Concrete resolution
New question for the next volume
Final chapter image
```

The volume must resolve something. It can open a larger mystery, but it cannot leave its own central promise unanswered.

## Batch Outline

A batch is 10–20 chapters. The default pilot batch is 10 chapters.

Each batch file should contain:

```text
Batch number
Chapter range
Batch goal
Starting condition
Starting relationships
Batch midpoint
Batch climax
Required character changes
Required power changes
Required world reveals
Open questions after the batch
```

Every chapter receives a card:

```text
Chapter:
POV:
Location/time:
Immediate goal:
Resistance:
Information revealed:
Action or decision:
Emotional change:
Power or resource change:
Continuity fact to preserve:
Ending type: decision / reversal / discovery / cost / earned hook
Next-chapter pull:
```

## Chapter Distribution

Do not make every chapter the same shape.

Use a distribution such as:

- 20% investigation or discovery
- 20% action or survival
- 20% character and relationship work
- 15% training or controlled experimentation
- 15% political or social pressure
- 10% recovery, aftermath, or emotional payoff

Adjust the distribution by volume. A political volume may contain more negotiation and investigation. A war volume may contain more action and recovery.

## Opening Chapters

The first ten chapters must establish:

1. The protagonist’s ordinary life or work.
2. A concrete immediate problem.
3. The central genre promise.
4. One meaningful power or system rule.
5. One relationship with friction.
6. A first small victory.
7. A first cost or failure.
8. A larger promise.
9. A clue to the central mystery.
10. A reason to continue beyond the opening arc.

Do not spend ten chapters explaining the world without giving the reader a goal and a change.

## Middle Volumes

The middle of a long novel should not be a sequence of stronger monsters. Each middle volume should change at least one of these:

- The protagonist’s identity.
- The meaning of the power system.
- The reliability of an ally.
- The political balance.
- The protagonist’s moral position.
- The interpretation of the central mystery.
- The cost of victory.
- The kind of problem the protagonist can solve.

Use a rolling pressure rotation:

```text
local survival
relationship pressure
training
investigation
political conflict
larger mystery
recovery
volume climax
```

Do not repeat the same crisis with a different monster.

## Final Volume

The final volume must include:

- The return of the central mystery.
- The final version of the protagonist’s flaw.
- A choice that costs something real.
- A climax built from earlier promises.
- Resolution of the main relationship and political conflict.
- A clear image of the world afterward.
- No new unexplained cosmic threat.
- A final answer that rewards earlier attention.

## Continuity Files

Maintain:

```text
state/current.md
state/continuity.md
state/open-threads.md
state/chapter-summaries.md
state/phase-ledger.json
```

At the end of every batch:

- Summarize every chapter in two to five sentences.
- Record new canon facts.
- Record unresolved promises.
- Record changed relationships.
- Record power costs and gains.
- Record any world rule discovered.
- Keep the previous 20 chapters available for immediate voice continuity; extend to Chapters 21–30 when the verified context budget safely allows.
- Store a compact volume index.
