Review files are written after each batch's review gate. `volume-01/batch-0001.md` holds the Volume 01, Batch 0001 review (Chapters 1–10) with its findings, the repairs applied in place, and the status line. `volume-06/batch-0004.md` holds the Volume 06, Batch 0004 review (Chapters 281–290) with its thirteen findings, the repairs applied in place, and the re-taken measurements. `volume-08/batch-0002.md` holds the Volume 08, Batch 0002 review (Chapters 361–370) with its eighteen findings, the fifteen applied on 28 September 2026, the two structural findings carried forward with the reason they were not repaired in the chapters, and the re-taken measurements. `volume-18/batch-0004.md` holds the Volume 18, Batch 0004 review (Chapters 891–900) with its ten required findings and five craft findings, what was repaired and what was ruled and carried with the reason, the ruling that the column of the first time is fourteen, the marker gap left flagged, and the re-taken measurements. Its reviewer's own output is preserved unedited at `logs/batch-0004.review.log`, and that log is the authority for the findings where the two differ in wording.

Two volume-close artifacts are absent and are carried as absences with a name on them, not as gaps to be filled. `volume-06/volume-06-close.md` is named by four state files as the audit of Volume 06 and has never been written; no phase has invented it. `volume-08/volume-08-close.md` is likewise absent: the Volume 07 close left `volume-07/volume-07-close.md` in place and the Volume 09 phase is required to read it, so the house's convention is that a volume close leaves an artifact here, and this one does not have one. It was not written, not invented and not audited around by the Volume 08 close.
