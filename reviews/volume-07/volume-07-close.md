## 1. Central pressure — *a restriction is not hunger, and a man can be angry at hunger and cannot be angry at a sentence*

**RULED: PAID, and paid in the form the volume's own governing sentence took, which the outline named before the chapters were written and which the chapters gave on the twenty-fifth of May, in a chandler's, in about nine people's hearing, by a man of about sixty of the first lay who counted it.**

The outline's central pressure has two limbs and they rule separately.

**The first limb — the volume's question is whether a coast can be starved on paper in eleven days and no longer in ninety — is PAID, and the answer is on the page of Chapter 350's closing paragraph in the volume's own hand: *a harbor can be closed on paper in eleven days and it takes about ninety to stop a coast, and nobody in this country won anything this month.* The second number is not a figure of deprivation. It is a stage in a trade — a man of thirty-eight of a pressure house was told on the fifteenth of June that his part of a season is ninety days, and every chapter of Batch 0005 carries its own day of the ninety on its own face, the eleventh on the twenty-fifth and the twentieth on the fourth of July.** So the ninety is a working man's calendar and not a metaphor, and the sentence is checkable by a man who was there.

**The second limb — a restriction is worse than hunger because a man can be angry at hunger and cannot be angry at a sentence — is PAID, and it is paid in the outline's own words and in twenty-one.** *A restriction that arrives in a sentence and not in a boat is a thing a man cannot be angry at, and a man who cannot be angry at it will be angry at something else by Friday.* The man of about sixty said it in a chandler's on Tuesday the twenty-fifth of May, counted it, said he did it on purpose, and noticed in about the fourth word that he was doing the very thing he had warned about on the twenty-first. **THE FIRST HALF IS TWENTY-ONE WORDS AND THE SECOND HALF IS UNNUMBERED, AND THE CHAPTER SAYS SO, AND THE COUNT IS RIGHT ON THE PAGE: this audit counts the first half off Chapter 311 word by word and it is twenty-one.**

**AND THE VOLUME PAID THE SECOND HALF. The man who cannot be angry at the sentence gets angry at the chandler, and the chandler's counter is shut to him, and he buys no candle at fourpence, and about sixty candles are four shillings, and the yard refuses to enter four shillings against a man's anger because four shillings is the price of candles and the thing that happened is not the candles (Ch 315). **AND THE VOLUME'S REAL ANTAGONIST TURNED OUT TO BE WHAT THE OUTLINE SAID IT WOULD BE, ON ITS OWN LINE 92: time, and a price list set four hundred miles away by people who will never come here.** A sheet dated the twenty-ninth of May, being a bill of a merchant's agent at Ternwatch and not a quotation, was read out loud at an end stall in about forty people's hearing on Tuesday the first of June, and a barrel of lamp oil went from three hundred and seventy-eight pence in February to four hundred and fifty pence, being a rise of six shillings exactly, with the February figure left visible above the new one and both dates on the board. **It did not come back down, and about eleven tons of oil came in on ten boats at night on no declaration in June and did not bring it down either, and the woman with the slate says so out loud and will not put a figure there, giving the reason that a woman who puts a figure on a board cannot take it off again.** RULED PAID, and the resolution clause's own sentence — *a working harbor of that coast is poorer on the last page of this volume than on the first, and the price of the cheapest oil in it has gone up and has not come back down* — is the last page's last fact and is not a side note.
