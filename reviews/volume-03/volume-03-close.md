# Part 1 — Volume 03 against its own outline, clause by clause

## 1. Central pressure — *the map, and what it costs*

**RULED: RESOLVED, and resolved in the opposite direction to the opening. The pressure was not the cutter. The pressure was the publication. Not deferred, and not discharged in another form — resolved, and the resolution is on a wall.**

The outline's sentence is that *reconstructing the old fleet route becomes a race against rival salvage crews and a naval cutter that wants the map erased*, and that *the volume's job is to make the map cost what a map costs*. Both halves are tested against the fifty chapters. The first half is **not** what happened. The second half is exactly what happened and is better than the plan.

**The cutter did not erase the map. It tried to, twice, over a market wall, and it emptied itself in the process.** The *Vigil*'s notice of Friday the twenty-eighth of July went up on the same nails as everything else in Morrow Quay, on the outside of whatever was there, over the yard's own fourth return of the twenty-second, in about forty people's hearing, with the *Hearthwake* named in it and four people in her and the words *no assistance will be rendered* (Ch 141). A second and longer notice superseded it on Friday the fourth of August, nailed up in the same place in the same afternoon, and the second one is the paper that says **the said vessel and every part of her contents being thereby Crown material**, in a naval officer's own hand, in a fourth and fifth line — and Mara Venn says in the market office on the Friday that it is the only line in either paper that reads like a man writing to himself and that **she does not know why a man writes a longer word in a longer notice** (Ch 145). **A claim brought in that form, over a party's own return, with living people in the boat, is not sustainable, and it was not sustained by argument. It was sustained by a party that produced its book whole, unshortened, in a hearing room on the Saturday, and by a man in a coat who asked four questions and was answered four times and wrote all of it down on his own page** (Ch 149).

**The rival crews are on the page, and they are the reason the cost became permanent.** Noll Tarner of the *Sea Wren* is at the naval pile with his gear, his pump, his papers and his own master's logbook in a naval file, and **this party has not thanked him and has not been asked to** (Ch 141, 150). Perry Ollett of the *Sallow Reach* has a compressor of his own and forty fathoms of his own hose, is a crew of Ashtide, **is not a villain, not a rescuer, not an office and not a device**, and named six marks in a doorway on a Sunday and was paid three at the mole and three is a debt (Ch 142, 147, 150). A man from Ashtide put a printed sheet of his own on the market wall on Monday the twenty-seventh of July in front of about thirty people and did not know what was going to be nailed over it (Ch 141, 143). Sella Brigg was told by this yard to go to a man with a compressor, and about four hundred feet of her own warp came up off the bottom of this harbor on Friday the twenty-eighth of July for twenty-six marks and a day (Ch 141). A printer in Veyr was told three lines in advance in the ordinary post at no cost, cannot be stopped, is not a villain and is a man doing his trade (Ch 138, 150).

**And then the party published on purpose, twice.** The yard's own fourth return went up on the market wall on **Saturday the twenty-second of July**, over the rival's printed sheet, with the argument in its own mouth that **the only thing that makes a wall worth reading is somebody else being able to be asked a question about it** (Ch 138). And on **Tuesday the fifteenth of August at about eleven in the morning, in a market, in daylight**, one sheet went up on the same nails carrying **the place of every copy of it** — about sixty boats of this harbor and of the first, second and third lays and of Ashtide, one copy in a city going to press, four of the eleven copies of the older public sheet not reached and never to be — and **the nine places as this yard is able to state them with a date against each**, and **the two columns, nine lines of the first and five of the second, with a reason against every line of the second** (Ch 150).

**The cost of the map was the map's only advantage, and the cost is named in the yard's own hand with a date, and it is not money.** The yard's advantage was a thing nobody else knew. It is gone. **And the price of having said it in public is a sentence that cannot be said again**: *a yard which has said a thing in a market and has not done it is a yard whose saying is worth nothing in any figure that yard puts on any wall in any year after this one* — entered in the first column in advance at nine o'clock on Monday the seventh of August, in the holder's own left hand, with the breaking and the whole of the cost written down before anybody was wet (Ch 146). Beside it, on the page: **a wage of four marks not paid on the fifteenth of August, the envelope not made, four marks short**, and **three marks owing to a man of Ashtide** (Ch 150).

> **RULING ON THE CLAUSE.** The outline's clause is **satisfied in its argument and discharged in its fact, and the chapter's form is the better one, and the outline is not amended.** The outline asks for a race. The volume gave away the thing the race was for in the first week of Movement 5, on purpose, in front of forty people, after a man of fifty told it in a shed that a wall with nobody able to be asked a question about it is a wall worth nothing. The outline asks for the cost of a map; the volume gives the cost of a **saying**, and a saying is the only thing this book can actually spend, because air and wages and marks all go on a creditor's page and come back and a sentence does not.
>
> **AND PLAINLY, AS THE PHASE ASKS: the central pressure of this volume is the publication and not the cutter. The cutter is the antagonist's method, not the pressure. The rival crews are the mechanism by which the publication became permanent. And the volume's own argument — that a route is public or it is nothing — is not the argument the outline asked for and is a better one, because the outline's version ends with a map won, and this one ends with a map nailed under a naval paper by a yard that is four marks short, cannot pay a boy, and has just broken a sentence it said out loud three weeks earlier.**
