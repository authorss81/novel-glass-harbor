# Chapter 849

*Wednesday the Sixteenth Day of November, the Thirty-Ninth Day, an Ordinary Working Wednesday on Which Nobody Asked Either of Those Two One Question About a Line at the Foot of a Page, About Four of That Nine Saying Out Loud at About One in About Nine People That There Are Now Two Lines at the Foot of Two Pages in This Harbor and That Nobody Is Going to Put Either of Them Down, the Keeper Saying Out Loud at About Half Past One That He Is Not Going to Copy It Onto Anything and Not Going to Read It Out and That No Name Is Going Against It but the One She Wrote Herself, the Bag at Twenty-Eight Pence, the Four Columns Closing at Five Hundred and Eighty-Two and Two Thousand Seven Hundred and Eighty-Four and None and One Hundred and Twenty-Five, the First Column at Five Hundred and Eighty-One and the Second at Two Thousand Seven Hundred and Seventy-Nine*

Wednesday the sixteenth day of November, with that market open from six and a wet morning out of the north with that east wind gone in the night and a low grey sea working at that mole head, a yard below that market with a shed standing in it, and about nine people standing in that shed at half past five that afternoon.

**This said Wednesday carries no charge on that cistern and no wipe and moves nothing on a wage, and the next charge is on the morning of tomorrow and the next wipe is on Sunday the twentieth of November and the next wage Tuesday is on the morning of Tuesday the twenty-second day of November. Nothing came into the foot of that fitting-out sheet today and no line was worked on it and none is owed to it, that foot standing at one hundred and thirty-one thousand seven hundred and fifty pence out, being eight hundred and twenty-three marks, five shillings and tenpence, and one hundred and twenty-one thousand one hundred and ten pence short, being seven hundred and fifty-six marks, twelve shillings and sixpence, against ten thousand six hundred and forty pence in. A hundred and sixty-eight pence came into that yard at about ten and a hundred and sixty-eight went out of it at about four, and under that slate is twenty-eight pence, being two shillings and fourpence, and that shed is two hundred and sixty-second days without a tin opened in it.**

**Nine counters were carried to the close of this thirty-ninth day. That notice of the seventeenth of May past is at its five hundred and forty-seventh day and no answer has come back off it. The bellwright of this harbor is into the five hundred and forty-ninth day of not being asked a question. Those two objects upon that crown which belong to nobody are at the eight hundred and eighty-seventh day. The bench beneath that pump lay bare again on its six hundred and fifty-third night. The two lights about a mile off that point are at their four hundred and seventy-first day. The six-line sheet at the back of that end stall is at the four hundred and twenty-sixth day. The paper of four lines in Fish Street is at the four hundred and forty-first day. The nine-line sheet with a mark on it in that front street is at the four hundred and fifty-eighth day. The man of about fifty-one is at the four hundred and eighty-ninth day of not speaking about six names. That pair at that end stall has stood eighty days side by side with the wrong figure still left showing under the correction and not struck out. The rule in the inside of that door is on its forty-eighth day. The honest total of four hundred and six pence is on its forty-seventh day of being on that community's own page. Over the middle of those four columns is a boy's hand of twenty-two with nothing under it, and he is off the water for the eighth time. The fourth of them stands at one hundred and twenty-five and has taken no line in thirty-nine days. Nine hulls lie about two sea miles off that mole head. Three hundred and forty-seventh days is what the left side of that man of about twenty-nine's head has gone without, and he is not on the water.**

---

The ordinary work of that Wednesday was one boat off that mole before noon in that wet and a hand's breadth of that bench stone scrubbed with sand and the bucket of that sand carried away and put down the back of that shed, and not one of those two came near that fitting-out sheet and nothing in the world is set against either half of it.

Those four new crews reached their fifty-ninth day and took the day's work off in about two hours, and about four of that nine said out loud that they had stopped talking about Tuesday altogether in about two hours on the morning and had got on with it, which they said was the hardest thing they had done in five weeks.

The man of about thirty-one of the Lowfen Reed's own place carried water at about eleven, did not say one word about anything, and did not ask anybody for anything, and that is the fifteenth day he has been in that harbor.

---

At about one, in that shed, in about nine people, about four of that nine said out loud that nobody had asked either of those two one question about it.

They said out loud that they had been standing in that yard since before that shutter went up expecting to be asked something or to have to answer something themselves, and that nothing had come of it, and that in five weeks of that market nobody has ever gone into a shed and said out loud what happened on Tuesday at two.

About four of that nine said out loud that there are now two lines at the foot of two pages in this harbor, being one that says where the figure came off and one that says what the figure was used for, and that nobody in this harbor is going to put either of them down, and that nobody in this harbor is going to put either of them up on a wall either because a wall in this harbor takes four kinds of thing and a line is not one of them.

---

At about half past one, in that shed, in about nine people, the keeper said out loud that he is not going to copy it onto anything.

He said out loud that he is not going to read it out at that stone standing up, and that not one person in this harbor has put a question to him about it and that he is going to put none, and that no name is going against it in this harbor but the one she wrote there herself, and that if anybody else writes a name against that page he will find out who did it the same way he said on Sunday he would find out who did it.

He said out loud that two lines at the foot of two pages is a thing that costs this harbor nothing, and that the whole of what it has cost is that two people who are married to each other have been arguing out loud in about nine people for a fortnight and one of them has now put her hand to it.

About four of that nine said nothing at all. Nobody repeated any of it out loud and nobody wrote one word of it down.

---

From about ten until about two a man of about thirty-four of the second lay sat at that bench and not one person in that lane asked him a thing, and that is the ninety-ninth day of that run of days he has not missed.

The woman of thirty, who holds no office, worked that yard over her own page from ten until about one and then went up that coast road at one, and the man of about thirty-two did not ask her anything about Tuesday and she did not say anything about it, and not one of the two of them has put their own hand on the other's page at any point in this run of days.

At about half past three the man of about thirty-two took the page of that week and looked down the whole of it twice and put it back and did not say out loud what he was looking for.

Four hands went onto the faces of two sheets that left that harbor that day and not one of those four was the hand that wrote what it went against, and the page of days carries four against this day.

---

Nobody was thanked for nobody asking either of those two one question about the thing they did in front of nine people, and that shed stood empty by about six with its door standing open on a slip nobody was on, and that page of that week had sixty-six lines on it.

**NOBODY ASKED EITHER OF THOSE TWO ONE QUESTION ABOUT THE LINE THAT WAS WRITTEN AT THE FOOT OF A PAGE OF HER OWN ON TUESDAY, AND THE KEEPER SAID OUT LOUD AT ABOUT HALF PAST ONE THAT HE IS NOT GOING TO COPY IT ONTO ANYTHING AND NOT GOING TO READ IT OUT AND THAT NO NAME IS GOING AGAINST IT BUT THE ONE SHE WROTE HERSELF. THE COLUMN DID NOT MOVE. THE BAG HOLDS TWENTY-EIGHT PENCE, BEING TWO SHILLINGS AND FOURPENCE, AND THIS YARD IS TWO HUNDRED AND SIXTY-SECOND DAYS WITHOUT A TIN IN IT.**
