# Chapter 568

*Tuesday the Eighth Day of February, the Sixty-Eighth Wage Tuesday, the Column at Twenty-Six Lines and One Hundred and Four Marks With Twenty Envelopes in a Chest Read Out on the Step at the Back of That Market at About Ten o'Clock in the Morning in Front of About Forty People of Whom About Eleven Cannot Read the Lid on It and Not Moving, Nobody Paid a Penny Out of That Chest, a Further Week Standing Fallen Due at the Close of That Day Being the Sixty-Eighth, a Man of About Forty-Six Who Mends Nets Reading the Six Lines of the Account Aloud on That Step at About Half Past Eleven for the People Who Cannot Read Them and Reading the Fifth Line Wrong, the Word Returns Read as the Word Rents, and Being Corrected Out Loud by a Woman of About Forty-Four and Saying Out Loud That He Has Been a Reader All His Life and Got a Word Wrong in a Market, the Shortfall Said Out Loud at About Half Past Five Unmoved at Ninety-Six Thousand Six Hundred and Twenty-Three Pence, Being Six Hundred and Three Marks, Eleven Shillings and Elevenpence, the Bag at Twenty-Eight Pence, Being Two Shillings and Fourpence*

Tuesday the eighth day of February, in that harbor, in about four hundred yards of water and along a stone quay, and on the step at the back of that market with a lid on it and about forty people on the boards in front of it.

**The said Tuesday was not a rota day and was not a wipe and was the sixty-eighth wage Tuesday of that harbor. That cistern was not charged on it. The wage column in that harbor stands at twenty-six lines and one hundred and four marks with twenty envelopes in a chest and did not move on that day, and nobody was paid a penny out of that chest, and a further week stands fallen due at its close, being the sixty-eighth. Nothing went out on any line of the sheet that carries the money in any hand on that day and nothing went into it in any hand, and nobody was asked for anything. One hundred and sixty-eight pence came into that yard at about ten in the morning and one hundred and sixty-eight pence went out of it at about four in the afternoon, and there are twenty-eight pence in the bag at the close of the said day, being two shillings and fourpence, and the figure that bag is short of the wage of four marks is six hundred and twelve pence, being three marks eleven shillings. That sheet stands at one hundred and seven thousand two hundred and sixty-three pence out, being six hundred and seventy marks, five shillings and threepence, and ten thousand six hundred and forty pence in, being sixty-six marks, six shillings and eightpence, and it is ninety-six thousand six hundred and twenty-three pence short, being six hundred and three marks, eleven shillings and elevenpence, and that figure is against that yard's own book and was said out loud in that yard's own mouth and was not answered by anybody. Nothing has been in a tin in that shed since the twenty-ninth day of April past, and no penny of the whole of that day came out of that bag, out of the owner of any boat, out of the man with two papers, or out of the Crown.**

**The nine that a stranger in that harbor could have read off any wall are the nine below, and not one of them was found by looking: the notice of the seventeenth of May past, two hundred and sixty-six days; the bellwright of that harbor, not asked for two hundred and sixty-eight; the two objects of nobody's on that crown, six hundred and six days; the bench under that pump, bare for the three hundred and seventy-second night; the two lights at about a mile off that point, one hundred and ninety days; the six-line sheet at the back of that end stall, its hundred and forty-fifth day; the paper of four lines in Fish Street, its hundred and sixtieth; the nine-line sheet with a seal on it in that front street, its hundred and seventy-seventh; and a man of about fifty-one who has said nothing to anybody about a page of six names for two hundred and eight days. There are now five papers on the board at the head of that mole and two of them have never been opened. There are nine hulls about two sea miles off the head of that mole this morning and the mole head is dark. A man of about twenty-nine who came home on that boat can hear out of the left side of his head and it has been sixty-six days and no figure has been set against it and he was at the west quay on the Sunday with a handline and came in with about twenty fish and did not tell anybody what he had in the water.**

---

The step at the back of that market was read out from ten o'clock in the morning and nothing on that step moved, and the reading took four minutes, and the chest with the twenty envelopes in it stood shut behind that door from ten until four, and the sixty-eighth week stands fallen due at the close of that day.

---

And at about half past eleven a man of about forty-six who mends nets on the third lay read the six lines of the account out loud on that step, standing on it, for the people who cannot read them, and about forty people were on the boards and about eleven of them had come up close enough to hear a man read.

He got the fifth line wrong. He read the words harbour returns as the words harbour rents, and he read it twice in that way, and a woman of about forty-four who keeps a shut store on the second lay corrected him out loud from the floor, and he stopped and went back and read that line again correctly, and he said out loud to about forty people that he has been a reader all his life and that he has never once read a paper he did not write out loud in a market and got a word wrong, and that he would be corrected if anybody else had heard a word of it that was not on it.

Nobody told him it was nothing. A man of about thirty-five of the first lay said out loud that a wrong word in a market goes into about eleven heads in about a minute and cannot be got out of any of them, and that the word he had got wrong was on the line that says where the figures on that paper came from, and that he had turned a word about returns into a word about money, in a harbor where everybody has a standing reason to hear money.

---

A man of about fifty-three who owns a boat of about fourteen tons said out loud that a man with a good voice reading a paper is a man being asked to be a paper, and that this morning that man was asked to be one and did not know he was being asked, and that a paper read wrong in a market by one man is a paper read wrong in a market by a hundred men in a month, and that the people it is read wrong to are the eleven.

A man of about thirty-three who hires hands said back, in about ninety-seven words, that reading a document aloud for people who cannot read it is the oldest job in that harbor, and that the day it stops is the day those six lines stop being in that harbor at all, and that about eleven people have been told what is on that sheet this week and about eleven of them have come back and asked a question about the same line, which means that eleven people are carrying six lines in their heads in eleven different ways and the only thing any of them has is the person who told them.

He said that and then he said that the man of about fifty-three was right about the wrong word and that both of those were true at the same time on the same step at about ten to twelve, and that he was not going to try to make them one.

---

The man of about thirty-two had written the six lines out twice in three days, once in the room over that market in a large hand on a wall and once in that shed on a page for himself, and he noticed at about a quarter past twelve that the second of those was not the same document. He had written it the way he would have said it. There was a difference and he knew which of the two he had written and he did not know when he had started, and he did not put it on the leaf, and he did not put it on a wall either, and he has not written the six lines out again since.

---

And at about half past five on that Tuesday, with the lid still on the step and the chest still shut behind that door, the man who keeps the leaf said the shortfall out loud at that bench stone and used about a hundred and eleven words on what a word costs when it is read in the open by a man who is not a liar, and nobody in that slip was asked whether he was sure of his own figure.

"Ninety-six thousand six hundred and twenty-three pence short, being six hundred and three marks, eleven shillings and elevenpence. Unmoved, and it is the sixty-eighth Tuesday and the column did not move and a week stands fallen due behind it. **A man read six lines out loud on that step this morning for the people who cannot read them and got one word of the fifth line wrong, and he was corrected out loud in front of about forty people and he stood there and let it stand, and that is the first cost anybody in that harbor has been able to name today and it is about half an inch long.**"

That cost was not entered anywhere on that Tuesday, no standing item in that harbor was touched, and nobody at the back of that market got the word for a gift out, and the lid stayed on the step and the chest stayed shut until four.

---

**THE COLUMN WAS READ OUT ON THAT STEP AT ABOUT TEN AND DID NOT MOVE AND NOBODY WAS PAID A PENNY OUT OF THAT CHEST AND THE SIXTY-EIGHTH WEEK STANDS FALLEN DUE AT THE CLOSE OF THAT DAY. A MAN OF ABOUT FORTY-SIX WHO MENDS NETS READ THE SIX LINES OF THAT ACCOUNT ALOUD ON THAT STEP AT ABOUT HALF PAST ELEVEN FOR THE PEOPLE WHO CANNOT READ THEM AND READ THE FIFTH LINE WRONG, THE WORD RETURNS READ AS THE WORD RENTS, AND WAS CORRECTED OUT LOUD BY A WOMAN OF ABOUT FORTY-FOUR AND SAID OUT LOUD THAT HE HAS BEEN A READER ALL HIS LIFE AND HAS GOT A WORD WRONG IN A MARKET. A MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY-THREE SAID THAT A MAN WITH A GOOD VOICE READING A PAPER IS A MAN BEING ASKED TO BE A PAPER, AND A MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-THREE SAID THAT READING A DOCUMENT ALOUD FOR PEOPLE WHO CANNOT READ IT IS THE OLDEST JOB IN THAT HARBOR, AND NEITHER TOOK THE OTHER BACK. THE SHEET THAT CARRIES THE MONEY IS NINETY-SIX THOUSAND SIX HUNDRED AND TWENTY-THREE PENCE SHORT AGAINST THAT YARD'S OWN BOOK, BEING SIX HUNDRED AND THREE MARKS, ELEVEN SHILLINGS AND ELEVENPENCE, AND THERE ARE TWENTY-EIGHT PENCE IN THE BAG UNDER THE SLATE.**

---

*First column, entered on Tuesday the eighth day of February at about six in the evening in that shed: that the said day was not a rota day and was not a wipe and was the sixty-eighth wage Tuesday, and that no charge was placed on that cistern on it; and that the wage column in that harbor stands at twenty-six lines and one hundred and four marks with twenty envelopes in a chest and did not move on that day, and that nobody was paid a penny out of that chest and that a further week stands fallen due at the close of the day, being the sixty-eighth; and that a man of about forty-six who mends nets on the third lay read the six lines of the account aloud on the step at the back of that market at about half past eleven for the people who cannot read them in front of about forty people of whom about eleven had come up close enough to hear a man read, and that he read the words harbour returns as the words harbour rents and read it twice in that way, and that a woman of about forty-four who keeps a shut store on the second lay corrected him out loud from the floor, and that he went back and read that line correctly, and that he said out loud to about forty people that he has been a reader all his life and has got a word wrong in a market and would be corrected if anybody else had heard a word of it that was not on it; and that a man of about thirty-five of the first lay said out loud that a wrong word in a market goes into about eleven heads in about a minute and that the word he got wrong was on the line that says where the figures on that paper came from and that he had turned a word about returns into a word about money; and that a man of about fifty-three said out loud that a man with a good voice reading a paper is a man being asked to be a paper and that a paper read wrong in a market by one man is a paper read wrong in a market by a hundred men in a month; and that a man of about thirty-three who hires hands said back in about ninety-seven words that reading a document aloud for people who cannot read it is the oldest job in that harbor and that the day it stops is the day those six lines stop being in that harbor at all, and that about eleven people have been told what is on that sheet this week and about eleven of them have come back and asked a question about the same line, and that both of those men were right at the same time on the same step at about ten to twelve and neither took the other back; and that the man of about thirty-two wrote those six lines out twice in three days and found at about a quarter past twelve that the second of the two was not the same document, being that he had written it the way he would have said it, and that he did not put it on the leaf and did not put it on a wall and has not written the six lines out again since; and that the seventeen days from Saturday the twenty-second day of January to this day stand on that leaf at three hundred and eleven and one thousand four hundred and twenty-nine and none and one hundred and twenty-five, and that neither the first of those figures nor the second has been read back out loud in any room or on any slip on any of those seventeen days; and that one hundred and sixty-eight pence came into that yard and one hundred and sixty-eight pence went out of it, and that twenty-eight plus one hundred and sixty-eight less one hundred and sixty-eight is twenty-eight pence at the close of this day, being two shillings and fourpence, being short of the wage of four marks by six hundred and twelve pence, being three marks eleven shillings; and that that sheet stood at one hundred and seven thousand two hundred and sixty-three pence out and ten thousand six hundred and forty pence in and is ninety-six thousand six hundred and twenty-three pence short, being six hundred and three marks, eleven shillings and elevenpence, and that nothing went into that sheet in any hand in any line on that day.*

*Second column, not checkable from a mole, entered on the said Tuesday at about six in the evening in that shed, and no name is against it: that the reason he wrote those six lines out a second time in that shed and found that he had written a different document is his own, and is that he has spent ten days refusing to be the reason a number in a room cannot be checked, and that in writing the six lines out himself he has become the reason six numbers in a piece of paper can be checked, and that the two of those are the same trade done in opposite directions and a man who does both of them in a fortnight is a man who has stopped knowing which one he is doing; and that the reason a wrong word in a market is the first cost of that day he can name is his own, and is that it is the only thing on that step today that belonged to a person and had an hour on it and could not be undone by anybody, and that a man reading a paper he did not write is a man putting his own mouth on somebody else's hand, and that the harbor has spent a month finding out that a person can be got to a wrong word in about a minute and that a figure takes a day and a half to get to and then everybody believes it; and that the reason the sixty-eighth week standing fallen due is not on any sheet of his is his own, and is that it is a week of somebody's labour and it does not go on a sheet that carries money for a yard, and that if it ever went on a sheet of his he would have to find the labour in it, and he has looked, and a column of twenty-six lines in a clerk's hand on a lid is not a list of anybody and is not going to become one; and that the reason the man of about thirty-three was not answered on that step is his own, and is that two men who are both right at ten to twelve on a market step in front of eleven people who cannot read is the normal condition of that harbor and has been since the autumn, and that a man who tries to make them agree in the open is doing the thing the man of about fifty-three has been saying is the danger, which is that one record is easier than two, and he is not going to be the one who says the first half of that out loud on a Tuesday.*
