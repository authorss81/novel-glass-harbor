# Chapter 598

*Thursday the Tenth Day of March, a Rota Day, the Hundred and Thirty-Second Charge on That Cistern at the Back of That Market at About Eight in the Morning in Eleven Trips by Hand and Walked on by None of It, Two Permitted Accounts Going Up at Ten o'Clock in the Morning on Two Coasts, One Here and One at the Port Four Days Down That Coast, About Nine People on Each Quay Knowing That the Other Nine Are Standing There at the Same Hour, a Man of About Fifty-Six of the First Lay Who Lands Fish Coming Back Down That Coast Road at About Ten o'Clock With a No From a Place About Five Miles Up It in About two hundred and fifty-seven Words, Four Communities Asked by That Hour and Two Agreed and Two Named on the Page as Having Not Agreed and Not Argued With and Not Asked a Second Time, Five More Gaps Entered at the End Stall in the Afternoon, the Shortfall Said Out Loud at About Half Past Five in About four hundred and ninety-three Words Unmoved at Ninety-Seven Thousand Six Hundred and Thirty-Six Pence, Being Six Hundred and Ten Marks and Three Shillings, the Bag at Twenty-Eight Pence, Being Two Shillings and Fourpence*

Thursday the tenth day of March, in that harbor, in about four hundred yards of water and along a stone quay, and in a shed with a roof on it, a bench stone, and about nine people standing in it at half past five in the afternoon.

**The said Thursday was a rota day and was not a wage Tuesday and was not a wipe. That cistern at the back of that market was charged for the hundred and thirty-second time at about eight this morning, in eleven trips by hand and no more, and the covers were down on it again before a quarter past nine. The butt stood filled to the rim of the second hoop until about four in the afternoon with not one bucket out of it, and the name at the head of that rota board is still off it. Nothing went into that sheet in any hand in any line on that day and nothing came off it in any hand, and the page of four communities that went onto that bench stone at about ten in the morning is on no sheet in this harbor and is in no minute, and neither of the two accounts that went up this morning is on any sheet. That yard drew a hundred and sixty-eight pence in at about ten in the morning and paid a hundred and sixty-eight pence out at about four in the afternoon, the figure being one mark and eightpence, and twenty-eight pence, being two shillings and fourpence, was in the bag when the day shut, and that bag is six hundred and twelve pence, being three marks eleven shillings, short of the wage of four marks. On that sheet there is one hundred and eight thousand two hundred and seventy-six pence out, being six hundred and seventy-six marks, nine shillings and eightpence, set against ten thousand six hundred and forty pence in, being sixty-six marks, six shillings and eightpence, and the gap between the two is ninety-seven thousand six hundred and thirty-six pence, being six hundred and ten marks and three shillings, and that gap is on that yard's own book and was said out loud in that yard's own mouth and was not answered. Nothing has been in a tin in that shed since the twenty-ninth day of April past, and nothing was paid for that day out of that bag, or out of the owner of any boat, or out of the man with two papers, or out of the Crown, and that includes two nights in a back room off a chandler's counter and a man's two boots and a boat both ways.**

**The nine that a stranger in that harbor could have read off any wall are the nine below, and not one of them was found by looking: the notice of the seventeenth of May past, two hundred and ninety-six days; the bellwright of that harbor, not asked for two hundred and ninety-eight; the two objects of nobody's on that crown, six hundred and thirty-six days; the bench under that pump, bare for the four hundred and second night; the two lights at about a mile off that point, two hundred and twenty days; the six-line sheet at the back of that end stall, its hundred and seventy-fifth day; the paper of four lines in Fish Street, its hundred and ninetieth; the nine-line sheet with a seal on it in that front street, its hundred and ninety-seventh; and a man of about fifty-one who has said nothing to anybody about a page of six names for two hundred and thirty-eight days. The board at the head of that mole carries five papers and two of them have never been opened, and the basket with the second asking in it is still in that boathouse with the paper in it. There are nine hulls about two sea miles off the head of that mole this afternoon. A man of about twenty-nine who came home on that boat can hear out of the left side of his head and it has been ninety-six days and no figure has been set against it and he is not on the water and nobody has sent for him.**

---

That cistern was charged for the hundred and thirty-second time a little after eight this morning, in eleven trips, and the covers were back on it before a quarter past nine, and the butt behind that market stood full to the top of the second hoop from about a quarter past nine until about four in the afternoon and no bucket came out of it, and the two men who carry it up that ladder have still never once been on that rota board and have still never once asked to be.

---

And at ten o'clock in the morning a permitted account went up on that quay, in the open, on the boards at the back of that market, and about nine people were standing there, and it is the four pages of the community that has permitted everything with the wrong hour still in it and the correction under it, and the woman of about thirty-nine who mends net on those boards read all four of them out loud again for the fourth time this week and about eleven people of that market were told what was on them by the man of about forty-six who mends nets on the third lay.

Four days down that coast, at the same hour of the same morning, a second permitted account went up, at a place about a mile inside that port which is not the port and has its own people and its own two hands that keep its crossings between them, and about nine people were standing on that quay when it went up.

The two of those things were arranged by nobody. The hour and the morning were in chalk on a page behind a press in this shed on Thursday the third of this month, and a man of about thirty-one who came up that coast on Saturday read that page and said out loud that he would be standing on that quay at ten o'clock on Thursday the tenth, and the woman of about thirty-nine heard him say it and wrote nothing down, and the man of about thirty-one put the question to the place about a mile inside that port on Saturday evening in their own room in about four minutes, and the answer came back down that coast on Wednesday night in his own hand.

And so about nine people on that quay know that about nine people on this quay are standing there at ten o'clock, and about nine people on this quay know that about nine people on that quay are standing there at ten o'clock, and not one of the eighteen of them has seen the other nine and not one of them has a way of finding out in less than a day and a night of a boat, and about four of them said so out loud afterwards and the rest of them did not.

---

A man of about fifty-six of the first lay, who lands fish at that end stall and is on nobody's crew, came back down that coast road at about ten o'clock in the morning and said what he had got, out loud, in about 257 words, on those boards, to about nine people, and he had been up that road since about six in the morning.

"**I walked up that road at about six and I put the question in one room at about a quarter to eight and I walked back down and got here at about ten, and I did it because a woman stood in a room on Monday and said she wanted somebody who was not her to come and ask, and nobody has asked me to do anything, and if that is a fault in it then it is a fault in it.** The answer is no, and it is no in about nine mouths and not in one, and one of the nine was a man who has kept their crossings for thirty years and one of them was his wife and one of them was the man who owns the boat they all crew, and the man who owns the boat said the most in the shortest time and the man who keeps the crossings said the least and said it twice.

They did not want to be asked. They wanted to know they had been asked, and now they know, and the second of those two is what a person gets out of it and there is no figure anywhere in this country for what the first of them costs, and I have not put his name on anything and I have not put her name on anything and there is no name in this at all except the name of the place, which is on the page and which is theirs and not mine."

Nobody argued with him. Nobody asked him what the man who keeps the crossings said the second time, and about four people in that crowd asked him what the crossing-keeping man had actually said and he told them, in about thirty words, exactly what the man had said, and did not put it on anything.

The page of four communities went onto that bench stone at about a quarter past ten with four lines in it, and the four of them are these. The port four days down that coast has not agreed, and is named as having not agreed, and is not to be asked again. This harbor has agreed. The place about a mile inside that port has agreed. The place about five miles up this coast road has not agreed, and is named as having not agreed, and is not to be asked again. Two have agreed and two have not, and two and two is four, and there is no fifth line on that page and nobody has added one.

Nobody was asked for the rest of it and nobody was thanked for a walk up a coast road and back in the dark. The man of about fifty-six went down to that end stall at about eleven and did not come back up those boards that day, and the page with four lines on it stayed on that bench stone until about four in the afternoon.

And at about four in the afternoon five more gaps went onto the sheet at the end stall, in the same four lines and the same four headings, being the largest number entered on one day this month, and one of the five was entered by the man of about thirty-one who had come down that coast on Wednesday night and had not slept, and nobody asked him anything at all about it.

And at about half past five on that Thursday the man who keeps the leaf said the shortfall out loud at that bench stone and used about 493 words on ten o'clock on two coasts, and nobody in that slip was asked whether nine is a crowd.

"Ninety-seven thousand six hundred and thirty-six pence short, being six hundred and ten marks and three shillings. Unmoved, and nothing went onto that sheet today and nothing came off it, and the bag is twenty-eight pence, and that cistern was charged for the hundred and thirty-second time before the market opened and not one bucket came out of it, and a man of about fifty-six walked up a coast road and back in the dark this morning and is not one penny the poorer for it and that is not the same as it being free.

**Two permitted accounts went up at ten o'clock this morning on two coasts, and about nine people on each quay knew that the other nine were standing there at the same hour, and neither side can find that out in less than a day and a night of a boat, and about four of them said so out loud and the rest did not.** The hour was in chalk on a page behind a press in this shed seven days ago. I did not arrange this morning and neither did anybody in this room, and the whole of the arrangement was a man reading a page and saying out loud that he would be there, and a woman hearing him and writing nothing down, and that is a thinner thing than a plan and it held on two coasts at ten o'clock this morning, and I have been trying to build a thick thing all month.

Four communities have been asked by ten o'clock this morning and two have agreed and two have not, and the two that have not are on the page by name of place, being the port four days down that coast and the place about five miles up this coast road, and neither of them has been argued with and neither of them is going to be asked again, and the second of those two was asked for the first time at about a quarter to eight this morning by a man nobody asked, and the answer came back down on his own two boots.

The third of those columns took nothing today and the fourth took nothing and stands at one hundred and twenty-five with no hundred and twenty-sixth line in it, and the reason it has no line in it is not on a page of mine and I have not entered it in twelve days. One line went in on the first column and five on the second, at about six this evening, and I have not read the first two figures of that leaf out loud in a room this month. I entered one line on the first column at about six that evening and five on the second, and that neither of the first two figures of that leaf has been read out loud in any room this month, and that is the forty-eighth day of it."

Nobody was thanked for the arithmetic and nobody was thanked for two accounts, and that shed stood empty by about six and the door stood open on a slip with nobody on it, and the page with four lines on it was still on that bench stone with the cistern's covers off down the lane.

A MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY-SIX OF THE FIRST LAY, WHO LANDS FISH AND IS ON NOBODY'S CREW, WALKED UP THAT COAST ROAD AT ABOUT SIX IN THE MORNING, PUT THE QUESTION IN ONE ROOM IN ABOUT NINE MOUTHS AT ABOUT A QUARTER TO EIGHT, WALKED BACK DOWN AND SAID OUT LOUD IN ABOUT 257 WORDS THAT THE ANSWER IS NO AND THAT THEY DID NOT WANT TO BE ASKED AND THAT WHAT THEY WANTED WAS TO KNOW THEY HAD BEEN ASKED, AND NOBODY ARGUED WITH HIM. FOUR COMMUNITIES HAVE BEEN ASKED BY THAT HOUR AND TWO HAVE AGREED AND TWO HAVE NOT, BEING TWO AND TWO IS FOUR, AND THE TWO THAT HAVE NOT ARE NAMED ON THAT PAGE BY THE NAME OF A PLACE AND NEITHER OF THEM IS TO BE ASKED AGAIN. NOBODY WAS ASKED FOR THE REST OF IT AND NOBODY WAS THANKED FOR A WALK UP A COAST ROAD AND BACK IN THE DARK. THE SHEET THAT CARRIES THE MONEY IS NINETY-SEVEN THOUSAND SIX HUNDRED AND THIRTY-SIX PENCE SHORT AGAINST THAT YARD'S OWN BOOK, BEING SIX HUNDRED AND TEN MARKS AND THREE SHILLINGS, AND THERE ARE TWENTY-EIGHT PENCE IN THE BAG UNDER THE SLATE.**
