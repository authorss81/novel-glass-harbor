# Chapter 685

*Sunday the Fifth Day of June, a Wipe and the Hundred and Ninth Time That the Wall Behind a Reeve's Bench Has Come Down Bare, Twenty-Six Minutes to Get It Off the Wall and Thirteen Standing Bare and Twenty-Six to Get It Back, Being Sixty-Five the Whole of It From About Ten Past Ten to About Fifteen Minutes Past Eleven, a Minute Longer Each Way and a Minute Longer Bare Than the Sunday Before and the Third Sunday Running That Nobody in This Harbor Has Accounted For, Two of the Nine Answers Not Yet Come and the Man Who Keeps the Leaf Writing Down That He Did Not Go and Find Them, Nothing Read Out of a Gap and No Figure Moved by One, the Money Sheet Untouched and the Bag at Twenty-Eight Pence, Being Two Shillings and Fourpence, the First Column at Four Hundred and Eighteen and the Second at One Thousand Nine Hundred and Sixty-Four*

Sunday the fifth day of June, with that market shut for the morning and standing open for the afternoon, a yard below that market with a shed in it, and about nine people in that shed at half past five that afternoon.

**A wipe, and the hundred and ninth time that wall behind a reeve's bench has come down, and no rota day and no wage Tuesday in it, and that butt behind the market covered from nine until four with no charge on it. Two men began to take that wall off its holders at about ten past ten and it was bare by about half past ten and it was back on its holders at about fifteen minutes past eleven, at the angle it has carried since the twelfth of January, and the thing above the frame is still not whole. Twenty-six minutes off the wall, thirteen minutes standing bare, twenty-six to get it back, which is sixty-five minutes the whole of it, and that is a minute longer each way and a minute longer bare than the Sunday before, and that is the third Sunday running that this harbor has spent a minute it cannot account for. Two of the nine answers had not come and this yard did not go and find them. Nothing went onto the money sheet and nothing came off it, and a hundred and sixty-eight pence came into that yard at about ten in the morning and a hundred and sixty-eight went out of it at about four in the afternoon, and under that slate at the close of the day is twenty-eight pence, being two shillings and fourpence, and no tin in that shed has been opened since the twenty-ninth of April past.**

**Nine counters were carried a day today and every one of the nine by somebody other than this yard. That notice of the seventeenth of May past is on its three hundred and eighty-third day. A bellwright of this harbor has not been asked for three hundred and eighty-fifth days, and his chest stays shut. Two objects of nobody's on that crown are on their seven hundred and twenty-third day. The bench under that pump has stood bare for the four hundred and eighty-ninth night. Two lights about a mile off that point are on their three hundred and seventh day without a figure against them. A six-line sheet at the back of that end stall is on its two hundred and sixty-second day. The paper of four lines in Fish Street is on its two hundred and seventy-seventh. A nine-line sheet with a seal on it in that front street is on its two hundred and ninety-fourth day. A man of about fifty-one has said nothing to anybody about a page of six names for three hundred and twenty-fifth days. The third column of that leaf has taken nothing, nobody has been put in it, and a boy's hand of twenty-two is at the head of it, his surname is on no page, and he is off the water. The fourth column of that leaf stands at one hundred and twenty-five and has taken no line and is neither shut nor struck. Two of the five papers on that board at the head of that mole have never been opened by anybody in this harbor. In that boathouse a basket stands with a paper in it and nothing else in it and nobody has put a hand in. About two sea miles off that mole head lie nine hulls this afternoon and there were nine there on the twenty-first of January, and no word about any of them easing has been said in this harbor. A man of about twenty-nine can hear out of the left side of his head and it is one hundred and eighty-third days, one more than the day before, no figure is set against it, and he is not on the water.**

---

That wall came down bare for the hundred and ninth time and it went back up at about fifteen minutes past eleven and it did not go up whole.

Twenty-six minutes to get it off the wall, thirteen standing with it bare, and twenty-six to get it back, which is sixty-five the whole of it from about ten past ten.

Three Sundays now. Twenty-two and nine and twenty-two on the eighth of May, twenty-three and ten and twenty-three on the fifteenth, twenty-four and eleven and twenty-four on the twenty-second, twenty-five and twelve and twenty-five on the twenty-ninth, and twenty-six and thirteen and twenty-six this morning.

About four people near enough to see it happen saw it happen and went on with what they were doing, which is what happened on the last four Sundays as well, and neither of the two men who does that work has said one word and this yard has stopped asking and is going to go on not asking.

---

At about eleven in the morning seven of the nine answers were in and two were not, and the two that were not were not on the road and not late.

One of the nine had come back on the Friday saying that its community would put two people on that water and would choose between them on the morning, and this yard wrote that down and asked no question about it, and this morning at about eleven it had still not named either of the two.

The other of the nine had not come at all since Wednesday and this yard did not go up that coast road and did not send a second paper and did not ask the cart.

At about half past eleven the man who keeps the leaf stood at the flat of that bench stone in about four people and said one sentence about it out loud, and the sentence was that this yard had asked nine and had heard from seven and that the two it had not heard from were not two that had said no.

Nobody in that four added anything to it and nobody thanked him for saying it.

---

At about two in the afternoon a woman of about twenty-seven with a burn scar across one forearm came down that lane into that yard and asked whether the five people were five people or five routes.

The man who keeps the leaf said that they were five people and that each one of them came from a different community and that the yard had asked nine communities and had heard from seven of them and that which five of those seven it would be was not his to decide and had not been decided.

She said that was the right answer and that she had asked it because she had been in a market in April where a thing like that had been decided by one man in about four minutes, and that she was not going to say anything else about it.

Nobody thanked her for asking and this yard wrote the hour against the question and wrote no name against either of them.

---

At about four in the afternoon the man who keeps the leaf wrote the Sunday line on the page of that week and it says three things, being the three numbers of that wall with the hour against them, that seven of the nine answers were in and two were not and that this yard did not go and find them, and that five people had been asked about and that it was not decided which five.

He wrote no state against any route and he wrote the word witness on that sheet for the first time this month and he wrote against it that a witness is not asked to hold anything and that this yard did not write the word on any of the nine papers going up that road.

Nobody thanked him for the line and nobody argued with the word.

---

And at about half past five on that Sunday the man who keeps the leaf took the shortfall out loud at that bench stone, in about six hundred and thirty-three words, to about nine people, and about four of those nine had been at that stone at about half past eleven in the morning.

"**Twenty-six off that wall this morning, thirteen standing bare, twenty-six back, from about ten past ten to about fifteen minutes past eleven, and that is sixty-five the whole of it and that is the hundred and ninth time, and it is a minute longer each way and a minute longer bare than the Sunday before, and that is three Sundays running. I have not asked either man and I am not going to. A wall that costs a minute more every Sunday is the only thing on this coast that has been going up by itself since the frost.**

**One hundred and one thousand two hundred and thirty-six pence short, being six hundred and thirty-two marks, nine shillings and eightpence. One line on that sheet since the twentieth of April. Ten thousand six hundred and forty pence has ever gone into it, being sixty-six marks, six shillings and eightpence. Four hundred and fifty pence is still on that board in the front street. No charge on that cistern and the covers lay on it from nine until four.**

**A hundred and sixty-eight pence in at about ten and a hundred and sixty-eight out at about four, and twenty-eight plus one hundred and sixty-eight less one hundred and sixty-eight is twenty-eight, and under that slate is twenty-eight pence, being two shillings and fourpence, and that is six hundred and twelve pence short of the wage of four marks, being three marks eleven shillings. No tin in that shed has been opened since the twenty-ninth of April past and this yard is now ninety-eight days without one.**

**Now the two sentences I have to say out loud on a Sunday when nobody can leave. This yard asked nine communities and has heard from seven, and the two it has not heard from are not two that have said no. I said that on that stone at about half past eleven this morning in front of four people and I am saying it again because on a Sunday a fact said once gets turned into an opinion by about two in the afternoon.**

**And the second one. Seven communities have offered to put a person on that bottom and this yard has not decided which five, and it is not going to decide, and if anybody in this market asks me today which five I am going to say that it is not mine to decide and that a yard which picks five out of seven has started to select, and I have spent four weeks refusing to select anything.**

**And one woman of about twenty-seven came down that lane at about two this afternoon and asked me whether the five were five people or five routes, and I said five people out of five communities, and she said that was the right answer and that she had asked it because she had been in a market in April where a thing like that was decided by one man in about four minutes. I have written her question down with the hour against it and no name against it, and I am not going to say one word more about it tonight than that.**

**And the last of it. I have written one word on that sheet this month that I have not written before, being witness, and I have written against it that a witness is not asked to hold anything. That word was not on any of the nine papers that went out of this yard on Wednesday. It went out in somebody's mouth on Saturday and it came back in a sheet of paper on Friday and I put it on a wall this afternoon after asking about it, and I am telling nine people that I did it after and not before.**"

Nobody was thanked for the arithmetic and nobody was thanked for sixty-five minutes, and that shed stood empty by about six with its door standing open on a slip nobody was on, and that page of that week had five lines on it, and that wooden tally slate was still lying flat on that bench stone with nothing on it.

**THAT WALL BEHIND A REEVE'S BENCH CAME DOWN BARE AT ABOUT HALF PAST TEN ON THAT SUNDAY AND WENT UP AGAIN AT ABOUT FIFTEEN MINUTES PAST ELEVEN AT THE ANGLE IT HAS BEEN AT SINCE THE TWELFTH OF JANUARY AND DID NOT GO UP WHOLE, IT HAVING TAKEN TWENTY-SIX MINUTES OFF THE WALL, THIRTEEN STANDING BARE AND TWENTY-SIX TO GET IT BACK, BEING SIXTY-FIVE MINUTES THE WHOLE OF IT FROM ABOUT TEN PAST TEN, AGAINST TWENTY-FIVE AND TWELVE AND TWENTY-FIVE ON THE SUNDAY BEFORE, AND NOBODY HAS SAID WHY IT IS GETTING SLOWER AND THIS YARD HAS STOPPED ASKING THE TWO MEN WHO DO IT. SEVEN OF THE NINE ANSWERS WERE IN AT ABOUT ELEVEN IN THE MORNING AND TWO WERE NOT AND THIS YARD DID NOT GO UP THAT COAST ROAD AND DID NOT SEND A SECOND PAPER. A WOMAN OF ABOUT TWENTY-SEVEN WITH A BURN SCAR ACROSS ONE FOREARM ASKED AT ABOUT TWO IN THE AFTERNOON WHETHER THE FIVE WERE FIVE PEOPLE OR FIVE ROUTES, AND WAS TOLD FIVE PEOPLE OUT OF FIVE COMMUNITIES, AND THAT IT WAS NOT THE KEEPER'S TO DECIDE AND HAD NOT BEEN DECIDED. THE MONEY SHEET IS AT ONE HUNDRED AND ELEVEN THOUSAND EIGHT HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-SIX PENCE OUT, BEING SIX HUNDRED AND NINETY-NINE MARKS AND THREE SHILLINGS, WITH TEN THOUSAND SIX HUNDRED AND FORTY PENCE IN, BEING SIXTY-SIX MARKS, SIX SHILLINGS AND EIGHTPENCE, AND THE DIFFERENCE IS ONE HUNDRED AND ONE THOUSAND TWO HUNDRED AND THIRTY-SIX PENCE, BEING SIX HUNDRED AND THIRTY-TWO MARKS, NINE SHILLINGS AND EIGHTPENCE, AND IT CARRIES ONE LINE AND NO SECOND. NO GAP WAS READ OUT OF AND NO FIGURE ON THE SHEET OF THE FOURTEEN MOVED BY ONE. UNDER THAT SLATE IS TWENTY-EIGHT PENCE, BEING TWO SHILLINGS AND FOURPENCE.**

*Second column, not checkable from a mole, entered on the said Sunday at about six in the evening in that shed, and no name is against it: that at about half past ten in the morning the reason the three numbers of that wall are written out again for the ninth time and not the sum is that the sum is the only figure anybody would repeat and the three numbers are the only ones anybody can check against their own Sunday, and that this yard has now nine Sundays of them and that is the most checkable thing on this coast and it is not going to be the thing anybody repeats; and that at about eleven in the morning the reason two communities not having answered is written as a fact and not as a worry is that a paper which records an absence as a refusal has cost this coast a route already this spring, and the whole of the difference between the two is whether anybody asked, and this yard asked and is going to say so in every slip for as long as it keeps a leaf; and that at about two in the afternoon the reason her question about five people or five routes is written down whole with no answer of this yard's own in it is that she did not ask this yard what it was going to do, she asked it what the shape of the thing was, and that a question about shape gets a shape and a question about action gets nothing on this coast until somebody has acted; and that at about four in the afternoon the reason the word witness went onto that wall in the afternoon and was not on any of the nine papers on Wednesday is that on Wednesday this yard did not have the word, and a word that arrives after the asking and before the doing is the honest order for it, and that this harbor has spent four weeks finding that out the slow way with about eleven inches of empty plaster; and that at about half past five in the afternoon the reason that slip says the word went on after and not before is that a slip is the only page in this yard that anybody reads without a ladder, and if the order of things went onto that wall and the slip did not say what the order was, then the wall would be the only account of it and this yard has four pages and does not want a fifth.*
