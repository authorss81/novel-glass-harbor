# Chapter 154

*The Pilot Comes Back Late, and the Yard Did Not Go and Look*

Wednesday the twenty-third and Thursday the twenty-fourth of August, on the mole's steps, on the crown above the third seam's mark, and in the shed.

**A boat of about forty feet with a leaded keel and no harbor on her anywhere came into the roads on the flood on the Wednesday morning and came alongside the mole about a quarter past nine with a sheet weighted with two stones, and the sheet went up onto the steps, and the stones stayed on it, and the man who put it there did not take the stones off.**

He was fifty-four or a little under and he had been on the water nineteen years and he kept a channel nine miles off this coast by hand with two other men and he had said in June that he would come in the autumn for nothing, late, when the water is the best it is. It was late.

Rowan Trewin put his hand flat on the sheet the way a man does who has carried something a long way and is not going to have it taken off him by a wind.

"**I have read your sheet of the fifteenth,**" he said. "**I read it at Ninefoot on the seventh, in a cabin, with a lamp, and I read it twice, and I want to say before anything else that I did not know it was on a market wall under a naval paper and I would have read it the same. That is not a compliment. It means a man can read your sheet anywhere, which is what you wanted, and I am the man it worked on.

"**I did not come for the bar. You know I did not come for the bar, because I made my figure four times in nineteen years and you have never had mine and I have never had yours, and we have both been perfectly polite about that for three months.

"**I came for five miles of coast.**"

---

**He said the thing the yard had published and had not said out loud, and he said it in about nine sentences, and the yard wrote all of them down.**

"**Your sheet carries the place of every copy of itself. I want that read back in my own words so that I know you have got it right.

"**A page that carries the place of every copy of itself is a page that tells a man where the copies are. That is not a fault in the page. That is what the page is for and you are entitled to it and I would have asked for it. But a man reads that line and he learns something he did not have before, which is that there are about sixty boats, and which sixty boats, and that one of them is in a city at a press, and that a man in a city can go and get it, and that a man in a city who has never been on this coast can hold your nine places in his hand in a fortnight and print them on the other side of it in a month.

"**I have been in nine of those boats. I have asked for a copy in none of them and I am not going to. That is mine to hold and it is why I can say the next thing and you cannot.

"**The next thing is that you have built a thing that only works if somebody can be asked a question about it, and you built it into a sheet that lists where the sheet is. And I want that said in a room and not in a market. I am not being clever. I am telling you where the argument goes: the reason a wall is worth reading is that somebody can be brought up in front of it and made to answer, and the reason this wall is going to be worth nothing in four years is that there is nobody standing in front of it. There is a man with a boat and two other men and a channel nine miles off, and we cannot be brought. Nobody in this harbor can make me answer for a figure I made at nine miles; there is no authority here that can put a man of a storm-belt island on this mole in front of this wall, and if there were I would not stand in front of it and I would not want to.

"**So put that in a room, and if there is no room, then you have put a wall up with nobody able to be asked a question about it, and I told you in a shed in June what that is worth and you have gone on and done it anyway, and I am not saying that to be unkind. I am saying it because I am the man who is going to still be here in nineteen years and you are not, and that is not a thing anybody says at a man at his own steps and it is being said to one now.**"

Calder Marr did not answer him for about a minute, and the tide was making and the mole head's light was not on behind them, and the two objects of nobody's were about nine feet off up on the crown under their tarpaulins and neither of them had moved.

"**Then say the other half,**" said Rowan Trewin, "**because I have said mine and you have a mouth.**"

"**The other half is no,**" said Calder Marr.

"**I know it is no. I want to hear a man say it and I want him to say it where I can hear whether he means it.**"

"**It is no and I mean it, and I am going to give you the reason and the reason is that the only room this party can be got into in the next three weeks is a room somebody else has arranged, and that room has a form in it about a book, and this party did not arrange the room and cannot un-arrange it and is not going to try. So if I take your advice and say the place of every copy in a room, then I am saying it in a room a man chose, at a time a man chose, on a question a man had already written down, and about a wall a man has already got. That is not a room. That is a hearing, and this party has never been in one of those that it did not arrange, and there is no reason in the world to start.

"**And I am not going to pretend to you that I did not choose that. I have chosen it, I know what it is, and I am going to write in my own book with the date that a man of fifty-four who keeps a channel by hand stood on these steps and said the wall ought to be asked questions in a room, and that this party has heard him, and does not agree, and is going on with a market.

"**And I am going to say the other thing while you are standing here, because you came for it and you are not going to go down that hill without it. This party has not gone and looked at five miles of that coast. Not in June, not in July, not in the last three weeks, and not this week, and nobody in this harbor has asked us to and you have not asked us to and I am not going to pretend that we are going to.

"**We published a road in June and we did not walk five miles of it. Eleven miles of water were run to look at two stones and a bar. That is on a wall in a city going to press and it is the one thing about that sheet that this yard cannot do anything about at all, and you are the man who knows it, and I am not going to stand here and be surprised by it.**"

Rowan Trewin looked at him for a while with his hand still on the sheet under the two stones.

"**Then I have got what I came for,**" he said. "**Both halves. That is more than I got in June and I did not come expecting it and I am not going to make a thing of you saying it out loud on your own steps, because a man who says a thing on his own steps is doing it for himself and not for me.

"**Now the bar.**"

---

**And the yard did the thing he asked, and it did it in a block at the top of the sheet, and the sheet went up on the market wall on the Friday.**

> *OURS ONCE, TWO AND THREE-QUARTERS. HIS FOUR TIMES IN NINETEEN YEARS, TWO AND A HALF. WE ARE NOT GUESSING.*

"**That is nineteen words,**" said Rowan Trewin.

"**It is nineteen words and I counted them on the steps and you counted them too and you said eighteen.**

"**I said eighteen.**"

"**Then it is eighteen and I am not going to argue about it with a man who has been counting tides for nineteen years, and the line goes up as it is and neither of us is going back to it.

"**And the reason it is on a wall and not in a book and not in a column is yours and I am going to say your reason out loud so that a man reading it in four years knows it was not ours. A book is ours. A column is ours. A wall is ours. A press is a man's, and there is one in a city with your figure in it going and my figure in it going, and neither of us can stop it, and both of those figures are on that press and neither is going to be withdrawn and this volume is not going to decide which of them is right and I am not going to argue it with you in a shed.

"**And the rest of what you asked for is not on this sheet and I am not going to put it there, and I have said out loud why and I am not going to explain myself out of it a second time.**"

He said it. Trewin took his hand off the sheet, took the two stones off it, and carried the sheet back down to his own boat and put it in a locker with a lamp and a slate in it, and said the last of it from the mole's steps with the tide on the turn.

"**I will be back in the spring and I will not be asked to, and you will not be able to pay me and I will not be able to leave. That is the arrangement and I set it and you did not argue.

"**And I am not thanked. I have not asked to be. I told you in June and I am telling you again because a man of fifty-four is the only person in this harbor who can say a thing like that and then not be asked about it, and I would like that written down with the date, and not on a wall.**"

---

**And on the Thursday the two of them did not go anywhere near the water and did not buy air and did not earn a mark, and they went up onto the crown above the third seam's mark at about eleven in the morning and stood in front of the two objects of nobody's and did not touch them and did not talk about them, and the ship's remark was that ninety-one days is a figure and not a subject.**

Then they came down and wrote it all out, and the two entries that are the price of the day are these, and both are in the first column with the date against them, and the shipwright read them back and he read them back after her.

*That on Wednesday the twenty-third of August instant a man who keeps a channel nine miles off this coast by hand came into the roads on the flood and put a sheet on the steps of this mole, and that he said that a page carrying the place of every copy of itself is a page that tells a man where the copies are; that he has been in nine of the boats and has asked for a copy in none of them and is not going to; and that the reason he is in a position to say that and this party is not is that no authority in this harbor can bring him to stand in front of this party's wall, and that he does not want to be brought and would not stand in front of it if he were; and that this party has entered the whole of it, in his own words and unshortened, and has not improved on any part of it.*

*And that this party has heard him and does not agree with him, and has entered why: that the only room this party can be got into in the next three weeks is a room somebody else has arranged and is holding a form about a book in; that a thing said in such a room is said at a time somebody else chose, on a question somebody else had already written down, and is therefore a hearing and not a room; that this party has never been in a hearing it did not arrange and is not going to start; and that this party has chosen a market, knows what it is doing, and is not going to pretend to a man of fifty-four that it did not choose it.*

*And that this party has not gone and looked at five miles of that coast and has not been asked to by anybody and is not going to in the season; and that the reason it is entered in the first column and not the second is that it is a figure and a man in this harbor can check it by asking this party whether anybody went; and that the reason given against it is a working reason and is on the page: that a yard of two and a half people that has spent a fortnight being asked for a book by an office does not have three days for five miles of a coast it published in June, and that this party is not going to pretend the two are unrelated.*

*And that three hours of a boat's time were given to this party on the Wednesday by a man who did not have to give them and this yard did not pay for them and did not ask for them and has entered that it did not ask, with the reason, which is that a man who keeps a channel by hand came up this coast for nothing in June and told this party he would come again in the autumn for nothing, and that this party did not call him and did not write to him and would have paid him nothing, and that a yard which has been given three hours of a boat for nothing and has entered it as a gift and not as a debt is a yard that can be given a thing.*

**Nothing came in and nothing went out of that bag on the Wednesday or on the Thursday, and a mark and nineteenpence is in it and it is the whole of the money this yard has, and a wage of four falls on the Tuesday next.**

**And the coast road is not resolved and this party is not going to resolve it. It was refused a license in nine sentences on the back of a paper and the road is published and cannot be un-published, and both halves of that are true at the same time: a road nobody else can use is not a road, and a road everybody can use is not this yard's. Both halves of that go up on a wall in this harbor on Friday and neither of them is going away afterward. Nobody in this harbor is going to call it anything else, and a man of fifty-four is going to keep sounding it in the spring and is not going to be thanked for it either.**
