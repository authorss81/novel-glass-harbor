# Chapter 180

*The Twenty-Fourth Wall, and a Reason Against the Line*

Monday the nineteenth and Tuesday the twentieth of October, at the mole's steps, at the head of the dock, and in the shed.

**The wall behind the reeve's bench came down at eight on Monday morning and it came down bare, and that was twenty-four Mondays since the first of May, and the clerk kept his own page and did not mention it, and a sitting was held in that room on Friday the sixteenth and nothing has been put on that board since.**

**And at about ten on the Monday morning the cistern behind the engine house was charged at no figure by four men this yard does not know and this party did not walk it, and that is the sixth rota day of this month and the nineteenth since the third of August, and this party has walked none of the nineteen, and it has held since the nineteenth of June.**

Alden Marr was at the far bench on the Monday from about ten until about four. The tin cup was on the end of it at about nine in the morning and was not on the end of it at about four in the afternoon, and neither brother saw who took it and neither brother has said a word about it and neither brother is going to.

**And at about half past ten on the Monday morning a man of the third lay came back up this hill about a day of work on a wharf, and this party took it, and it is booked for Friday the twenty-third instant, and the figure is not entered because the board is not written until the Friday and a figure written on a note is not a figure paid until somebody puts the money on the counter.**

---

**And on Tuesday the twentieth of October at about eleven in the morning the wage of four marks went in, into an envelope, in the holder's own left hand, with the week written on the outside of it, and it was the third wage out of two days of work in four weeks.**

Twenty-three lines and ninety-two marks in the column, the fifth of March the oldest and staying there. Seventeen envelopes on a bench in the chest. Three weeks fallen due this year with no envelope standing against them, being the fifteenth of August, the fifth of September and the sixth of October, entered in the same breath.

**A mark and twelve shillings in the bag.** That is three hundred and four pence, and it is the same figure the bag held on the evening of the thirtieth of September, less four pence, and the four pence was the postage of a letter in the ordinary post on the second of October, and it is the only money this yard has spent on itself in twenty days, and this party has entered that it does not know what the fifth time is going to cost and is not going to find out by not spending it.

---

**And at about half past nine on the Tuesday night in the shed, with the lamp lit, and the two of them, and the day's entries read back from the top by the ship's hand and read back after her by the holder, and nobody else in the room, the whole of the month went into the first column and four lines of it went into the second.**

Nobody was asked to do anything. There is no instrument in this harbor that would let them and no money and no name in any office that they could use and no person in this town they are allowed to ask.

"**Then say the whole of it out loud once,**" said Nessa Pike, "**and then let us both stop.**

"**Because we have had a month, and the month has been a form about four inches by three and a line in a record in a city and a number in the first column, and if we do not say it out loud tonight in this shed then in four years one of us is going to have to say it in a market and it is going to come out wrong.**"

He said it out loud, in about four minutes, and it is in the book, and it went like this.

"**A leaf went out of a file in a room on a second floor of this town on the second day of September, on the application of a commissioned officer of the Crown Navy by letter, and the Registry agreed to it, and the Registry wrote down a reason on a form of its own, and the two words on that form are *risk management*, and the leaf is not to be produced and the form is not to be produced and a copy of the leaf is held.

"**On Friday the ninth a woman of fifty-eight who has been up those stairs twice in her life handed that form to a woman of thirty at a copying counter, and on Monday the twelfth the woman of thirty came up this hill and told us in a doorway, and she did not ask for anything and we did not ask her anything, and she told us the first of her four grounds of the eleventh of November is about a plate in eleven feet in this mole.

"**On Thursday the fifteenth a clerk of the circuit office served a paper on this yard in that doorway that says the Registry file is before that inquiry, that a leaf is called for and is not produced, and that the reason of the non-production is on a form of the Registry's own which is not going to be produced to us. And on Friday the sixteenth a clerk of that sitting read a schedule into a record in a room and asked one question, and the answer is in a record in a city now and it says there is a woman in this harbor and the yard of Morrow Quay will not say who she is.

"**And a man of fifty-one wrote to a city and would not put his word under a line about water and came up this hill and gave us the letter and would not take a shilling for it. And a man of fifty asked a room in the market office on the Tuesday and then came up this hill on the Wednesday and asked us for a woman, and we sent him down the hill once, and he says he asked in a doorway as well on the Saturday and this party has entered that it does not know that and is not going to.

"**That is the month. That is all of it. And here is the whole of what I can do with it on Monday morning, and I have been going round it in my head for four days, and there are five things and four of them cost money we have not got.

"**One. Go to the second floor and ask for the page. That is a request to an office of a third party about a document in that office's own file, and there is no form for it, and I would be asking a woman of fifty-eight to go up a third time, and she is not going and neither am I.

"**Two. Write to a city. I have not written to a city since the third of May and I am not going to start on a Monday, and a party that has published itself is not a party that gets to be sorry in a street and mean it.

"**Three. Go to the harbor authority and ask for a tenth thing in that ledger. The ninth is the last and a tenth may not be entered and the price of the ninth fell on a man of the first lay out of a Monday and has not been paid and is not going to be thanked for, and that office told me in the open air that its ledger is spent, and I am not going back up those steps with the same request twice.

"**Four. Go to the woman. She priced that on the twenty-ninth of September and the price was not money and I could not pay it and I am not going to offer it now in October because it is the same price and it has not moved and I would be a man trying the same door twice.

"**And five. Be in a room and say it. And we were in a room on Friday and I said a different thing, and the thing I said is in a record in a city, and the thing I said was true, and it is going to be read in about a fortnight by about four hundred people, and it says there is a woman in this harbor and we will not name her.**"

He stopped there, and Nessa Pike let it stop, and neither of them said anything for about a minute.

"**Then those are the five and there is no sixth,**" she said, "**and I am going to say the one thing about them that you have not said, and it is about the fifth one and not about you.

"**You have read that list as a man who has been shut out of four rooms. And you are right, and that is not what is happening. Four of those five are shut, and one of them is not shut, and the one that is not shut is the one we went into on Friday, and it is not shut because a hearing is a room a man may be in without paying for it, and this yard has had two of them in five weeks and has been in the second of them for forty minutes and has not been asked a question about the water.

"**And what came out of it was a line in a record that says there is a woman in this harbor the yard will not name. That is not the door being shut on us. That is us, in a room, with a person, saying a true thing, and the true thing going onto a page a hundred miles off, and neither of us being able to take it off. And you have had your hand on that sentence since the seventh of July and you have known exactly what it does and you have been treating it as a wall.

"**A wall is not a room. A wall has nothing in it you can be made to answer for. A room has a person in it and a clerk with a pen and a thing you said forty minutes after you have said it.**"

"**Say the other half of it,**" said Calder Marr, "**because I have been treating it as a wall since July and I would like to know what it is instead.**

"**Both of those are true and neither of them cancels the other. A hearing is a room this party can stand in for nothing, and what comes out of it goes on a page in a city and cannot be got off. The room is open and the page is not, and those are two different things about the same morning and I have been using one of them to talk myself out of the other for five months.**"

Nobody in that shed remarked on the shape of that sentence, and neither of them improved on it, and it is in the book.

*First column, entered on Tuesday the twentieth day of October instant at about half past nine in the evening in this shed: that the wage of four marks for the week ending on the twentieth instant went in into an envelope in the holder's own left hand at about eleven in the morning, that the column stands at twenty-three lines and ninety-two marks with the fifth of March the oldest, that the count of envelopes on the bench in that chest is seventeen, and that the number of weeks fallen due this year with no envelope standing against them is three, being the fifteenth of August past, the fifth of September past and the sixth of October instant, entered in the same breath and not one after the other; and that the bag held a mark and twelve shillings on the morning of that day, and that is three hundred and four pence, and that the same bag held a mark and twelve shillings and fourpence on the evening of the thirtieth of September past, and that the difference between those two figures is the four pence of the postage of the letter of the second of October instant, and that the whole of what this yard has spent on itself in twenty days is four pence.*

*And that a day of work on a wharf was booked for Friday the twenty-third instant on Monday the nineteenth instant at a hirer's own figure and was taken, and that the figure of it is not entered because the board is not written until the day, and that a figure written on a note is not a figure paid until somebody puts the money on the counter, and that there is no money in the bag out of that day and there never has been.*

*And that the crew's book of this yard was produced whole and unshortened in the hearing room over the tide-gang store on the west basin on Friday the sixteenth instant at ten of the morning, un-represented, and that not one line of it was read aloud in that room, and that no copy of any record was made in that room, and that a schedule of the records before that inquiry was read into the record by the clerk of it and a leaf of the file of the ASTER GANNET was called for and was not produced; and that the holder has entered that the reading of that book in a room has never happened in the whole of this party\'s business and did not happen on the sixteenth, and that it is coming, and that this party has entered that it knows it is coming and is not going to soften a line and is not going to leave one out, and that the week of it is not in this party\'s keeping and is not going to be.*

*Second column, not checkable from a mole, entered on Tuesday the twentieth day of October instant at about half past nine in the evening in this shed, and no name is against it: that the holder entered on the sixth day of October instant that he had got a number in the first column and a woman at a counter and a file on a second floor and a person he cannot pay, and on the twentieth instant he has entered the whole of the month, and that the entry is four lines long and every one of them has a reason against it, and that the reason for the four of them is one figure, which is that the bag holds a mark and twelve shillings and a wage is four marks; and that the fifth of the five things he named is not shut, and that a hearing is a room a party may stand in without paying for it, and that what comes out of such a room goes on a page in a city and cannot be got off, and that he has been treating the second of those as if it were the first since the seventh of July, and that the shipwright told him in this shed that a wall has nothing in it you can be made to answer for and a room has a person in it and a clerk with a pen; and that the reason this line is in this column and not the first is that a man on a mole can check what a day brought and cannot check what a room will be worth in November, and that a party which has said out loud in a market that it produces its book whole is going into the week in which that book is read out whole, and that he is not going to read the second column out, and that he entered on the tenth of September that he did not know whether that was a rule or an answer, and that he entered on the sixteenth of October that he still did not know, and that he is not going to improve on that entry now.*

*And that Alden Marr was at the far bench on the second, the fifth, the eighth, the twelfth, the eighteenth and the nineteenth days of October instant, being six days in twenty, and was not asked for anything on any of the six and asked nothing on any of the six, and that Calder Marr did not go over to him on any of the six, and that neither brother has said why and neither brother is going to, and that this is a single line in this column and either brother can check it on any day of a week.*

**No money came into this yard on the Monday and on the Tuesday a wage of four marks went out of it and nothing else did, and a mark and twelve shillings is in the bag and is the whole of it. Thirty-one on the note, not re-written, three counts unmoved since the fourth of January. Five marks of compressor's gland on that note, unpaid, and useless, because the thing after the gland is the hose. There has been nothing in a tin in that shed since the twenty-eighth of April. Three marks owing to a man of Ashtide for the eighth of August, unmoved, and he was not named in that book again and was not asked a question this month. Seven pence stands owing to this yard's own shipwright for the postage of a letter of the twentieth of August out of her own pocket, and it is a different figure from the four pence of the second of October and the two are not on the same line. Eight marks and a day of a boat owing to this harbor's own bellwright since the twenty-third of May, unargued, and he was not asked anything this month, and the chest in that shed was not opened in this month. Against a woman of this harbor, a weaver of warp, eight shillings and tenpence, undiminished, with her name on the figure because she asked for it and with nothing on any wall. Forty marks standing whole to a boy of twenty-two, and the three nots of the eighth of June stand, and the head of the third column keeps his hand, and the column has not been filled since the first of August. Forty marks to a person of no office, first after food, unpaid since the fifteenth of October, and not one mark of it paid, and a letter of hers in the ordinary post out of a penny of her own, and neither that nor a fair copy at a penny a sheet is a payment. The writ runs to midnight on the first of March next, its four names are four, and the second season is a named absence. The plate at the fifth seam is in eleven feet, seen and not measured and not touched, and the note against it is open, and the eight-clause plan of the seventeenth of January is refused in a record with a date on it. The harbor's own bell is in eight and a half feet off the south end with a frame cut through the casting on one side and no tongue on it at all, and it is not going out and is not going to go out inside four years. The delayed note stands at eleven logged occasions and two of them with no call in them, and nothing was entered in this month and nothing was measured. The custody room was not asked for and no digit of any number in it is in this book. Both figures for the bar of gravel are in type in a city side by side, neither is withdrawn, and the Kettle was not looked at. The two objects of nobody's are on the crown of this mole about a hundred and forty-nine days and neither of them has moved. The light is not on. The north berth is out. The tide gang is four men short. The hard at Ashtide is unanswered. The nineteen winters of a herring skipper are in a naval file, in a list in a city, and in the hands of the woman making that list up, and this yard's four returns went into her hands with the ship's relief in August. The cistern was charged at no figure on six rota days in October and on thirteen days before that and this party walked none of the nineteen, and it has held since the nineteenth of June. The market wall was not touched on any day of this month and the hearing room was used once, on the sixteenth, and the two of them are never the same day.**
