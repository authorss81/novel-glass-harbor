# Chapter 344

*Monday the Twenty-Eighth of June, the Sixtieth Time That Wall Came Down Bare With the Counter Not Restarting, the Fifty-Ninth Charge Walked on None, a Boy of About Seventeen Carrying a Copy Up From the Quay to That Shed for Nothing, Four Copies Written Out on the Backs of Four Papers in a Drawer and One of Them Put on a Boat Going Up That Coast for Coal, a Man of About Fifty-One of Six Men With No Boat Watching Ten Boats Land, and This Yard's Own Count of the Three Standing Figures on the Face of Its Own Ledger*

Monday the twenty-eighth of June, in that shed in the morning, and on the fish quay from about seven in the morning.

**The wall behind the reeve's bench came down bare at eight in the morning on the said Monday for the sixtieth time since the first day of May past, and the counter did not restart, and the clerk kept his own page and did not mention it, and no sitting was held in that room on the said day. The cistern behind the engine house was charged at no figure at about ten in the morning on the said Monday by four men this yard does not know, and this party walked none of it, and that is the fifty-ninth of the charges this party has entered walked on none of since the third day of August past. Nothing came into this yard on the said day and nothing went out of it, and there is nothing in the bag, which is no pence and no shilling and no mark, and the figure that bag is short of the wage of four marks is six hundred and forty pence, being four marks, and it is not going to be paid.**

**And the far bench was occupied on the said Monday from about ten in the morning until about four in the afternoon and at no other hour of it, and the holder of that writ did not go over to the man on it at any hour of the said day, and nothing was put on it and read as a gift, and neither brother has said why. Mara Venn was in that harbor on the said day and going nowhere, of no office, not on the crew, forty marks first after food, neither reduced nor paid. Tobias Wren was on the crown of that mole from ten until four, bandaged in the right hand, hook in the left, eleven pieces of a boat behind him, not thanked, not sent for, not asked, and was not on the fish quay at seven in the morning and about nine people were on that crown in the afternoon and none of them said one word to him about ten boats.**

**And this yard's own count of the three standing figures, made in that shed on the said Monday and not taken out of any other book: in these ten days the wall has come down bare once, being the sixtieth on the said day, and the next is the sixty-first on Monday the fifth of July next, which is outside these ten; the cistern has been charged twice and walked on none, being the fifty-ninth on the said day and the sixtieth on Thursday the first of July next; and no wage Tuesday has yet fallen in these ten days, and one falls on Tuesday the twenty-ninth for the thirty-sixth week, and the column will not move on it, so that thirty-five weeks stand fallen due with no envelope against any of them at the hour of this entry and thirty-six will stand at the close of the next day.**

---

They landed at about seven in the morning at the fish quay and there is no page in this harbor that says what the crossing cost anybody, and there are four things on that quay at seven in the morning and this yard has all four of them and has not put a price on one of them.

The first was physic. A man of about twenty-eight of that harbor's own second lay carried four boxes of it up the quay steps at about ten past seven and put them on the end stall's board, and the woman of about fifty-eight took one box and opened it and looked at what was in it, and then gave it away in that market before eleven o'clock, to about nine people, in about nine small parcels, and she would not take money for any of it and she was asked twice by the chandler's wife and refused twice in the same words, being that a thing that has been on a boat all night is not a thing to charge a price to at eight in the morning.

That figure has been on her board since February and it is not coming down. It is four hundred and fifty pence, being thirty-seven shillings and sixpence, quoted to a woman on the first day of June, and the February figure of three hundred and seventy-eight pence is left visible above it with both dates on the board, and on the February figure that light was out in October, and the man who read the ninth figure of the board out loud on Sunday morning said there were nine figures and one of them had two figures and two dates on it and the other eight had not moved since February, and that still stood on Monday morning and was not going to be moved by ten boats.

The second was flour. About four tons of it in sacks, put in a shed on the quay that belongs to nobody, with a slate hung on the door of it and no name on the slate and every man's name in that yard's own book against what he brought, being Voke, and Sallow, and Kettle, and Lund, and Pride, and Pree, and Rundle, and Ower, and Slee, and Birt, being ten, and Coll Mabry's share not landed because Coll Mabry is off the water for nine days and did not fetch it and nobody fetched it for him.

The third was oil. About eleven tons of it in a lighter that came alongside the quay at half past seven in the morning and was lighter by about a ton than she went out with, and Jens Ower said out loud in about nine people's hearing, and to nobody, that the water went across her on the crossing and that he had no figure for it in tons and would not make one up, and that a lighter has a mark on her that says how much she ought to be down by and that his mark says she is a ton light and that his mark is wrong or the water is wrong and that he did not go out there to find out which.

The fourth was three women.

They came down the steps at about a quarter to eight in the morning and they were wet to the knee and they were cold and one of them was about sixty-two and the other two were about fifty-eight and about fifty-five, and the eldest of them said one thing to the fish quay of that harbor and then did not say anything else for the rest of that day.

"**We have been on the other side eleven days going to a burial and we could not come across, and that is the whole of what we have to say, and we are not going to say it again today, and if anybody wants to hear it they can hear it and we are not going to tell it twice.**"

Nobody asked them a second question. A woman of about thirty-four of that harbor put her hand on the eldest one's arm and walked her into the end stall's back and that is the whole of what was done about it, and the woman of about thirty-four works in no office and is not on a crew and has not been asked about anything and was not thanked.

---

And the man of about fifty-one of the six was on that quay at about eight in the morning and he had not been asked to be and he came anyway, and he stood at the end of it with his hands behind his back for about a quarter of an hour and nobody spoke to him and he did not say anything at all.

Then he said two sentences to a man of about thirty-eight of no standing whatever, and not to the holder of that writ, and nobody improved on either of them.

"**That is four ton of flour and four boxes of physic and eleven ton of oil and three women off a boat, and I am not going to stand here and be a man who says a thing was a good thing.**"

"**I am not going to argue you,**" said the man of about thirty-eight. "**I would say the same two sentences and I have said them at a stall and in this market and in that shed and I have said them four times in ten days and I am not going to say them a fifth time in a market.**"

"**Then don't,**" said the man of about fifty-one, "**and here is the third thing and it is not for you. I am going to lose a berth on Monday and I do not know yet how much of it. I did not ask for this and I was not asked, and there is no boat in this harbor that is mine, and I have been standing on this quay since half past seven this morning working out what it is worth, and it is worth four ton of flour to somebody and it is worth nothing at all to me.**"

Nobody in that harbor put a figure on it, and about nine people were within hearing of it, and it went into the second column of the yard's own book with a reason against the line and no name against it, and the reason is that this yard has no figure for a man of fifty-one losing a berth and has not invented one.

---

The copies went out at about two in the afternoon and there was no money in it and no paper bought, and that is the whole of it and it is worth saying how it was done.

Four sheets came out of the drawer in that shed, which holds seven pieces of paper and the place of the charter is empty, and the four of them were ruled on the back with the columns of the sheet itself and were written out in four different hands, being a chandler's counting wife of about fifty-two for the one going up that coast, a boy of about seventeen who can write his own name for the one going to the end stall, a man of about fifty-one of that harbor for the one going to himself, and the ship's own left hand for the one going to the man of about thirty-one of the third lay, and the man of about thirty-one took his copy and put it in the bag with the chart in it and nobody saw what was on it and he was not asked and did not offer.

The fifth copy is the yard's and it is in that drawer with the charter's place empty and nobody is going to get at it, and the sheet says so on its own face, and the sheet says where the other four are, and the sheet says that the copy going up that coast is to be put up by whoever puts it up and that this yard is not writing to that town and has not written to that town and is not going to.

The boat it went on was an ordinary boat going up that coast for coal, and the coal was for a chandler's stove, and the sheet went in with the coal and it cost the sheet nothing and it cost the coal nothing and the man who took it said that a sheet of paper is a penny and a shilling is forty and that he was not going to charge anybody for a bit of paper that came up his own coal, and that is the whole of the money that changed hands anywhere in this harbor on the night of the twenty-sixth or the morning of the twenty-seventh or this afternoon.

A boy of about seventeen carried it up from the quay to that shed at about ten past two in the afternoon for nothing, having been paid one penny for one word on the nineteenth day of June past, and he did not ask to read it and nobody offered him a copy and he did not have one and did not want one.

---

*First column, entered on Monday the twenty-eighth day of June next at about half past nine in the evening in this shed: that ten boats landed at the fish quay of that harbor at about seven in the morning of the said day, being four boxes of physic, about four tons of flour, about eleven tons of oil in a lighter a ton light, and three women of about sixty-two, fifty-eight and fifty-five who had been on the other side eleven days going to a burial; and that the physic was given away in nine small parcels in that fish market before eleven o'clock by the woman of about fifty-eight with the end stall, who refused money for it twice in the same words, being that a thing that has been on a boat all night is not a thing to charge a price to at eight in the morning; and that the flour was put in a shed on that quay belonging to nobody with a slate hung on the door of it and no name on the slate and ten men's names in that yard's own book against what each of them brought, being Voke, Sallow, Kettle, Lund, Pride, Pree, Rundle, Ower, Slee and Birt, and that the share of a man of about forty-five of the third lay did not land and nobody fetched it for him; and that the oil was about eleven tons and a ton light, and that a lighterman of about twenty-nine said out loud in about nine people's hearing that the water went across her on the crossing and that he had no figure for it in tons and would not make one up, and that a mark on a lighter says how much she ought to be down by, and that the mark says a ton and that either the mark is wrong or the water is wrong and that he did not go out there to find out which.*

*And that the wall behind the reeve's bench came down bare at eight in the morning for the sixtieth time since the first day of May past, the counter not restarting, the clerk keeping his own page and not mentioning it, and no sitting held in that room on the said day; and that the cistern was charged at about ten in the morning and walked on none, being the fifty-ninth; and that this yard's own count of the three standing figures across these ten days is one wipe, two charges walked on none, and no wage Tuesday yet with one to fall on the twenty-ninth.*

*Second column, not checkable from a mole, entered on the said Monday at about half past nine in the evening in this shed, and no name is against it: that a man of about fifty-one of that harbor, being one of six men of that harbor who own five sixths of a boat of about nineteen tons on the foul off the south end of that mole, stood at the fish quay from about half past seven in the morning for about a quarter of an hour and said three sentences to a man of no standing whatever and not to the holder of that writ, being that he was not going to stand there and say a thing was a good thing, and that the man of no standing whatever said he was not going to say the two sentences a fifth time; and that the man of about fifty-one said then that he was going to lose a berth on Monday and did not know how much of it, and that four tons of flour is worth four tons of flour to somebody and nothing at all to him; and that nobody in that harbor put a figure on it and about nine people were within hearing of it, and that it was entered here with a reason against the line and no name against it, and that the reason is that this yard has no figure for a man of fifty-one losing a berth and has not invented one.*

*And that a woman of about thirty-four of that harbor put her hand on the arm of a woman of about sixty-two at the end of that quay at about a quarter to eight in the morning and walked her into the back of the end stall, and that she is of no office and is not on a crew and has not been asked about anything and was not thanked; and that the said woman of about sixty-two said one thing on that quay, being that they had been on the other side eleven days going to a burial and could not come across, and that it was the whole of what she had to say and she was not going to say it again that day, and nobody asked them a second question.*

*And that four copies of the said sheet were written out on the backs of four further sheets from the drawer in that shed on the afternoon of the said day, in four hands, being a chandler's wife of about fifty-two, a boy of about seventeen, a man of about fifty-one of that harbor, and the ship's own left hand, and that a man of about thirty-one of the third lay took his copy and put it in the bag with the chart in it and was not asked and offered nothing; and that the copy bound for a market wall in a town four days up that coast went on an ordinary boat going up for coal at about two in the afternoon, and that the man who took it said that a sheet of paper is a penny and a shilling is forty and that he was not going to charge anybody for a bit of paper that came up his own coal; and that this yard is not writing to that town and has not written to that town and is not going to, and that the sheet says on its own face who is to put it up and that the place of every copy of itself is on it and that the copy of this yard's is in a drawer with the charter's place empty and nobody is going to get at it.*

*And in the fourth column of the leaf ruled on the twenty-seventh day of March past, that night, in the ship's left hand, the sixty-ninth line:*

*What it is not: — that four boxes of physic given away in a market before eleven o'clock is not a harbor fed and is not a figure and is not going to be on a board, and a woman who will not take a price for a thing is not a person who has found a better price; and that eleven tons of oil coming in on a lighter at half past seven in the morning is not a price moving and is not a reason for a price to move and does not touch the ninth figure on the end stall's slate, which is four hundred and fifty pence with the February figure of three hundred and seventy-eight above it and both dates on the board; and that three women off a boat is not a man rescued and is not a thing anybody in this harbor did and is not going to be written down as a thing anybody in this harbor did.*
---

**Nothing came into this yard on the said Monday and nothing went out of it, and there is nothing in the bag, which is no pence and no shilling and no mark, and the figure the bag is short of the wage of four marks is six hundred and forty pence, being four marks, and it is not going to be paid and nobody is to write one word under it saying anybody is going to. The wall came down bare for the sixtieth time and the cistern was charged for the fifty-ninth and walked on none. The far bench was occupied from ten until four and was not approached, and Tobias Wren was on the crown of that mole from ten until four and came down off it and did not. The two objects of nobody's are three hundred and eighty-two days up the crown of that mole and the bench under that pump is bare for the hundred and forty-eighth night, and neither of them has moved. A mole-head light is not lit and its lens has been out since the seventh day of May past and the last figure anybody in this harbor has for oil is a barrel at four hundred and fifty pence, which was three hundred and seventy-eight in February, and on the February figure that light was out in October. A wreck of about nineteen tons lies on the foul off the south end going down by the head and six men of that harbor have no boat and one of them is going to lose a berth on Monday and nobody has a figure for what a berth is worth to him. A harbor can be closed on paper in eleven days and it takes about ninety to stop a coast, and this is the fourteenth day of the ninety, and four boxes of physic came into this harbor this morning and were given away in a market before eleven o'clock, and nobody in this country has won anything.**

**Nothing in that shed was signed. Nobody in that yard was thanked. Nobody in that harbor got a penny out of it.**
