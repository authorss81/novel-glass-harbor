# Chapter 810

*Saturday the Eighth Day of October, the Fiftieth Day, an Ordinary Saturday With No Charge on That Cistern and No Wipe and No Wage Movement, the Count of the Second Hand Taken Once at About Two in Writing and Not Read Out in That Shed or in the Slip, the Four Columns Closing at Five Hundred and Forty-Three and Two Thousand Five Hundred and Eighty-Nine and None and One Hundred and Twenty-Five, the Page Carrying the Rule Made in Public on the Thirtieth of September Going into the Drawer in the Corner of That Room Over the Chandler's Store at About Half Past Three and Leaving No Building, a Child of About Nine at That Door Told the Practice and Not the Contents, the Bag at Twenty-Eight Pence*

Saturday the eighth day of October, with that market open from six and a fine still morning with no wind at all in it and the water glassy at eight and the tide making, a yard below that market with a shed standing in it, and about nine people standing in that shed at half past five that afternoon.

**An ordinary Saturday, being a day with no charge on that cistern and no wipe and no wage movement in it, and there is no charge on that cistern inside this run of days and the next wipe is outside it. Nothing settled into the foot of that fitting-out sheet today and no line was worked on it and none is owed to it, that foot standing at one hundred and twenty-seven thousand and eighty-two pence out, being seven hundred and ninety-four marks, three shillings and sixpence, and one hundred and sixteen thousand four hundred and forty-two pence short, being seven hundred and twenty-seven marks, ten shillings and twopence, against ten thousand six hundred and forty pence in and nothing else in it in fifty days. A hundred and sixty-eight pence came into that yard at about ten on a Saturday as on any other day and a hundred and sixty-eight went out of it at about four, and under that slate is twenty-eight pence, being two shillings and fourpence, and that shed is two hundred and twenty-third days without a tin opened in it, the last having been the twenty-ninth of April past.**

**Nine counters were carried to the close of this fiftieth day. That notice of the seventeenth of May past is on its five hundred and eighth day. That bellwright of this harbor is on the five hundred and tenth day of nobody asking him anything, and the chest against his wall stays shut. Those two objects belonging to nobody on that crown are into the eight hundred and forty-eighth day of themselves. That bench under that pump is on the six hundred and fourteenth night bare. Two lights about a mile off that point are on their four hundred and thirty-second day and no figure of money stands against either of them. The six-line sheet at the back of that end stall is on the three hundred and eighty-seventh day of its standing. The paper of four lines in Fish Street is on the four hundred and second day. The nine-line sheet with a mark on it in that front street is on the four hundred and nineteenth day. On the fiftieth day of this run the man of about fifty-one is on the four hundred and fiftieth day of not speaking about six names, and this run of fifty days ends without his having said one word. The third of those four columns closes empty under that boy's hand of twenty-two, his surname on no page, and nothing is appointed over it and nobody is put on the water by anything on this last day. The fourth of those four closes at one hundred and twenty-five, unlined, unstruck and unshut, and it takes nothing on the last day of this run any more than it took on the first. The nine hulls are off that mole head at the close of this run of fifty days and this harbor closes its fifty days without having said anything whatever about them. The last of the fifty figures of that hearing is the three hundred and eighth day and it is not a better figure than the first of them, and he is not on the water and nobody in this harbor set a figure against it.**

That Saturday's ordinary work was three boats off that mole before noon on a glassy sea, a hatches out of the water by three in the afternoon, and a length of line cut to length against a gauge with the door propped on a brick, and no line went onto that fitting-out sheet and no figure of money is set against any of that. The four new crews were on their twentieth day and did the day's work in about two hours under the man of about twenty-seven of the second lay, and about four of that nine said out loud that this is the last day they will be on that work under that man, and about four said nothing.

The first column of that leaf stood at five hundred and forty-two at half past five on that Saturday and the fiftieth entry went into it at about six, and three of that day's five in the second column had gone in before the reading began and two went in after it, and the four columns close this run of fifty days at five hundred and forty-three and two thousand five hundred and eighty-nine and none and one hundred and twenty-five.

At about two the count of the second hand was taken once. The man of about thirty-one of the second lay who keeps the leaf wrote it out in his own hand on a sheet of his own, and the writing on that sheet is that of the fifty days of this run, twenty-eight of them carry a figure in that column and ninety-one marks went onto faces in these fifty days. He read it back to himself before he put the chalk down and about four of that nine saw him do that and nobody has asked him about it. It was not read out in that shed and it is not being read out now, and it is written down once on that sheet and once on the page of days, and that is the only time in these fifty days it has been written down.

---

At about half past three the keeper carried one sheet into the room over that chandler's store and put it into the drawer in the corner of it, by hand, standing at the drawer. That sheet is the five lines of the rule made in public at that working place on the thirtieth day of September in about nine people, written out in a keeper's hand and with second hands on its face. It went into that drawer and not onto any wall and not onto any board in that lane, and it did not leave that room and it was not sent anywhere, and nothing went up on that wall today and nothing came down off it.

The keeper then wrote on the outside of that drawer what is in it and the day it went in, in the same hand as the five lines and about the size of them, and nobody in that room asked him to and nobody in that lane will read it unless somebody tells them it is there.

A child of about nine was standing at that visitor's door when he came out, and asked him what was in the drawer, and the keeper told that child the practice and not the contents. He said that when a page is going out of a room, one of the people who had to do with it puts a mark on the face of it and that this is what a second hand is. He said that a person is never a second hand on a person. He said that a mark by a second hand is a promise about the future and not a thing anybody gets back, and that a page which has been in a drawer for a year cannot be read by the person who signed it. He did not tell that child what the five lines said and he was not asked to and there is no page in this harbor that has the age of that child on it.

---

The two sheets at that end stall were on their forty-first day at the same size and a hand's breadth apart with the wrong figure left showing underneath the correction and not struck out, and neither of them has been taken down. The leaf from the Cinder Bell was on its thirty-sixth day a step to the left of that refusal and the sheet with the fifteen gaps stood at fifteen, and nobody has moved either of those three sheets and nobody is going to. The wall of the room over that chandler's store is bare and has stood bare since the fourteenth of August, and the four heads in the room over that fish market are claims, corrections put up in public, refusals and restricted records, and all four have been used and there is nothing else on that wall.

The eighth day of October was the day a body sitting inland was to vote on a schedule as a whole and on one entry of it by itself, and what it came to is not on any page of this run of fifty days, and nobody has come down that road about it, and this yard sent a person to be there and has not sent anybody to ask and is not going to.

Nobody went up that hill in these fifty days, the sealed record stayed shut, and the claim against it stands released and is not exercised and is not argued about and not printed here. A man of about thirty-four of the second lay was on that bench from about ten until about one and was not asked anything by anybody, and it is the sixtieth day of that run of days, that run having begun on Wednesday the tenth day of August.

Ten hands went onto the faces of four sheets that left buildings in that harbor that afternoon, and every one of those ten is the hand of a person who was not the person who wrote what the hand went against, and one of those four sheets is the sheet of hours for the week and goes up that coast road in the morning, and the page of days carries ten against this day.

*Second column, not checkable from a mole, entered on the said Saturday at about six in the evening in that shed, and no name is against it: that the reason the count of the second hand was taken in writing and not said in that shed is that a sum said out loud is heard by nine people and written down by one, and the whole of what a column is for is that a stranger can come at it afterwards with a pencil and get the same two figures; that the reason that sheet went into a drawer and not onto that wall is that the wall in that room is read by people passing and a drawer is opened by people who have been told there is something in it, and a rule is for the second of those two and not the first; that the reason what is in it and the day it went in is written on the outside is that a drawer nobody can open without a conversation is a drawer that gets emptied in ten years by somebody who was not told what was in it, and that is a worse thing than a page nobody reads; that the reason a child at that door is told the practice and not the contents is that a practice can be had again tomorrow by a person who is nine today and a rule of five lines cannot, and that the whole of what a rule like that one is worth is that somebody can act on it without knowing what it says.*
