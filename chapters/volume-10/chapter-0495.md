# Chapter 495

*Saturday the Twenty-Seventh Day of November, the Party Under Way Since about Four o'Clock on the Friday and Moored off the Third Lay of That Harbor at about Ten o'Clock in the Morning, Eighteen Hours Out of a Figure of Two Days, a Case, a Lock and a Lining of Oilcloth at Three Hundred and Twenty Pence, Being Two Marks, Going onto the Fitting-Out Sheet at About Eleven in the Morning with the Twenty-Seventh Against It and Nothing Going into It in Any Hand and Nobody Asked for Anything, the Shortfall at Nineteen Thousand Nine Hundred and Eighty-Eight Pence, Being One Hundred and Twenty-Four Marks, Twelve Shillings and Fourpence, a Woman of About Thirty-One Who Crews Nothing and Sells Nothing Putting One Sheet of Paper Into a Case and a Case Being a Thing That Can Be Carried and a Case Is Not a Hand, a Brown Page Not Going into That Case and Not Being Cut and Not Being Folded and Being on No Sheet in That Boat or in That Harbor, a Master of That Boat of About Nineteen Tons Coming Ashore With His Two Men and His Boy and Not Being Thanked and Nobody Asking Him Why He Came and Nobody Asking Him to Have Come and No Date Asked For and No Date Given, a Roommate of About Thirty-Eight Not Going into Any Room Over a Fish Market and a Man of About Thirty-One With a Chart in a Bag Not Being Asked for It and Not Producing It, and a Machine Six Centuries Old Still on the Bottom of That Trench With a Crown Man's Marks Set Around It and Nobody in That Harbor Given It and Nobody in That Harbor Stopped From Knowing About It, the Bag at Twenty-Eight Pence, Being Two Shillings and Fourpence, Against a Wage of Six Hundred and Forty*

Saturday the twenty-seventh day of November, in that harbor, in about four hundred yards of water and along a stone quay, and on a boat of about nineteen tons that is lying off the third lay.

**The said Saturday was not a rota day and was not a wage Tuesday and was not a wipe, and no charge was placed on that cistern on it, and the wall behind a reeve's bench in that harbor did not come down on it at all. A case, a lock and a lining of oilcloth went onto the sheet that carries the money at about eleven in the morning at three hundred and twenty pence, being two marks, and nothing went onto that sheet in any hand in any line on that day and nobody was asked for anything. One hundred and sixty-eight pence came into that yard at about ten in the morning, being a day's work with a boat and two men at a berth at the figure in chalk, and one hundred and sixty-eight pence went out of it at about four in the afternoon, being a day asked for by name, and there are twenty-eight pence in the bag at the close of the said day, being two shillings and fourpence, and the figure that bag is short of the wage of four marks is six hundred and twelve pence, being three marks eleven shillings. That sheet stands at thirty thousand six hundred and twenty-eight pence out, being one hundred and ninety-one marks, five shillings and eightpence, and ten thousand six hundred and forty pence in, being sixty-six marks, six shillings and eightpence, and it is nineteen thousand nine hundred and eighty-eight pence short, being one hundred and twenty-four marks, twelve shillings and fourpence. Nothing has been in a tin in that shed since the twenty-ninth day of April past, and not one penny of the whole of that trip came out of that bag, out of the owner of any boat, out of the man with two papers, or out of the Crown.**

**The nine that a stranger in that harbor could have read off any wall are the nine below, and not one of them was found by looking: the notice of the seventeenth of May past, one hundred and ninety-three days; the bellwright of that harbor, not asked for one hundred and ninety-five; the two objects of nobody's on that crown, five hundred and thirty-three days; the bench under that pump, bare for the two hundred and ninety-ninth night; the two lights at about a mile off that point, one hundred and seventeen days; the six-line sheet at the back of that end stall, its seventy-second day; the paper of four lines in Fish Street, its eighty-seventh; the nine-line sheet with a seal on it in that front street, its hundred and fourth; and a man of about fifty-one who has said nothing to anybody about a page of six names for a hundred and thirty-five days. There is no physic in that harbor at fourteen pence a bottle and there is none of it, and there has been none since the middle of September. There is no barley in that market either, and the north berth is out, and the hose is dead until the spring, and the tide gang is four men short. A man of about twenty-nine on that boat cannot hear out of the left side of his head and no figure is set against it anywhere, and the holder of that writ could not get his cup down off that counter at breakfast without putting two fingers on it and no figure is set against that anywhere either.**

---

**A case, a lock and a lining of oilcloth came off that quay at about eleven in the morning on the twenty-seventh and went onto that sheet, and the man who keeps the leaf said the whole of what it was before he wrote it, and nobody argued with him about it.**

"Case, lock and oilcloth. Three hundred and twenty pence. That is the case and not the crate, and the crate is a different thing and is not on this line and is not coming into this harbor in anybody's hand." He put the pen down. "And nobody is to write a second line under that one this week saying what is going to be put in it. Whatever goes in it goes in it in that room over that market, and it is not put in it here and it is not put in it in a shed."

---

**And the sheet of paper went into that case at about two o'clock in the afternoon in a room over that market, with the lock shut afterward and the key in the keeping of the woman who wrote the minute, and nobody in that harbor read it and nobody in that harbor was shown it and nobody in that harbor was asked what it said.**

Sabra Loach, of about thirty-one, who crews nothing and sells nothing, did that, and she said out loud one sentence while she was doing it.

"I want it said that a case is a thing that can be carried and a case is not a hand, and that a thing in a hand is a thing one man has, and that this is a thing a room has. I am not opening it in here and I am not going to say what is in it to anybody in this room, and if anybody in this room cannot read it, that is what the next four days are for and not this afternoon."

Nobody thanked her. Nobody argued with her. Nobody in that room asked her to open it and nobody in that room was told what was in it.

**And the brown page did not go into that case, and it was not cut and it was not folded, and it went up to that room over that market flat on a board with a weight on it and a second weight on the board, and it is on no sheet in that boat and it is on no sheet in that harbor, and a woman of thirty of no office is the only person who knows exactly where it is and she has said that out loud once and has not said it twice.**

---

**And the master of that boat of about nineteen tons came ashore with his two men and his boy at about eleven o'clock in the morning, and he was not thanked, and nobody asked him why he came, and nobody asked him to have come, and nobody asked him for a date, and he gave none.**

"I have said once what I am going to do with my boat and my two men and my boy and it is on a flood that suits me," he said, at about half past eleven, to nobody in particular, "and that was on the eleventh and it has not moved since and nobody is going to get anything out of me by asking me a second time. I would rather be asked once by nine people than nine times by one. I have been thanked by nobody since the eleventh and I have not asked anybody to thank me, and that is the arrangement and I am not changing it this morning because I have come in from two days off."

Nobody thanked him. Nobody argued with him. Nobody put a date on it and nobody wrote one down, and his boat is not put to sea again on any day of that trip.

---

**A roommate of about thirty-eight on that quay was told, at about four hundred yards off it, that there is a sheet of paper in a case in a room over that market, and he said he would not go and look at it and would not be told what was in it, and nobody argued with him and nobody thanked him and nobody asked him why.**

Ruben Skay said it at about one o'clock.

"I have got a thing of my own and I am not going to be asked about it and I am not going to have it, and that is the whole of what I am going to say about it. If you put a second paper in a second box in a second room four hundred yards from that, that is two boxes and not one fact, and I know what two boxes turns into. I have not been in that room and I am not going. You asked me nothing and I have told you nothing, and both of those are the arrangement."

**A man of about thirty-one of the third lay with a chart in a bag was not asked for that chart and did not produce it, and nobody in that harbor has been given that chart and nobody has been stopped from knowing about the machine, and the word that would name the seal on a warrant is in no mouth in that boat and in no mouth in that harbor.**

---

**And at about half past five in the evening the man who keeps the leaf read the sheet that carries the money back in full with the day against every line, and it carries no count of persons, and the three who said no stand on it at the same size as the figures, and none of the three has been asked a second time in any of the forty-five days of that leaf and nobody in that harbor has been asked for the rest of it.**

"Ten thousand six hundred and forty pence in, being sixty-six marks, six shillings and eightpence. Thirty thousand six hundred and twenty-eight pence out, being one hundred and ninety-one marks, five shillings and eightpence. **Nineteen thousand nine hundred and eighty-eight pence short, being one hundred and twenty-four marks, twelve shillings and fourpence, and that figure is against this yard's own book and is nobody's else, and it is said out loud in this yard's own mouth and it has not been answered by anybody and no figure has been asked of any person in that harbor to make it otherwise.** One line went out on that day and nothing went in, and a stranger with a leaf and a pencil can take all three of those figures off that sheet in about a minute and find that they agree.

And on every day of this trip so far one hundred and sixty-eight pence has come into that yard and one hundred and sixty-eight pence has gone out of it, and the whole of the forty-five days is seven thousand five hundred and sixty pence each way, being forty-seven marks, three shillings and fourpence each way, and twenty-eight plus seven thousand five hundred and sixty less seven thousand five hundred and sixty is twenty-eight pence, being two shillings and fourpence, and not one penny of the whole of that trip came out of that bag, out of the owner of any boat, out of the man with two papers, or out of the Crown."

Nobody argued with him. Nobody was asked for the rest of it. The four columns at the close of that day stand at two hundred and twenty-eight, one thousand and fourteen, none and one hundred and twenty-five.

---

**A SHEET OF PAPER WENT INTO A CASE IN THAT HARBOR AT ABOUT TWO IN THE AFTERNOON AND A CASE IS A THING THAT CAN BE CARRIED AND A CASE IS NOT A HAND, AND NOBODY IN THAT HARBOR READ IT AND NOBODY WAS SHOWN IT AND NOBODY WAS ASKED WHAT IT SAID. THE BEST EVIDENCE THIS PARTY HAS EVER HAD IN ITS HANDS IS STILL IN THE RACKS IN A ROOM ON THE BOTTOM OF THAT TRENCH, AND A MACHINE SIX CENTURIES OLD IS STILL ON THAT FLOOR WITH A CROWN MAN'S MARKS SET AROUND IT UNDER A CHAMBER WITH A STOVE IN IT, AND NOBODY IN THAT HARBOR HAS BEEN GIVEN THE MACHINE AND NOBODY IN THAT HARBOR HAS BEEN STOPPED FROM KNOWING ABOUT IT, AND THE WORD THAT WOULD NAME THE SEAL ON A WARRANT IS IN NO MOUTH IN THAT BOAT AND IN NO MOUTH IN THAT HARBOR. NOTHING STANDING IN THAT HARBOR WAS PAID, ADVANCED, REDUCED, IMPROVED, STRUCK OR CLOSED ON THAT DAY. THE MASTER CAME ASHORE AND WAS NOT THANKED AND WAS NOT ASKED WHY HE CAME. THAT SHEET IS NINETEEN THOUSAND NINE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-EIGHT PENCE SHORT AT THE CLOSE OF THIS DAY, BEING ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-FOUR MARKS, TWELVE SHILLINGS AND FOURPENCE, AND NO FIGURE HAS BEEN ASKED OF ANYBODY IN THAT HARBOR TO MAKE IT OTHERWISE.**

*Second column, not checkable from a mole, entered on the said Saturday at about half past five in the evening in that shed, and no name is against it: that the reason the case and the lock and the oilcloth were bought on the twenty-seventh and not on the twenty-second is that woman's of the twenty-second, and is that a case bought nine weeks ago and standing empty in a shed for nine weeks is a case a man has been thinking about every day since, and that a case bought the day a thing is going into it is a case; and that the reason nobody was to write a second line under it saying what was going in is the man who keeps the leaf's own, and is that a line that names the contents of a case is a line that puts the contents in that man's mouth, and that the man who keeps that sheet does not know what is in that case and is not going to be the man who put it there; and that the reason a roommate of about thirty-eight was told about the case in about four hundred yards of water rather than in that room is his own, and is that a man who is keeping a thing of his own that nobody may ask him about cannot also be the man who is told where a second one has been put, and that a yard which cannot keep a thing in two places without making a man the place it is kept in will end up keeping it in him; and that the reason a good passage was not entered as a figure anywhere is the man who keeps the leaf's own, and is that eighteen hours out of a figure of two days is a boat that came home early and not a man who can do it twice, and that a season that flatters a yard once is not a season a yard should be planning on; and that the reason not one standing item in that harbor moved on that Saturday is the yard's own mouth, and is that there is no physic in that market at fourteen pence and there is none of it, and that the whole of that harbor's standing condition was carried in full on the seventh day of November in a sheet of a penny that came out of a coat and was not read out in any other month, and that a day on which a party comes home with a thing in a case has not thereby become a day on which any of it is settled.*
