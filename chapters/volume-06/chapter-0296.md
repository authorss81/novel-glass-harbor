# Chapter 296

*Monday the Tenth of May, the Fifty-Third Time That Wall Came Down, the Forty-Fifth Charge Walked on None, and a Thing Said Twice in a Room With Two People In It*

Monday the tenth of May, in a room in this harbor, and in the shed on the mole in the evening.

**The wall behind the reeve's bench came down bare at eight in the morning on the said Monday for the fifty-third time since the first of May past, and the counter did not restart, and the clerk kept his own page and did not mention it, and no sitting was held in that room on that day or on any other day of this book. The cistern behind the engine house was charged at no figure at about ten on the said morning by four men this yard does not know, and this party walked none of it, and that is the forty-fifth of the charges this party has entered walked on none of since the third of August past.**

**The said Monday was not a wage Tuesday. Nothing came into that yard on it and nothing went out of it. And the far bench was not occupied on the said Monday at any hour, and the holder of that writ did not go over to him at any hour of it, and a man of sixty-one was not asked for anything and asked nothing, and nothing was put on that bench and read as a gift.**

---

Her room is up two flights over a chandler's on a street four streets off the front the weather came through on the seventh, and there is one room in it and there is a table and a chair and a bed and a drawer, and it is hers, and she has been of this harbor since the fourth of January past and she is thirty and she is of no office.

She had the door shut before he came up the stairs. There were two people in that room and there was nobody else in it and nobody was in the passage and nobody was sent.

"**Before you start,**" she said, "**I am going to say the price, and then you are going to pay it, and then we are both going to have been in a room.**"

"**Say it.**"

"**Say it twice.**" She did not sit down and he did not either. "**You said the first time in a shed at half past ten at night in about nine seconds in a voice you told me you could not have repeated if you had wanted to. I have carried that for four years and I have never once been able to use it, because I have never once been able to say to anybody *a man told me in a shed*, and a man in a shed is a man in a room, and that is the whole of what that first time bought and it bought nothing at all.**"

"**And the second time.**"

"**And the second time in your own voice, out loud, in a room, with me in it. Not written down. I am not going to write one word of it and you are not going to write one word of it, and neither of us is going to speak it in a market or on a mole or in about forty people's hearing or in a shed, and if either of us ever does that then one of us has broken this and not the weather.**"

"**Agreed.**"

"**And the price of that is mine and not yours,**" she said, "**and it is this. In about four years there will be a record of this harbor and a man in a city will read it, and there will be nothing in it about tonight, and there will be nothing in it about a thousand nights, and the only thing that will be true of tonight is that a man of thirty-six and a woman of thirty were in a room on the tenth of May. I have decided I can live with that. You have not, and I am not going to help you.**"

"**Then say it,**" he said, "**and I will say it twice.**"

---

"**There is a mark on it.**"

That is all he said the first time, and he said it the way a man says a thing he has rehearsed on a mole for a year and a half and has never said.

"**It is a sound. It is not on the plate and it is not in the plate and it is not a crack and it is not a maker's mark and it is not a number. It comes up out of the bottom of this harbor in a ground line's delay. Three marks and a flick, in a call a boy on a quay used to answer me with when we were eleven, and I have known what it is since the fourteenth of October, and I have not written it in any book, and there is no office in this country that has it, and it is not on any chart and it is not in the custody room and it is not in the reeve's page.**"

"**Whose.**"

"**My brother's.**" He said it to the middle of the table. "**It is Oren's and it is the reason I went into that shard on the thirteenth of October, and it is the reason I want the bottom back, and it is the whole of the reason, and I put two hundred and forty checkable words in a slate instead of it and I have been doing that for four years and you are the only person in this country who knows that.**"

He did not stop. He took about two seconds and he said it again, out loud, in the same room, to the same woman, in the same voice.

"**Three marks and a flick. My brother's call. In no book, in no office, in no chart. The whole of the reason, and never written down.**"

---

She was quiet for long enough that the pump on the street below them could be heard, and it was a different pump.

"**Right,**" she said. "**There. And here is what I am not going to do, and I would like you to hear all four of them, because I have thought about this for a week and I am not going to improve on them tonight.**"

"**Go on.**"

"**One. That does not restore anything. The standing of a certificate of method in a market went on the twenty-second of April and it is not coming back and nothing said in this room brings it back, and the copy of the sheet I was issued is in a drawer in a town four days up this coast in somebody else's keeping and this yard is not going to write to that town. Two. Your forty marks to me stand first after food and they are not reduced and they are not paid and they are not going to be paid because of a thing said in a room, and if anybody in this harbor thinks they have been reduced by tonight they are wrong. Three. I am not going to forgive you and you did not ask and I would think less of you if you had. Four.**" She stopped and started again. "**Four. I am not going to be able to do this again. Whatever it is that a person is, I have got one of them and I have just used it, and there is not a second one behind it, and you should know that tonight because it is the only thing in this room that is mine.**"

"**I know it,**" he said. "**I have known it since the second of January.**"

"**Then you have known it for four months and you have not said one word, and that is the fifth thing, and I am not going to give you the fifth thing.**"

"**I did not ask for five.**"

"**No. You have not asked for anything. That is the whole of what you have never done and it is the reason I am here and it is not going to be the reason for anything else.**"

---

Then she sat down, which she had not done for the whole of it, and neither of them said anything for about a minute.

"**This is not a reunion,**" she said, to the table.

"**No.**"

"**And I am not going to call it one and you are not going to call it one and if a man in a market in four years asks either of us what this was, the true answer is that a man of thirty-six and a woman of thirty were in a room on the tenth of May and that is all, and the second part of the true answer is that the second part of the true answer is in the second part of the true answer.**"

"**That is a very good way of putting it.**"

"**It is the only way there is. I have had a week.**" She got up. "**Go down the hill slowly. It is dark and the front got a pane out of it on Friday and this street did not, and that is not a sign and I am not going to say that it is.**"

He did not thank her and she did not thank him, and neither of them said one word about a brother, and the door was shut and it was said twice in a room with two people in it and it is not in any book and it is not going in any book and it is not going on any wall in this harbor.

---

*First column, entered on Monday the tenth day of May next at about half past nine in the evening in this shed: that the wall behind the reeve's bench came down bare at eight in the morning of the said day for the fifty-third time since the first of May past, the counter not restarting and the clerk keeping his own page, and that no sitting was held in that room on the said day; and that the cistern behind the engine house was charged at no figure at about ten on the said morning and that this party walked none of it, being the forty-fifth of the charges entered walked on none of since the third day of August past; and that no figure was entered against any day in that month that is not a Monday or a Thursday.*

*Second column, not checkable from a mole, entered on the said Monday at about half past nine in the evening in this shed, and no name is against it: that a concealment owed to this yard and owed to nobody else was told on the said evening in a room in this harbor, once and once only, and it was told twice in that room at the price that the person it was told to set it, being that the first time it was told in a shed in about nine seconds in a voice the man said he could not have repeated, and the second time in his own voice out loud where it could be repeated; and that there were two people in that room and there was nobody else in it and nobody was in the passage and nobody was sent.*

*And that the said concealment is in no record, in no office, in no book, and not in any chart notation, and that neither of the two of them wrote one word of it down, and that in about four years the only thing that will be true of the said evening is that a man of thirty-six and a woman of thirty were in a room; and that the reason against the line of writing it down is the reason of the first of March past about a record a man cannot check, and it is not improved on and is not going to be.*

*And that the said thing does not restore the standing of a certificate of method in a market, which was spent on the twenty-second day of April past and is not withdrawn and cannot be and is not going to be restored; that the forty marks first after food stand and are not reduced and are not paid and were not paid by a thing said in a room; and that neither of the two of them said that it was a reunion and neither of them is going to.*

---

**Nothing came into this yard on the said Monday and nothing went out of it, and there are five hundred and ninety-seven pence in the bag, which is three marks, nine shillings and ninepence, and the figure the bag is short of the wage of four marks is forty-three pence and it is not going to be paid. The two objects of nobody's are three hundred and thirty-three days up the crown of that mole and the bench under that pump is bare for the ninety-ninth night, and neither of them has moved, and a thing was said twice in a room and it is in no book, and there is nothing in that shed this evening that says so.**
