# Chapter 281

*The Word Is Named Once in a Shed and Goes in No Column, and a Counter Off the Fish Market Is Decided On*

Sunday the twenty-fifth of April, in the shed on the mole.

**The wall behind the reeve's bench did not come down on the said Sunday and the cistern was not charged on it, and the said Sunday was not a wage Tuesday, and nothing came into that yard on it and nothing went out of it, and the figure in the bag is the figure it has been since Wednesday night.**

**And the far bench was not occupied on the said Sunday at any hour, and the holder of that writ did not go over to him at any hour of it, and a man of sixty-one was not asked for anything and asked nothing.**

---

She was at the bench inside the door because it is a harbor in the third week of April and there is nowhere else to sit in that shed that is not a working bench.

He had been at the other end of it since eight with the leaf open in front of him and nothing written on it, and she watched him do that for about an hour and a half and then she said the thing she had come up the hill to say, which she had not come up the hill to say and had come up the hill about a form.

"I have not got anything you want," she said. "**I have got one word and I have been carrying it about for eleven weeks and I cannot put it down anywhere, and that is the reason I am in this shed on a Sunday.**"

"Put it in the second column," said Calder Marr.

"**No.**"

"No."

"**No, and the reason is that I have got a column on that leaf for a thing a man cannot check, and the second column is for those, and a word is the worst one there is.** A thing I can go and look at is a fact. A number is a fact. A figure cut in a stone is a fact. A word is a person saying a thing in a shed, and the only man in this country who can go and look at that is you, and you were not listening, you were looking at the leaf."

He put the pen down.

"Say it once," he said, "and then I will not ask you a second thing about it for as long as we are both in this harbor."

"**It is called the quietening.**"

It is a soft word. It took up about as much room in that shed as the word *Tuesday* takes.

"**And it is not a name for anybody, and it is not a name for a place, and it is not a title, and I am not going to write it on that leaf and it is not going on any wall in this harbor while I am standing upright.**"

"Then what is it," said Nessa Pike.

Mara Venn turned her hands over on her knees and looked at them, which she does when she is putting a thing into words she has not had to pay for.

"**It is a name for a practice,**" she said. "**That is all it is and that is why I have not been able to put it down. Nine years I was in an office and that office did one thing over and over and the thing had a name. Two people would come in with what they had each read out of the same piece of work, and the two readings could not both be true, and the office would not have the room going on with two of them. So one of the two people was asked, in writing, in a room with a second person in it, to withdraw the reading. Not to change it. To withdraw it. There is a form. I filled in the forms. I asked the questions.**"

"And they withdrew," said Calder Marr.

"**Some of them withdrew and some of them cried in the room and some of them went home and thought about it for a week and then withdrew. And the ones who did not withdraw went on doing it, on their own account, in a shed, with nobody asking them anything, and that is the part of it that matters, and I have not got to the bottom of it and I have had nine years.**"

Nobody said anything for a while. A gull went over the shed roof and did not come back, and the rain that had been in the air all morning came onto the mole in about four drops and stopped, and somewhere down the hill a cart went over the same stones it goes over every day of the week and nobody in that shed heard it stop.

"**I want to say one more thing and then I am going to stop,**" she said. "**I am telling you about a method. I am not telling you about an event. Whatever it was that the method was used on is four hundred miles west under seventy fathoms of water and I have never been within a mile of it and I have never seen one piece of glass out of it and I could not tell you the make of it if you put it on this bench.** And nobody I ever asked said who wanted it. Not one, in nine years, and I asked, and I stopped asking in about the fifth year because I could see what it did to the room."

"**And that is a hole,**" said Calder Marr, "**and a hole in the middle of a thing is not a hole at the back of it.**"

"**Then do not fill it,**" she said. "**You have never filled one in your life. That is the entire reason anyone has ever believed a word either of you said.**"

---

And then he said the thing that made the day, and he said it to the leaf and not to her.

"**I am going down that hill on Monday morning to the counter in the room off the fish market, and I am going to buy a sheet, and I do not know what sheet.**"

"That is a bad reason to spend money," said Nessa Pike.

"**It is the only reason in this harbor that is left.** A sheet is a penny and forty sheets a shilling and that woman has kept that counter for thirty-one years and she will copy anything that is put in front of her and she has never once asked anybody what it is for. **Every other counter in this country wants to know what a thing is for before it will touch it. That is not wickedness, that is what a counter is for. She is the only one I have ever found who will take the money and not the question.**"

"**And what do you think is on the other side of that counter,**" said Mara Venn, and she had not sat down either and she had her coat still on, which she does when she means to be going.

"**I think there is nothing on the other side of that counter and I am going to find out, and I have not got a better plan than that and I have had nothing better than that since the sixth of March.**"

"**Then go on Monday and buy nothing,**" she said, "**and come back and tell me what she said, and do not tell me what is on the sheet if there is one, and if there is one you are going to want to tell me very badly, and that is the day I stop coming up this hill.**"

"I will not tell you what is on it," he said.

"**You will not want to be able to not tell me,**" she said, "**and that is worse, and you know it is worse, and I have not come up this hill for nine days to be comfortable.**"

---

And in the fourth column of the leaf ruled on the twenty-seventh of March past, that evening, in the ship's left hand, the twelfth line:

*What it is not: — that the word is not a name for anybody and is not a name for a place and is not a title and is not a name for an office, and is not on any wall in this harbor; and that a word said once in a shed by a person of no office is not a record and is not a page and is not a drawer, and no man in this country can go and check it, and this yard is not going to write it down in order to look as though it could.*

---

*First column, entered on Sunday the twenty-fifth day of April next at about half past nine in the evening in this shed: that a person of no office of this harbor, thirty, said out loud in this shed on the said morning that the word quietening is a name for a practice and not for a person and not for a place and not for an office, and that the said practice is that a person who holds a reading of a made thing which cannot be true at the same time as another person's reading of it is asked in writing and in a room with a second person in it to withdraw the reading and not to change it; and that the said woman was in that office nine years and filled in the forms and asked the questions; and that she said that the word was said once on the said morning and that it is not written on any wall in that harbor and is not written on the said leaf in any column, and that the said woman came up that hill on her own account and was not asked for anything and was paid nothing.*

*And that on the said day the holder of that writ said out loud in this shed that he was going down that hill on the Monday morning following to the counter in the room off the fish market and was going to buy a sheet, and that he said in his own words that he did not know what sheet, and that the reason against the line of the going is that a sheet is a penny and forty sheets a shilling and the said counter will copy anything and has never asked anybody what it is for, and that the holder of that writ has had no better plan since the sixth day of March past.*

*And that the far bench was not occupied on the said day at any hour and was not approached, and that a man of sixty-one was not asked for anything and asked nothing, and that nothing was put on that bench and read as a gift.*

*Second column, not checkable from a mole, entered on the said Sunday at about half past nine in the evening in this shed, and no name is against it: that the concealment is owed to this yard and was not told on the said day, and that it may not be told in a shed or on a mole or in a market or in about forty people's hearing, and that it is owed to a room with two people in it at a price, and that a person of no office was in that shed on the said day and was not told and did not ask.*

*And that the four people of that coast, four days down it, who go into deep water without a book, were not asked on the said day and were not going to be asked on the Monday, and that the reason against the line of all of it is the reason of the second day of April last about a trade and a price, and that the fact that this yard has a method for reading a made floor four hundred miles from where those four people keep their page is not a reason to ask them and was not used as one.*

*And that Tobias Wren was not sent for on the said day and nothing was asked of him, and that nothing in the said day made him a teacher and nothing made him a door, and that the reason against the line of the not-sending is the reason of the second day of April last and is not improved on.*

---

**Nothing came into this yard on the said Sunday and nothing went out of it, and the figure in the bag is the figure it has been since Wednesday night. A word was said out loud in that shed on the said morning, and it is not on that leaf in any column and it is not on any wall in that harbor, and a man in about four years can read that whole book and will not find it in it, and that is the price of having said it out loud and the price was named before it was paid.**
