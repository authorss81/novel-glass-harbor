and that a man with a board under his arm who walked away from it on the twenty-first has not been let go and has not got away with a thing and is not a defeat and is not a victory and is not in a room and is not in a return; and that a squadron without a ship is not a fall and is not a lesson and is not a thing that yard has won, and that not one penny of it came into that yard and nothing in that leaf came of it; and that the one day of the first of July past stands lost on that line with its reason and no name and no figure, and that no day of any week before that one has been entered against it and none is going to be, and that paying the cost of it is not naming it.*

*And in the fourth column of the leaf ruled on the twenty-seventh day of March past, that night, in the ship's left hand, the hundred and twenty-fifth line, and it is the last thing in that book:*

*What it is not: — that seven boats in company on a flood with a sheet in a master's pocket is not a fleet and is not a company and is not a crew and is not the beginning of one; and that four men saying no is not a defeat and is not a wound and is not a figure against them; and that a hand at the foot of a sheet and an hour against it is not a signature and is not a return and is not a wage; and that a loss of flour and a loss of time and a thing done to a hull is not a death and is not a casualty and is not a figure and nobody was counted; and that a door with a man standing at it is not an opening and a look at one thing is not a release and a time named on a paper is not a price; and that a harbor poorer tonight than it was in May is not a defeat that explains anything and is not a price list improved by anything that happened this month; and that the hundred and twenty-fifth line is the last line in that book and the column is not closed.*
