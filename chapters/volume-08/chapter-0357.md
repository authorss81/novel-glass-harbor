# Chapter 357

*Sunday the Eleventh of July, the Twenty-Seventh Day of the Ninety, Eleven Leaves Read One at a Time in That Shed at About Four in the Afternoon in Six Hands and Five Places, a Boy of Twenty-Two Copying at the Bench Who Is Not on the Stone, a Woman of About Seventy and a Man of About Forty Standing in a Corridor of the Circuit Office Who Were Not Asked and No Request Was Made, a Hand That Is Not a Thing and Cannot Be Handed Over, and Nothing at All Coming into That Yard or Going Out of It*

Sunday the eleventh of July, in that shed, and on that mole, and at the end of the third lay.

**The said Sunday was not a wage Tuesday, was not a rota day and was not a wipe, and the wall behind the reeve's bench came down and went up whole. Nothing came into that yard on the said day and nothing went out of it, and the bag holds seventy-six pence, being six shillings and fourpence, and the figure that bag is short of the wage of four marks is five hundred and sixty-four pence, being three marks and seven shillings, and it is not going to be paid and nobody is to write one word under it saying anybody is going to.**

**And the far bench was occupied on the said Sunday from about ten in the morning until about four in the afternoon and was not approached on it, and the holder of that writ did not go over to him, and nothing was put on that bench and read as a gift, and neither brother has said why. Mara Venn was in that harbor and going nowhere, of no office, not on the crew, not restored, not forgiven, forty marks first after food, neither reduced nor paid, and she read five of the eleven leaves in that shed on the said day in her own hand. Tobias Wren was on the crown of that mole from ten until four, being sixty-one, with eleven pieces of a boat behind him and a boat's hook in his right hand, and he was not sent for and not asked and not thanked, and he is not a teacher and not a door and not a sacrifice, and he is not the man on the far bench, and neither brother went near the other at any hour of the said day.**

---

They read the eleven at about four in the afternoon because that is the hour that shed does things, and there were four of them in it and the four were the holder of that writ, the woman of no office, the ship's hand, and a boy of twenty-two who was not on the stone and has not been on it since the fifteenth of May past and is not going to be put on it this week.

The boy of twenty-two copied. He was not asked to read and he was not asked to write and he was asked to copy, and he copied the headings and the numbers and the hours and did not copy a single word of prose, and that was said out loud in the shed and he said that was all right.

**THE HAND AT THE HEAD OF THE THIRD COLUMN IS HIS AND HAS BEEN SINCE THE FOURTH DAY OF APRIL PAST, AND NOBODY HAS SCRATCHED IT OUT, AND THE NAME THAT SITS BESIDE IT IS NEITHER HIS NOR THE HOLDER'S AND WAS SAID OUT LOUD ONCE IN A BOAT ON THE THIRTEENTH DAY OF MAY PAST AND HAS NOT BEEN SAID SINCE AND IS NOT GOING TO BE SAID AGAIN IN THIS HARBOR. The third column is empty. Nobody has been appointed to it. It has been empty on every day of this volume and on every day of the last one and it is not going to be filled by a boy of twenty-two copying headings at a bench on a Sunday, and the woman of no office said one sentence about that and it was that a column you cannot check is not worth filling and this one cannot be checked by anybody including the man who invented it.**

The five came first, and the woman of no office read them herself and in her own order and not in the order the instrument named them.

"**The first leaf says the two glass objects left this town's deck under one witnessed surrender, and it gives the date, and it gives the two of us who were there, and it says the handover was into the custody room of the circuit office and not into anybody's keeping.**"

"**The second says what was measured, and the third says what was measured a second time by a different man with a different instrument and the two agree inside a sixth of an inch, and the fourth says the cut edges and what a Crown yard case specification looks like, and the fifth says the seal on the case and the fact that the seal is not a claim and is not consent and does not say what the case was for.**"

And she stopped on the fifth leaf for a moment and then said the sixth thing, which was not a leaf.

"**That is what is in the five. What is not in the five is that I made them in about two months, and that the two months were not in any of my working hours, and that nobody has ever paid me for one day of any of it, and that there is a line in that yard's own second column for the first of July with a reason against it and no name in it, and I know what that line is and I have known since the day it went in, and that line is a day of mine, and no name is going on it, and it is not a stage and it is not going to become one.**"

**AND SHE SAID THAT OUT LOUD, ON THE ELEVENTH DAY OF JULY, IN THAT SHED, IN FRONT OF FOUR PEOPLE, AND THE HOLDER OF THAT WRIT DID NOT ASK HER ONE QUESTION ABOUT IT AND WAS NOT ASKED ANY BY ANYBODY, AND NO NAME WENT ONTO THE LINE AND NONE IS TO BE PUT THERE BY HER AND NONE IS TO BE PUT THERE BY ANYBODY IN THAT YARD.**

Then the two in the drawer.

"**The sheet of forty lines, and I have read it out in full twice and I am not going to read it out a third time in a shed on a Sunday.**" — that was the ship's hand, and the drawer was not opened and the sheet was not taken out and nobody asked her to.

"**The corrections leaf, which is mine, and which has one correction on it and one date, and the wrong figure is still on the leaf under the right one and it is not scratched out, and that leaf is the only one of the eleven that anybody can walk in here and read without asking anybody for anything, and I have not thought about that for about a month and I have thought about it about four times since Wednesday.**"

And then the four copies were not read, because two of them are in this harbor and two of them are not, and the woman of no office described where each of them was and did not read one of them, and said that a person who wanted to know what was on a copy should ask the person who wrote it and not ask her.

**AND THE SIX HANDS ARE: hers, five leaves; the ship's hand, two; a copyist of about fifty-nine at a counter off that fish market, one; a herring skipper of fifty-one, one, on the *Anna Ray*; a man of about thirty-six out of his father's book, one, in a coal locker in a town four days up that coast; and a man of about thirty-eight of no standing whatever with a boat of about thirteen tons, one, in his own lockers in this town. Six hands, eleven leaves, five places for the sheet itself and a drawer for two of them.**

---

Line thirteen of the sheet of thirteen lines is a corridor, and the woman of no office said what is in it, and she said it flat and without anything, because it is hers and she was in it in April.

"**There is a woman of about seventy and a man of about forty in that building, on a bench in a corridor, on a Tuesday and a Friday, and they are mother and son, and they have a claim on one of the three things in that room, and the claim is a municipal one and it was entered in a book in April by a clerk of about sixty who wrote both names down and did not ask them for anything.**"

"**The man cannot read. He is the ninth of the eleven in this harbor that cannot, or near enough to it, and I have not counted him twice and I have not counted him at all this week because nobody asked me to.**"

"**They were not asked anything about that paper. Nobody has been sent to them. No request has been made. And the claim cannot be taken back without a request, and this yard is not going to make a request, and I am not going to make a request, and there is nobody in that building that this yard has a reason to speak to.**"

And she said the last part of it looking at the man at the bench and not at the holder of that writ.

"**And that is the thirteenth line and it is a place and no price and no payer, and it is going to stay a place and no price and no payer for as long as it takes, and I have put the reason at the head of the sheet where anybody with a pencil can read it, and that is all I have done about it.**"

---

At about half past five in the evening Calder Marr said the wrong thing, out loud, and it is entered because it was said and not because it was wrong.

"**If it came to it, I would hand the five over.**"

The woman of no office did not answer for about four seconds.

"**You would not. And if you did it would not do what either of us would want, and here is the reason and it is the whole of the last paragraph and it took me a week to see it.**"

"**Five of the eleven are my hand. That is not a thing about paper. Somebody in a city we have not been to is asking for the shape of a hand, and a hand is not given away by a man who happens to be standing near the box it is in. If you gave those five leaves up on Wednesday you would not be giving up a record. You would be giving up a person, and you would be doing it without asking her, and the record would be in your hands and the person would be in an office and neither of those is custody and both of those are a sale.**"

"**And you have not asked me one question about that paper in four days. Not one. You have stood in a market and read four sentences to a woman of sixty-one and you have not come up that hill and asked me what I would do with my own hand, and I have been sitting on that bench since the first of May waiting to be asked and you are not going to, and I have not asked you for anything either, and I am not going to, and I would like it noticed that two people can sit in a house for seventy days not asking each other a question and that it takes one of them saying a wrong sentence out loud to find out.**"

And Calder Marr said nothing for a while and then said that he had not asked her because he had thought there was nothing she could be asked that was any use, and she said that was the answer to the question she had not asked, and that it was a figure and it was a figure of a man about his own age, and that she would not have been thanked for it.

**AND HE DID NOT ASK HER ON THE SAID DAY, AND SHE WENT DOWN THE HILL AT ABOUT SIX AND DID NOT COME BACK UP IT THAT DAY, AND THE FORTY MARKS FIRST AFTER FOOD WERE NEITHER REDUCED NOR PAID.**

---

And at the third lay on the same evening a man of about thirty-two tried the fire party's hose with the standpipe coupling and it was dead and it has been dead since before the spring, and it is on a list and the list is not a schedule and does not bind anybody, and nobody in that lay said anything about it except a man of about forty-five of the third lay who has two fingers of his left hand stiff and has not been to a bonesetter on his own account, who said that a hose is the one thing on that list that costs nothing to write down and nothing to say.

**AND HE WAS NOT ASKED FOR HIS HAND. Nobody in that harbor has a figure for what a hand is worth to a man who has a boat of about thirty tons and it is not on any board in this town and no figure was invented for it and the woman of about fifty-eight with the end stall has not been asked and is not going to be.**

---

*First column, entered on Sunday the eleventh day of July next at about half past nine in the evening in this shed: that the eleven leaves were read one at a time in that shed at about four in the afternoon of the said day in the presence of the holder of that writ, of the woman of no office and of the ship's hand, and of a boy of twenty-two who is not on the stone and has not been on it since the fifteenth of May past and was asked to copy headings and numbers and hours and no prose; and that the said boy copied for about forty minutes and the hand at the head of the third column is his and has been since the fourth day of April past and was not scratched out on the said day, and that the name beside it is neither his nor the holder's and was said out loud once on the thirteenth day of May past and has not been said since; and that the third column was empty on the said day and nobody has been appointed to it; and that the five leaves were read by the woman of no office in her own order and are the date and the two witnesses of the witnessed surrender of two glass objects into the custody room of the circuit office, what was measured, what was measured a second time by a different man with a different instrument and the two agreeing inside a sixth of an inch, the cut edges and a Crown yard case specification, and the seal on the case together with the fact that the seal is not a claim and is not consent and does not say what the case was for; and that she said out loud that the five took about two months, that the two months were not in any of her working hours, that nobody has ever paid her for one day of it, and that there is a line in that yard's own second column for the first of July with a reason against it and no name in it, and that she knows what that line is and no name went on it and none is to be put there by her or by anybody in that yard; and that the sheet of forty lines was not taken out of the drawer and was not read out a third time; and that the six hands are hers with five, the ship's hand with two, a copyist of about fifty-nine with one, a herring skipper of fifty-one with one, a man of about thirty-six with one, and a man of about thirty-eight of no standing whatever with one, being six hands and eleven leaves and five places for the said sheet and a drawer for two of them.*

*Second column, not checkable from a mole, entered on the said Sunday at about half past nine in the evening in this shed, and no name is against it: that in a corridor of the office of the Morrow circuit there is a woman of about seventy and a man of about forty, mother and son, on a bench on a Tuesday and a Friday, and that they have a claim on one of the three things in the building and that the claim is municipal and was entered in a book in April by a clerk of about sixty who wrote both names down and asked them for nothing, and that the man cannot read, and that they were not asked anything about the instrument of the seventh, that nobody was sent to them and that no request was made, and that the claim cannot be taken back without a request and that this yard is not going to make one; and that the holder of that writ said at about half past five in the afternoon of the said day, out loud, that if it came to it he would hand the five leaves over, and that the woman of no office said that five of the eleven are her hand and that a hand is not given away by a man standing near the box it is in, and that he did not ask her one question about the paper in four days, and that he said he had not asked her because he had thought there was nothing she could be asked that was any use, and that she said that was the answer to the question she had not asked; and that the fire party's hose was tried with a standpipe coupling at about half past five by a man of about thirty-two and is dead and has been dead since before the spring, and that it is on a list and the list is not a schedule.*

*And in the fourth column of the leaf ruled on the twenty-seventh day of March past, that night, in the ship's left hand, the eighty-second line:*

*What it is not: — that a hand is not a thing and cannot be handed over and is not a record and is not custody, and that five leaves in one person's hand are not five leaves and the instrument asks for a shape and not for a sheet; that a woman of about seventy and a man of about forty on a bench in a corridor are not a claim that anybody here can use and are not a route and are not a constituency and are not the thirteenth line of a sheet of thirteen lines, they are two people who come on a Tuesday and a Friday; that a day lost on the first of July with a reason against it and no name in it is not a stage and is not a sacrifice and was not named on the eleventh of July and was not named on the first, and the woman who knows what it is said so out loud once in a shed and was not thanked for it and did not ask to be.*
