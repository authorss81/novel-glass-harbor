# Chapter 370

*Saturday the Twenty-Fourth of July, the Fortieth Day of the Ninety, a Leaf Read Back in Full With the Count on the Face of It and the Definition of Every Figure on the Face of It, Three Statements About Three Things and a Fourth About a Paper Nobody Asked About, the Whole Standing Inventory Once and Nowhere Else, and a Mole Head at Ten Past Five in the Evening With a Light Not Lit and a Boat of About Ninety Tons Still at the Outer Water*

Saturday the twenty-fourth of July, in that shed at about four in the afternoon, and on that mole at about ten past five in the evening.

**The said Saturday was not a wage Tuesday, was not a rota day and was not a wipe, and the wall behind the reeve's bench came down and went up whole at the angle it has been at since the twelfth and nobody put it back. Nothing came into that yard on the said day and nothing went out of it, and there are two hundred and four pence in the bag, being one mark and three shillings and eightpence, and the figure that bag is short of the wage of four marks is four hundred and thirty-six pence, being two marks and nine shillings and eightpence, and it is not going to be paid and nobody is to write one word under it saying anybody is going to. Nothing has been in a tin in that shed since the twenty-eighth day of April past, and a boat with a man in her for a day is one hundred and sixty-eight pence, being one mark and eightpence, so that bag is a day and a piece of a day, and it is the most money that has been in that bag since the eighteenth day of May past, and this is the second time that figure has been said out loud in that shed and it is not going to be improved on.**

**And the far bench was occupied on the said Saturday from about ten in the morning until about four in the afternoon and was not approached on it, and the holder of that writ did not go over to him, and nothing was put on that bench and read as a gift, and neither brother has said why. Mara Venn was in that harbor and going nowhere, of no office, not on the crew, not restored, not forgiven, forty marks first after food, neither reduced nor paid, and she came up that hill for the reading and for nothing else, and it is the fourth time she has come up it since she came down that coast, being the twenty-second for his leaf, the twenty-third for her answer in writing and this one for the reading. Tobias Wren was on the crown of that mole from ten until four and was on it at about ten past five with a boat's hook in his right hand and eleven pieces of a boat behind him, and he was not sent for and not asked and not thanked, and he is not a teacher and not a door and not a sacrifice, and he is not Alden Marr, and neither brother went near the other at any hour of the said day.**

**And this yard's own count of the three standing figures, made in that shed on the said day and not taken out of any other book: in these ten days the wall came down bare once, being the sixty-third on Monday the nineteenth of July, and the next is the sixty-fourth on Monday the twenty-sixth, which is outside these ten; the cistern was charged three times and walked on none of them, being the sixty-fourth on Thursday the fifteenth, the sixty-fifth on Monday the nineteenth and the sixty-sixth on Thursday the twenty-second, the rota being a Monday and a Thursday; and one wage Tuesday fell, being Tuesday the twentieth for the thirty-ninth week, and the column did not move on it, so that thirty-nine weeks stand fallen due with no envelope against any of them.**

---

They read the leaf back at about four in the afternoon, which is the hour that shed does things, and there were three of them in it and the woman of no office was on the bench.

**The first column of that leaf has one hundred and two entries in it at the hour of the read-back and one hundred and three at the close.** There were ninety-three of them at the close of Wednesday the fourteenth day of July past and ten have gone in since, one for every day from Thursday the fifteenth to Saturday the twenty-fourth, and not one day in two of them, and this evening's own entry is the tenth and makes one hundred and three.

**The second column has three hundred and eighty-seven lines in it at the hour of the read-back and three hundred and eighty-nine at the close.** Three hundred and thirty-nine of them were in it at the close of the fourteenth day of July past. Three of the day's entries went in before the reading of that day and two after it, and that is the shape of that shed's habit and is not a rule, and no reader in any office can check it, and that is what the second column is for. Ten days of that shape is fifty, and three hundred and thirty-nine and fifty is three hundred and eighty-nine, and three of this Saturday's went in before the reading and make three hundred and eighty-seven at the hour of it. **The said figure is that book of that yard's own and is not a count of anything anybody can check from a mole. The first column's one hundred and two and the fourth column's ninety-four, at the hour of this read-back, are counts of entries and of lines, and a day of the first column carries one entry against it whatever else that day printed, so that a stranger with a leaf and a pencil can count both of them. Those are the two definitions and they are printed here so that a later writer with a pencil knows which of them they are counting and at which of the two moments.**

**The third column is empty and nobody has been appointed to it, and the hand of a boy of twenty-two is at the head of it with a name beside it that is not that boy's and is not the holder's, and that name was said out loud once in a boat on the thirteenth day of May past and has not been said since and is not going to be said again in this harbor. The four lines above his named line are four, nameless, and are not struck out, and they are in about sixty boats on that coast. He was not on the stone on any of these ten days and was put on nothing, and nobody asked him one word about the three nots standing against the eighth of June, and they were not asked about on that day or after it.**

**And the fourth column has ninety-four lines in it at the hour of the read-back and ninety-five at the close, and the ninety-fifth is the last thing in that book, and the column is not closed and nobody has closed it and nobody in this yard is going to.**

Then the three of them said what the ten days were, and the holder of that writ said twice, and none of it was argued with and none of it has been improved on since.

**The first was about the water, and the ship's hand said it.**

"**On Thursday the fifteenth a paper of two leaves came down that coast in the coal asking two people to come to a room in Veyr, and both went up on an ordinary boat and came into a room off a street behind the fish market there with a table and six chairs and seven lines under glass, and a man of about fifty keeps the room and has no title in it, and a clerk of about sixty in a room off it wrote the hour and asked nothing. On Saturday the seventeenth the seven lines were read out in about six minutes, and the fifth requires a measuring of that harbor's own mole where it stands in eleven feet to be ordered, and the ordering is the whole of what the room does and the work has not been done and nobody in that harbor wants it done. On Sunday the eighteenth a municipal paper naming two boats and three men was put on the table beside eight lines, and the room wrote that a civilian convoy under such papers is not a vessel returning from beyond the line so the sixth line's door is open to it as well as to the others, and it binds nobody who does not use it. That is the water.**"

**The second was about the harbor, and the woman of no office said it from the bench, and she was not asked for it.**

"**Two people went to a city and came back. Four things were said out loud standing in that room about what a refusal costs, and I said I have no figure for it and will not invent one. A leaf was written under a procedure costing a day and a leaf, and an answer was put back keeping five leaves where they are with no forgiveness and no thanks on it. And nothing came off any board. The cheapest thing in this town is still four hundred and fifty pence, being thirty-seven shillings and sixpence, with February's three hundred and seventy-eight pence, being thirty-one shillings and sixpence, above it and both dates on the board, and the mole-head light is out, and the fire party's hose is dead, and the tide gang is four men short, and a herring skipper with papers of his own said he will not use them for a convoy and nobody said he was wrong, and a man with no papers said he wants none and nobody said he was wrong.**"

**The third was about this yard, and the holder of that writ said it, and he did not look at anybody while he said it.**

"**Two hundred and four pence stayed in that bag for ten days and nothing came in and nothing went out, and that is one mark and three shillings and eightpence and it is about a day and a piece of a day of one boat with one man in her and it is the most money that has been in that bag since the eighteenth of May past. One wipe and three charges and one wage Tuesday went by and the column did not move on the Tuesday, and a man of about forty-four was off the water nine days and is back on it with his hand bound, and a shipwright of about sixty said the binding would hold for a boat's work and a bonesetter of about fifty said it was not his work and would not touch it. And on Wednesday the twenty-first on an ordinary boat down that coast I said in my own voice in front of her once that I am sorry I did not ask her, and she did not forgive me and did not thank me and asked for a procedure instead, and the procedure is a leaf in writing a day before and no asking out loud, and it is not a resolution of anything else, and nothing is to be put under that.**"

"**And there is a boat at the outer water and I have no idea what it is for and I have not sent anybody and I will not, and there is a third channel on a borrowed instrument that nobody has measured and the instrument has gone back up that coast, and a page of forty lines in a town four days up that coast with a man's name against every line of it and nobody knows who copied it onto that wall. And I would like the three numbers on that leaf read out at the end of this month by somebody who was not me, and I would like the second one read out as well even though nobody can check it.**"

"**And there is one more thing and I am going to say it out loud in a shed so that it is on a leaf and not in my head. That paper of the eighteenth names two boats and three men and a tide. Nobody has asked those three men whether they will go. Nobody has written a day on it. There is a room four days up that coast that says the door is open to a convoy with papers in order, and there are two boats on this coast that could be one, and a man of fifty-one with papers of his own who has said in the open he will not, and a man of thirty-eight with none who says he wants none. So there is a door and there is nobody at it. And whoever put that paper on that table was not in the room and gave no name, and the man who keeps it said he would not be asked for one there, which is his right and is also the reason it is still nobody's. I do not know who left it and I am not going to stand on a hill and guess.**"

The woman of no office said nothing at all to that, and the ship's hand wrote it in the second column with the reason against the line that it was said in a shed and not in a room.

---

And then at about ten past five in the evening he went up to the mole head, because that is where he goes, and there is nothing up there and nobody sent him.

The mole-head light is not lit. Its lens has been out since the seventh day of May past and it is out on the twenty-fourth of July as it was on the fourteenth of May, and a light costs oil, and the last figure anybody in this harbor has for a barrel of lamp oil is four hundred and fifty pence, being thirty-seven shillings and sixpence, quoted to a woman on the first day of June, which was three hundred and seventy-eight pence in February, being thirty-one shillings and sixpence, and on the February figure that light was out in October, and the notice of the seventeenth day of May past has been on a wall in a different street for sixty-eight days and has not been taken down and has not been compared with anything by anybody in that harbor.

The small steam tender of about ninety tons was still at the outer water at about ten past five, with two lines out and a lamp at her masthead, and she has not had that lamp before the twentieth, and nobody in that harbor has walked out to the end of the quay to see it lit, and nobody in that harbor has been aboard her and nobody had spoken to anybody in her, and the young man of about twenty-three was in her own wheelhouse and had come out onto the roof twice in ten days and had not hailed.

On the crown of that mole, about nine feet apart under two tarpaulins and about four feet from a page with six names on it in a hand nobody corrected, the two objects of nobody's are four hundred and eight days old, and one of them has had a corner off it since the seventeenth of June past and it has not been lashed back, and neither has moved, and nobody has asked whose either is.

Tobias Wren was on that crown at about ten past five with a boat's hook in his right hand and eleven pieces of a boat behind him, and he did not look up at the mole head and nobody went over to him. The other of them was on the crown of that mole from ten until four on every one of these ten days as well, and the two of them work a crown forty feet apart and have not once gone to each other, and the yard has entered both of them and has not entered a reason for either.

Across on the far bench a man of sixty-one was not there, because it was after four, and had been there from ten until four on every one of these ten days, and there was one shoe on that bench beside him and the shoe is not his, and it has been on that bench since the third of May past and nobody has moved it and nobody has asked.

The bench under that pump is bare for the hundred and seventy-fourth night, and it adds one a night.

The mark on the face of the second seam is fouled about a foot and seven inches on the weather side and nothing on the other, where it was on the nineteenth day of May past, and it was not looked at on any of the ten days, and the mark four hundred yards off it is of a make nobody in this harbor makes, and the season of both is nine marks and neither has been paid and neither is going to be, and the use of the first was taken once on the eighteenth day of June past and closed, four figures and no fifth at any hour of any day, ladder in another man's book, nobody to explain it, not Fisk.

**AND THERE IS NOTHING ON THE PAGE OF THAT CROWN AND NOTHING ON THE LEDGER OF THAT SHED AND NOTHING IN THE MOUTH OF ANY PERSON IN THAT HARBOR THAT ANSWERS THE QUESTION THIS YARD HAS BEEN CARRYING SINCE THE THIRTY-FIRST DAY OF MAY PAST, AND IT WAS NOT ASKED AGAIN ON ANY OF THESE TEN DAYS AND IT IS NOT ASKED AT THE MOLE HEAD TONIGHT AND NOBODY IN THIS COUNTRY IS GOING TO BE ASKED IT BY THIS YARD, and in ten days at that mole head and in that shed not one man has stood in front of another and asked him to stand behind him, and there is a word for a number of men doing that and the word was not said, and the not saying of it is not an achievement and is not going in the fourth column tonight.**

*First column, entered on Saturday the twenty-fourth day of July next at about half past nine in the evening in this shed: that the said leaf ruled on the twenty-seventh day of March past was read back in full at about four in the afternoon of the said day by the ship's hand in her own left hand, all four columns including the empty ones, in the presence of the holder of that writ and of a woman of no office, and that the two definitions of every figure in it were stated on the face of that reading, being that the second column's figure is that ledger's own and is not a count of paragraphs, and that the first column's figure and the fourth column's figure at the hour of the reading are counts of entries and of lines and are checkable by a stranger with a pencil; and that the said leaf holds one hundred and three entries in its first column at the close of the said day, being ninety-three on the fourteenth day of July past and one entry against each of the ten days since, with no day in two of them, and one hundred and two of them at the hour of the reading; three hundred and eighty-nine lines in its second; none in its third; and ninety-five in its fourth, of which the ninety-fifth is the last thing in that book, and the column is not closed and nobody has closed it.*

*And that the whole of what came into that yard in the ten days ended on the said day is nothing, and the whole of what went out of it is nothing, and that two hundred and four pence, being one mark and three shillings and eightpence, stayed in the bag from the fourteenth to the twenty-fourth, and that a boat with a man in her for a day is one hundred and sixty-eight pence, being one mark and eightpence, so that bag is a day and a piece of a day, and that the figure that bag is short of the wage of four marks, being six hundred and forty pence, is four hundred and thirty-six pence, being two marks and nine shillings and eightpence, and it is not going to be paid; and that nothing has been in a tin in that shed since the twenty-eighth day of April past; and that the wage of four marks was not paid on the one wage Tuesday of the ten days, so that the column stands at twenty-six lines and one hundred and four marks with twenty envelopes on a bench in a chest and thirty-nine weeks fallen due with no envelope against any of them.*

*And that on Thursday the fifteenth day of July past a paper of two leaves came down that coast in the coal asking two people to come to a room in Veyr, and that both went up on an ordinary boat and came into a room off a street behind the fish market there with seven lines under glass, a man of about fifty keeping it with no title and a clerk of about sixty in a room off it writing the hour and asking nothing; and that on Saturday the seventeenth the seven lines were read out in about six minutes and the fifth requires a measuring of that harbor's own mole where it stands in eleven feet to be ordered, the ordering being the whole of what the room does and the work not done and nobody wanting it done and no person in any water and none in any stone; and that on Sunday the eighteenth a municipal paper naming two boats and three men was put on the table beside eight lines and the room wrote that a civilian convoy under such papers is not a vessel returning from beyond the line so the sixth line's door is open to it and it binds nobody who does not use it; and that on Tuesday the twentieth the holder said out loud what a refusal costs, being four things, and a woman of no office said she has no figure and will not invent one; and that at about four on the said day the holder said out loud in that shed that the municipal paper of the eighteenth names two boats and three men and a tide and that nobody has asked the three men and nobody has written a day on it, and that there is a door in a room four days up that coast and there is nobody at it, and that whoever left that paper on that table was not in the room and gave no name and the man who keeps it will not be asked for one there, and that the woman of no office said nothing to it and the ship's hand wrote it in the second column with the reason against the line that it was said in a shed and not in a room; and that on Wednesday the twenty-first on the water the holder apologised in his own voice in front of her once and she did not forgive and did not thank and asked for a procedure of a leaf in writing a day before costing a day and a leaf; and that the sheet of thirteen lines was not recopied and none of its five places has a fresh copy on it because of anything that happened on the said ten days.*

*Second column, not checkable from a mole, entered on the said Saturday at about half past nine in the evening in this shed, and no name is against it: that a room in Veyr is a room and not a court and convicts nobody; and that a tender of about ninety tons lay at that harbor's outer water from the tenth day of July to the twenty-fourth and did not come inside the line and did not hail and no officer came ashore and no paper came ashore and nobody in that harbor went aboard her or spoke to anybody in her; and that a third channel found on an adrift buoy off that foul with a borrowed instrument on the twelfth was not measured and the instrument has gone back up that coast; and that on Wednesday the twenty-first the holder said he is sorry he did not ask her and she said that was the answer to the question she had not asked, and he did not say he was protecting her and did not hold any hiding as an excuse, and nothing is to be put under that; and that about eleven people of that harbor cannot read the notice, being a count of people and not a figure arrived at by two people counting separately and adding, that nobody in that yard is to tell any of them anything, that nobody was sent and nothing was written, and that the man of about thirty-two two days down that coast does not know there is a room and is not going to be told by anybody in that yard; and that nine communities hold the records one in each, and that the said communities have held them six centuries, and that the office that answers them is three hundred years old, and the office's three hundred years was not handed to the communities; and that the whole standing inventory of that harbor and that yard as it stands on the said night is: three marks to a man of Ashtide since the eighth of August, unsent for; seven pence to that yard's own shipwright; eight marks and a day of a boat to that harbor's own bellwright since the twenty-third of May, unargued, chest shut, not asked in seventy days, no digit printed; eight shillings and tenpence to a woman with her name on the figure; forty marks first after food to a person of no office, neither reduced nor paid; forty marks whole to a boy of twenty-two, the three nots standing, third column unfilled and unappointed; a page of six names on a mole with a boat as a price and no figure; a municipal claim on one of the three things in the circuit custody room that cannot be taken back without a request nobody in that harbor is going to make; a barrel of lamp oil at four hundred and fifty pence, being thirty-seven shillings and sixpence, quoted the first of June, with February's three hundred and seventy-eight pence, being thirty-one shillings and sixpence, above it and both dates on the board; physic at fourteen pence with none in this harbor; the mole-head light not lit with its lens out since the seventh of May; the chandler's note at thirty-one with its three counts at twenty-two, thirteen and eleven, unadvanced; the delayed note at eleven logged occasions and two of them with no call in them, unmeasured, the call not named; the custody room with three items and three numbers, no digit quoted, none returned, none asked for, a woman of about seventy and a man of about forty on a bench in a corridor with a municipal claim and the man cannot read; the second seam fouled about a foot and seven inches where it was on the nineteenth of May, use closed at four figures with no fifth, ladder in another man's book; the two objects of nobody's four hundred and eight days old; the bench bare for the hundred and seventy-fourth night; the far bench occupied ten until four with one shoe not his since the third of May; the fourth column's ninety-fifth line last in the book; and a harbor poorer on the twenty-fourth of July than on the fourteenth of May.*

*And in the fourth column of the leaf ruled on the twenty-seventh day of March past, that night, in the ship's left hand, the ninety-fifth line:*

*What it is not: — that two people going to a room in a city and coming back with a foot and not a victory is not an organisation and is not the beginning of one, and that eleven leaves in six hands staying where they are is not a position and is not a front and there is nobody standing behind the four sentences that were said in that room and nobody stood behind them but the people who said them; and that a boat of about ninety tons at the outer water for fifteen days is not a blockade and is not the lifting of one, and the notice is on a wall in a different street on the sixty-eighth day of it and has not been touched, and the sixth line has been the reason this harbor is open since the seventeenth of May past and that line was written by an office that did not write it for this; and that a man saying he is sorry once on a boat is not forgiven and is not thanked and is not improved on by anybody, and a procedure costing a day and a leaf is not a pardon and does not reduce forty marks by one penny, and the harbor is poorer on the twenty-fourth day of July than it was on the fourteenth of May and the cheapest thing in it went up six shillings on the first of June and has not come back down, and nobody in this country won anything this month.*
