# Chapter 356

*Saturday the Tenth of July, the Twenty-Sixth Day of the Ninety, a Sheet of Thirteen Lines with Three Rules at the Head of It and a Name and an Hour on All Thirteen and a Price and a Payer on Eleven, Two of the Thirteen Carrying a Place and No Price and No Payer and the Reason for the Two Blanks Printed at the Head of the Sheet in the Hand of the Woman Who Wrote It, the Whole of It Going up That Coast in the Coal of an Ordinary Boat at a Price of Nothing, and a Small Steam Tender of About Ninety Tons Standing at the Outer Water at Four in the Afternoon With a Young Man of About Twenty-Three in His Own Wheelhouse and a Line Out and Nothing Else*

Saturday the tenth of July, in that shed, and on that mole, and at the outer water.

**The said Saturday was not a wage Tuesday, was not a rota day and was not a wipe, and the wall behind the reeve's bench came down and went up whole. Nothing came into this yard on the said day and nothing went out of it, and the bag holds seventy-six pence, being six shillings and fourpence, and the figure that bag is short of the wage of four marks is five hundred and sixty-four pence, being three marks and seven shillings, and it is not going to be paid and nobody is to write one word under it saying anybody is going to. And it is a working harbor and the price of a barrel of lamp oil is four hundred and fifty pence, being thirty-seven shillings and sixpence, and the mole-head light is not lit and its lens has been out since the seventh day of May past, and a coal boat that goes up that coast twice a month was paid nothing to carry a sheet of paper up it and was asked for nothing and said yes before it was asked twice.**

**And the far bench was occupied on the said Saturday from about ten in the morning until about four in the afternoon and was not approached on it, and the holder of that writ did not go over to him, and nothing was put on that bench and read as a gift, and neither brother has said why. Mara Venn was in that harbor and going nowhere, of no office, not on the crew, not restored, not forgiven, forty marks first after food, neither reduced nor paid. Tobias Wren was on the crown of that mole from ten until four, being sixty-one, with eleven pieces of a boat behind him and a boat's hook in his right hand, and he was not sent for and not asked and not thanked, and he is not a teacher and not a door and not a sacrifice, and he is not the man on the far bench, and neither brother went near the other at any hour of the said day.**

---

The sheet is one leaf and it is the back of a reeve's office docket, and the hand on it is the woman of no office's, and it took her about an hour and a half, and there are three rules at the head of it and they are the same three rules that are at the head of the sheet of forty lines in the drawer in the other room, and she copied them out and she did not improve on them and she did not say whose they were.

> **A decision goes on at the moment a man says it.**
> **A man may take it back and both go on.**
> **It stops when somebody says it stops and not one line after.**

And then, under the third rule, before the first line, in the same hand and about half an inch lower, a fourth thing that is not a rule and is a reason, and it is the reason for the two blanks and it is printed on the face of the sheet so that anybody with a pencil can see why the sheet is short two:

> **The price against a line is what the man said he was paying. The payer against a line is the man himself. Where nobody was asked there is a place and no price and no payer and the place is the price of not asking.**

And then the thirteen lines.

Line one is his own answer, in his own hand, at the top, with his name against it and the hour of it, and the price against it is the word *nothing*, and the payer against it is himself. It is four sentences long and it is the whole of it and nobody in that shed improved on it and he was not thanked for writing it down.

> **The four things on that paper are not mine. Two of them are in this shed and one of them is in this shed and I keep them and I do not own them. One is in a room off the market, one is on a boat, one is in a coal locker in a town four days up that coast, and one is in a man's lockers in this town, and the fifth leaf is mine and it is in a drawer and it is not for sale. I am not signing the last paragraph. I have told a woman of sixty-one what that paper wants and I did not ask her for her name and I am not going to, and one of about eleven people in this harbor cannot read and there is nobody in this yard who is allowed to read it to her. If a thing needs eleven men to say yes and one of them cannot be asked, then it does not have eleven men. It has ten and a hole. I am the man who wrote the ten and the hole. Whoever sent that paper can come and count them himself and I will not send it in for him.**

Lines two to ten are the nine refusals of the Friday morning, in the woman's hand, with the name of the man and the hour of the market, and the price against all nine is the word *nothing* and the payer against all nine is the name of the man who gave it. Line eleven is the answer of the boat's man of about forty-one, and the price against it is the word *nothing* and the payer is him, and against his line, in brackets, in the woman's hand, is the note that it is not a refusal and is not a yes and was not counted as either, and that is her note and not his.

Line twelve has a place on it and no price and no payer. The place is: *a market wall in a town four days up that coast, where a page of forty lines has been since the previous month, and the name of whoever put it there, which nobody in this yard knows and did not ask.*

Line thirteen has a place on it and no price and no payer. The place is: *a table in a corridor of the office of the Morrow circuit, where two of the three things in that building are, and where one of the two cannot read, and who was not asked, and no request was made, and nobody has been sent.*

**THIRTEEN LINES. A NAME AND AN HOUR ON ALL THIRTEEN. A PRICE AND A PAYER ON ELEVEN, and the eleven are the word *nothing* against eleven names, and the two that carry neither are the two places where nobody was asked, and the reason is printed at the head of the sheet in the hand of the woman who wrote it and is not a rule and is not improved on.**

The sheet went up that coast in the coal of an ordinary boat at about two in the afternoon, and it went in the middle of a load of coal, and it was not rolled and it was not wrapped and it was folded once and put in the inside pocket of the boat's own boy, who is about seventeen and who was not paid and who took it himself and did not know what it was and did not ask.

**THIS YARD WROTE TO NONE OF THE FIVE TOWNS. It did not write to the town with the room off the market and it did not write to the town with the boat and it did not write to the town with the coal locker and it did not write to the town with the market wall. It charged nobody for the writing of the sheet, it charged nobody for the docket it was written on, it charged nobody for the reading of the instrument on the Wednesday, and it charged nobody for the man of about thirty-four who came off a lighter with a wet leather pocket on the Monday. There is no figure against any of that and there is not going to be one.**

---

At about four o'clock in the afternoon the holder of that writ was at the top of that mole and looked out and there was a small steam tender of about ninety tons lying at the outer water, inside nothing, outside the line, with a line out and a second line out.

She had been lying there since about half past two, and nobody in that harbor had seen her come in past the head, and the girl of about nineteen at the steps of the pilot's house said she had come round the point in thick weather off a coal boat and had not come in past the line at all and had not hailed anybody and had put two lines out on a wreck and had said nothing to anybody.

**IN HER OWN WHEELHOUSE THERE WAS A YOUNG MAN OF ABOUT TWENTY-THREE WITH A TELESCOPE IN HIS HAND AND HE CAME OUT ONTO THE WHEELHOUSE ROOF AND LOOKED AT THAT HARBOR FOR ABOUT FOUR MINUTES AND HE CAME BACK IN AGAIN, AND HE IS TWENTY-THREE AND HE IS NOT AN OFFICER OF ANYTHING AND NOBODY HAS ASKED HIM ONE QUESTION AND HE HAS NOT SAID ONE WORD TO ANYBODY IN THAT HARBOR.**

And along the quay there was a small vessel of perhaps eighteen tons with a man of nineteen at her wheel who was levering a boat's hook at a bight of line round a bollard and getting it round, and getting it round twice, and he was not in a hurry, and he is nineteen, and nobody in that harbor had spoken to him, and **HE IS THE ONLY PERSON THAT DAY WHOSE WHOLE WORK COULD BE DESCRIBED AS A DUTY, and he was not asked what his duty was and he did not say, and the only thing anybody in that harbor entered against him is that he was patient with a bight of line and did not cut it.**

Then the girl of about nineteen at the steps said the last thing that happened on that water, and she said it to nobody in particular and about six people heard it.

"**She has had two steamers alongside her at Ternwatch for four days and this one is the size of a fish box and she has come ninety miles on a tide.**"

Nobody in that harbor said anything about that. The notice of the seventeenth day of May past is still on its wall in a different street and has not been withdrawn, and the fifth clause of it is a breach and the sixth clause of it is free, and that tender is outside the line and has come to no attention of that harbor and has declared nothing, and the first naval act on that coast since the notice went up is a boat of about ninety tons at the outer water with a line out.

**AND THE SQUADRON CAME NO CLOSER THAT DAY, AND NO OFFICER CAME ASHORE, AND NO PAPER CAME ASHORE, AND NOBODY HAILED ANYBODY, AND THE SIXTH CLAUSE OF THE NOTICE WAS NOT READ ALOUD ON THE SAID DAY IN THAT HARBOR OR IN THAT MARKET.**

---

The woman of no office came up the hill at about six and was told about the tender by a man of about twenty-four who could not tell a tender from a lighter and said so, and she said one thing about it.

"**Nobody has declared anything and nobody has been told anything, and that is the sixth clause doing the work it has been doing since May, and it will keep doing it until a day when it cannot, and I have not got a figure for that day and I am not going to invent one.**"

And Calder Marr said that he had not thought about the sixth clause once in three days, and she said that he had not, and that was the whole of what she said about it.

*First column, entered on Saturday the tenth day of July next at about half past nine in the evening in this shed: that a sheet of one leaf was made in that shed on the said day in the hand of the woman of no office, being the back of a reeve's office docket, in about an hour and a half, and that it carries three rules at the head of it, being that a decision goes on at the moment a man says it, that a man may take it back and both go on, and that it stops when somebody says it stops and not one line after; and that the said rules are the rules of the sheet of forty lines in the drawer in the other room and were copied out and not improved on and it was not said in that shed whose they are; and that under the third rule and above the first line there is a fourth thing which is not a rule, being that the price against a line is what the man said he was paying and the payer against a line is the man himself and where nobody was asked there is a place and no price and no payer and the place is the price of not asking; and that the said sheet has thirteen lines, and that it carries a name and an hour on all thirteen and a price and a payer on eleven, and that the price against all eleven is the word *nothing* and the payer against all eleven is the name of the man who gave it; and that the two lines carrying neither are the twelfth, being a market wall in a town four days up that coast where a page of forty lines has been since the previous month and the name of whoever put it there, which nobody in that yard knows and did not ask, and the thirteenth, being a table in a corridor of the office of the Morrow circuit where two of the three things in that building are and where one of the two cannot read and who was not asked and no request was made and nobody has been sent; and that the said sheet went up that coast in the coal of an ordinary boat at about two in the afternoon of the said day, in the inside pocket of that boat's own boy of about seventeen, who was not paid, who took it himself and did not know what it was and did not ask; and that this yard wrote to none of the five towns, and did not write to the town with the room off the market, nor the town with the boat, nor the town with the coal locker, nor the town with the market wall, and charged nobody for the writing of the sheet or the docket it was on or the reading of the instrument or the man who carried the instrument.*

*Second column, not checkable from a mole, entered on the said Saturday at about half past nine in the evening in this shed, and no name is against it: that a small steam tender of about ninety tons lay at the outer water of that harbor from about half past two in the afternoon of the said day, outside the line, with two lines out, and that she had come round the point in thick weather off a coal boat and had not come in past the line and had not hailed anybody; and that in her own wheelhouse there was a young man of about twenty-three with a telescope in his hand who came out onto the wheelhouse roof and looked at that harbor for about four minutes and came back in again, and that he is not an officer of anything and was asked nothing and said nothing; and that along that quay there was a small vessel of perhaps eighteen tons with a man of nineteen at her wheel levering a boat's hook at a bight of line round a bollard and getting it round twice, and that he was not in a hurry, and that the only thing anybody in that harbor entered against him is that he was patient with a bight of line and did not cut it; and that a girl of about nineteen at the steps of the pilot's house said the tender has had two steamers alongside her at Ternwatch for four days and came ninety miles on a tide, and that nobody in that harbor said anything about that.*

*And in the fourth column of the leaf ruled on the twenty-seventh day of March past, that night, in the ship's left hand, the eighty-first line:*

*What it is not: — that a sheet of thirteen lines with a name and an hour on all thirteen is not a counter-move and is not an answer to the instrument and is not a document, and that a price of nothing paid by a man eleven times is not a concession and is not a figure and is not entered in the second column of this yard's own book and would not be checkable from a mole if it were; and that the two lines with nothing against them are not refusals and are not refusals by anybody, they are two places where nobody was asked, and the reason is printed at the head of the sheet above the first line so that a stranger with a pencil does not have to guess; and that a boat of about ninety tons at the outer water with two lines out is not a blockade and is not a lifting of one and is not a notice, and the notice is on a wall in a different street and has not been touched, and the sixth clause has been doing this coast's work since the seventeenth day of May past and it will go on doing it until a day it cannot.*
