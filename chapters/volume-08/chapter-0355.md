# Chapter 355

*Friday the Ninth of July, the Twenty-Fifth Day of the Ninety, a Fish Market at Eleven in the Morning in About Forty People's Hearing, Six Holders of Eleven Leaves Asked in Five Towns and One Market, Nine Refusals Given in Nine Different Words and One That Was Not a Refusal and Was Not a Yes, Three Elevens in One Week That Are Not the Same Eleven, and Nothing at All Coming into That Yard or Going Out of It*

Friday the ninth of July, in that fish market, and in that shed, and on that mole.

**The said Friday was not a wage Tuesday, was not a rota day and was not a wipe, and the wall behind the reeve's bench came down and went up whole. Nothing came into this yard on the said day and nothing went out of it, and the bag holds seventy-six pence, being six shillings and fourpence, and it is the same seventy-six pence that was in it on the Thursday, and the figure that bag is short of the wage of four marks is five hundred and sixty-four pence, being three marks and seven shillings, and it is not going to be paid and nobody is to write one word under it saying anybody is going to.**

**And the far bench was occupied on the said Friday from about ten in the morning until about four in the afternoon and was not approached on it, and the holder of that writ did not go over to him, and nothing was put on that bench and read as a gift, and neither brother has said why. Mara Venn was in that harbor and going nowhere, of no office, not on the crew, not restored, not forgiven, forty marks first after food, neither reduced nor paid, and she stood at the back of that market for the whole of it and said four sentences. Tobias Wren was on the crown of that mole from ten until four, being sixty-one, with eleven pieces of a boat behind him and a boat's hook in his right hand, and he was not sent for and not asked and not thanked, and he is not a teacher and not a door and not a sacrifice, and he is not the man on the far bench, and neither brother went near the other at any hour of the said day.**

---

They asked in a market because that is where that harbor says things, and they asked at the end of the end stall, and the woman who holds the end stall is of about fifty-eight and she let them use her board and she did not say why and was not asked.

**THE WHOLE OF WHAT WAS ASKED, and it is four sentences, and they were said once, at about eleven in the morning, in about forty people's hearing, and not in a room: *A paper with a seal on it has been read out in that shed. It wants four things given up. They are on the face of it. It also wants a name under a sentence about a ferry. We are not going to give the four things and we are going to say so out loud, and we are going to say who is saying it, and you can write that down if anybody wants to write it down, and if nobody wants to write it down then it is said and that is all.* And about forty people heard that and nobody asked a question about it and nobody answered it.**

Then the woman of about fifty-eight put her thumb on her own board and said the first refusal, and it is hers and it is the one that is on the page.

"**I have not got a copy. I have got a slate and a board and a wall, and the wall has a notice on it from the seventeenth of May past with two older naval notices further along it, and under my notice there is one sheet of this yard's own fourth return that has been on it since April and I have not moved it and I am not going to.**"

"**And here is the thing I want said. Four of those eleven leaves are copies of a sheet of forty lines. One of the four is in a coal locker in a town four days up that coast, and somebody up there has copied it onto a market wall, and I do not know who and they do not know who put it there. So a leaf this yard cannot get at has already been read by a person this yard has never met and put up where strangers can stand in front of it, and that happened without anybody asking anybody for anything, and it is the only thing that has happened on this coast in two months that I would not have believed, and I have not got a figure for it and I am not going to put one on my board.**"

"**I am not the Crown's enemy. I have never said I was and I am not going to start. I am a woman who sells fish and I am holding a piece of paper up for people who cannot get to the coast, and that is all I am, and that is not a reason anybody can argue with, because there is nothing in it to argue with.**"

Then the copyist, who is of about fifty-nine and has been in a room off that market for twenty-two years, said one sentence and went back to her counter.

"**Twenty-two years I have copied what has been put in front of me and I have not asked what it was for once and I am not starting now, and there is one hand of mine on one of those four copies and it is in my room and off my wall, and I am not going to say anything else about it to anybody.**"

A herring skipper of fifty-one said it in one word and then said seven more, and the seven were about a hard at Ashtide that has been unanswered since the ninth of November past and not one word about it.

A man of about thirty-eight of no standing whatever with a boat of about thirteen tons said no, and then said that he would like it on whatever sheet anybody was keeping, and the woman of about fifty-eight said there was a slate, and he said the slate would do, and his no went on the slate in her hand and nobody copied it.

The ship's hand said no in writing, on a leaf of her own, in her left hand, at about half past twelve, and handed it to the woman of no office, and did not explain it and was not asked.

And a man of about thirty-six with a coaster of about thirty tons and eleven weeks of a season is a day up that coast, and his copy is written out of his father's book in his father's house and it is in a coal locker in a town four days up this coast, and **on the said day he had not answered and his line on the sheet that went up on the Saturday was empty, and it was not a hole, and it was filled in on the Monday morning when his answer came back down that coast road in the coal, and that answer is on the page of the thirteenth: no, and one sentence, being that his father wrote the spelling and not him and that a man does not give up his father's book because a piece of paper came ashore.**

And in the town four days up that coast, where a page of forty lines has been on a market wall since the previous month and nobody in this yard knows who put it up, **nobody came back at all.** There is a coal locker in that town and there is a coal boat that goes twice a month and this yard did not write to that town, did not ask who put the page up, and did not send anybody, and the not sending is entered on the page with the reason against it, being that this yard holds one of the five places of that sheet and has never once written to the other four and is not going to start with a letter that begins *we are writing to ask whether you will*.

---

And there is one more answer, and it is the eleventh, and it is not a refusal and it is not a yes, and it came at about one o'clock in the afternoon from a man who was asked in about nine people's hearing and who took about forty minutes to answer.

He is a boat's man of about forty-one and he is of the second lay and he can read. He was asked the four sentences and he said he had four things to ask first and he asked them in the order he had them, and he asked what a figure is, and he was told, and he asked what a party subscribing is, and he was told, and he asked who the eleven are, and he was told the eleven leaves and not the people, and then he stopped for about ten minutes and then he said this:

"**I have been asked whether I will give up a thing. I have not got a thing. I have got a boat and I have got a wage that is four marks on a Tuesday and there is not one of them in the paper and I am not on the paper. If I put my name under that sentence I am not giving anything up, I am standing there while somebody else gives something up, and the only way I can do that is if I am one of the people it is about, and I do not know that I am, and I have never been asked, and nobody has told me what I would be called if I said yes.**"

And then he said that he was not saying no, and everybody in that market waited for the rest of it, and he said: **"**I am saying I am not going to be one of eleven people saying something I do not know the size of. Ask the man who made it what he means by subscribing. Ask him in a room and get it in writing. I have a boat and I cannot read a word of that leaf, and I would be signing a thing I have never seen, and a man who does that is no better than the man who cut that shackle.**"

**AND THAT WAS ENTERED AS WHAT IT WAS, WHICH IS NOT A REFUSAL AND NOT A YES AND WAS NOT COUNTED AS EITHER, AND THE FOUR THINGS HE ASKED FOR WERE FOUR THINGS AND EACH OF THEM WAS TOLD TO HIM BY A PERSON STANDING IN THAT AISLE, AND HE ASKED THEM IN THE ORDER HE HAD THEM, AND THE MAN OF ABOUT THIRTY-EIGHT STOOD AT THE END OF THE SAME AISLE FOR FORTY MINUTES AND SAID NOTHING AT ALL, AS HE SAID ON THE THIRD DAY OF JULY THAT HE WOULD, AND HE WAS NOT ASKED.**

And the woman of no office, who had been standing at the back, said her four sentences after the market had gone and not before, and none of the four was for the market.

"**You did it right. I want that said in the second column and not in the fourth.**"

"**And you did it in about nine people's hearing in six of them and about forty people's in the seventh, and the sixth of the seven is a stall two in from the end where a woman of sixty-one cannot read, and she is one of them, and you did not ask her, and the seventh answer on that slate is from a man who has a boat and a wage and no figure of his own in any of it, and neither of those two is the same as the other five and no two of the seven said the same words.**"

"**And a man of about thirty-six wrote his no at a table in his own house about his father's book, and a man four days up that coast has not answered at all and we have not written to him, and there is a hole in this and I am not going to call it full.**"

"**And the offer on that sheet is for four things and the last paragraph is for a number of people it does not say, and the number is the eleventh thing about that leaf that is not on the leaf, and the number is why this is going to take a month and not a day.**"

*First column, entered on Friday the ninth day of July next at about half past nine in the evening in this shed: that in the fish market of that harbor at about eleven in the morning of the said day, at the end of the end stall, in about forty people's hearing and not in a room, four sentences were said once, being that a paper with a seal on it had been read out in that shed, that it wants four things given up and they are on the face of it, that it also wants a name under a sentence about a ferry, and that the four things will not be given up and the men saying so will say so out loud with their names against it; and that nine refusals were given in nine persons' own words in five towns and one market, being the woman of about fifty-eight with the end stall, a copyist of about fifty-nine in a room off that market, a herring skipper of fifty-one, a man of about thirty-eight of no standing whatever with a boat of about thirteen tons, the ship's hand in her own left hand on a leaf of her own, a man of about thirty-six with a coaster of about thirty tons, whose line was empty on the said day and was filled in on the Monday, and two in a market on this coast; and that one answer was neither a refusal nor a yes, being from a boat's man of about forty-one of the second lay, and that it is entered as what it was and is not counted as either; and that in a town four days up that coast, where a page of forty lines has been on a market wall since the previous month, no answer came back at all, and that this yard did not write to that town, did not ask who put the page up and did not send anybody.*

*Second column, not checkable from a mole, entered on the said Friday at about half past nine in the evening in this shed, and no name is against it: that the owners of the eleven leaves are about six people in five towns and not eleven people, being the woman of no office with six, the ship's hand with one, and four others with one each, one of whom is in a room off that market, one of whom is a herring skipper, one of whom is a day up that coast with his father's book in his house, and one of whom is in a town four days up that coast and is not known to anybody in this yard; and that the last paragraph of the said instrument wants the subscribing parties described and does not describe them, and that the number of subscribing parties is the eleventh thing about that leaf which is not on the leaf; and that the ship's hand's price of eleven conversations was her own reading and is entered as her reading and not as a fact of the instrument; and that three elevens have stood in this harbor in one week, being eleven leaves, about eleven people who cannot read the notice, and eleven conversations, and that they are three different numbers, and that nobody in that yard has confused them and that the yard has entered all three separately and separately is the right way round.*

*And in the fourth column of the leaf ruled on the twenty-seventh day of March past, that night, in the ship's left hand, the eightieth line:*

*What it is not: — that nine refusals in nine different words is not a document and is not a sheet and is not on any wall, and that the four sentences said in that market are not a speech and were not written down before they were said and were said once; that the woman of about fifty-eight holding a piece of paper up for people who cannot get to the coast is not a witness and is not a symbol and is not a constituency and would not accept the word, and the slate is her own and the rule about figures is her own and neither was given to anybody; that the answer of the boat's man of about forty-one is not a refusal and is not a yes and is not the eleventh refusal, and a man who will not sign a thing he has not read is not a coward and is not a lawyer and is not on anybody's side; and that a man of about thirty-six who wrote his no at a table in his own house about his father's book has not given anything up that is anybody's, and the man four days up that coast has not answered and there is a hole in this and it is not full and nobody here is going to call it full.*
