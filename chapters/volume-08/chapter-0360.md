# Chapter 360

*Wednesday the Fourteenth of July, the Thirtieth Day of the Ninety, a Leaf Read Back in Full With the Count on the Face of It and the Definition of Every Figure on the Face of It, Three Statements About Three Things, the Whole Standing Inventory Once and Nowhere Else, and a Mole Head at Ten Past Five in the Evening With a Light Not Lit and a Boat of About Ninety Tons Still at the Outer Water*

Wednesday the fourteenth of July, in that shed at about four in the afternoon, and on that mole at about ten past five in the evening.

**The said Wednesday was not a wage Tuesday, was not a rota day and was not a wipe, and the wall behind the reeve's bench came down and went up at the angle it has been at since the twelfth. Nothing came into that yard on the said day and nothing went out of it, and there are two hundred and four pence in the bag, being one mark and three shillings and eightpence, and the figure that bag is short of the wage of four marks is four hundred and thirty-six pence, being two marks and nine shillings and eightpence, and it is not going to be paid and nobody is to write one word under it saying anybody is going to. Nothing has been in a tin in that shed since the twenty-eighth day of April past, and a boat with a man in her for a day is one hundred and sixty-eight pence, so that bag is a day and a piece of a day, and it is the most money that has been in that bag since the eighteenth day of May past, and this is the first sentence in three months on that subject and it is not going to be improved on.**

**And the far bench was occupied on the said Wednesday from about ten in the morning until about four in the afternoon and was not approached on it, and the holder of that writ did not go over to him, and nothing was put on that bench and read as a gift, and neither brother has said why. Mara Venn was in that harbor and going nowhere, of no office, not on the crew, not restored, not forgiven, forty marks first after food, neither reduced nor paid, and she came up that hill for the reading and for nothing else, and it is the tenth day of that month she has come up it. Tobias Wren was on the crown of that mole from ten until four and was on it at about ten past five with a boat's hook in his right hand and eleven pieces of a boat behind him, and he was not sent for and not asked and not thanked, and he is not a teacher and not a door and not a sacrifice, and he is not Alden Marr, and neither brother went near the other at any hour of the said day.**

**And this yard's own count of the three standing figures, made in that shed on the said day and not taken out of any other book: in these ten days the wall came down bare twice, being the sixty-first on Monday the fifth of July and the sixty-second on Monday the twelfth, and the next is the sixty-third on Monday the nineteenth, which is outside these ten; the cistern was charged three times and walked on none of them, being the sixty-first on Monday the fifth, the sixty-second on Thursday the eighth and the sixty-third on Monday the twelfth, the rota being a Monday and a Thursday; and two wage Tuesdays fell, being Tuesday the sixth for the thirty-seventh week and Tuesday the thirteenth for the thirty-eighth, and the column did not move on either, so that thirty-eight weeks stand fallen due with no envelope against any of them.**

---

They read the leaf back at about four in the afternoon, which is the hour that shed does things, and there were three of them in it and the woman of no office was on the bench.

**The first column of that leaf has ninety-two entries in it at the hour of the read-back and ninety-three at the close.** There were eighty-three of them at the close of Sunday the fourth day of July past and ten have gone in since, one for every day from Monday the fifth to Wednesday the fourteenth, and not one day in two of them, and this evening's own entry is the tenth and makes ninety-three.

**The second column has three hundred and thirty-seven lines in it at the hour of the read-back and three hundred and thirty-nine at the close.** Two hundred and eighty-nine of them were in it at the close of the fourth day of July past. Three of the day's entries went in before the reading of that day and two after it, and that is the shape of that shed's habit and is not a rule, and no reader in any office can check it, and that is what the second column is for. Ten days of that shape is fifty, and two hundred and eighty-nine and fifty is three hundred and thirty-nine, and three of this Wednesday's went in before the reading and make three hundred and thirty-seven at the hour of it. **The said figure is that book of that yard's own and is not a count of anything anybody can check from a mole. The first column's ninety-two and the fourth column's eighty-four, at the hour of this read-back, are counts of entries and of lines, and a day of the first column carries one entry against it whatever else that day printed, so that a stranger with a leaf and a pencil can count both of them. Those are the two definitions and they are printed here so that a later writer with a pencil knows which of them they are counting and at which of the two moments.**

**The third column is empty and nobody has been appointed to it, and the hand of a boy of twenty-two is at the head of it with a name beside it that is not that boy's and is not the holder's, and that name was said out loud once in a boat on the thirteenth day of May past and has not been said since and is not going to be said again in this harbor. The four lines above his named line are four, nameless, and are not struck out, and they are in about sixty boats on that coast. He was not on the stone on any of these ten days and was put on nothing, and nobody asked him one word about the three nots standing against the eighth of June, and they were not asked about on that day or after it.**

**And the fourth column has eighty-four lines in it at the hour of the read-back and eighty-five at the close, and the eighty-fifth is the last thing in that book, and the column is not closed and nobody has closed it and nobody in this yard is going to.**

Then the three of them said what the ten days were, in about three lines each, and none of it was argued with and none of it has been improved on since.

**The first was about the water, and the ship's hand said it.**

"**On Wednesday the seventh day of July past a man of about thirty-four of a pressure house at Ternwatch came ashore off a lighter at ten in the morning with a sealed instrument in a wet leather pocket, and it wanted four things and a sentence, and there are eleven leaves in it in six hands in four places and one of the eleven is a correction and one date and nothing else, and the fourth thing it wanted was not a thing. On Friday the ninth we asked in a market at the end of an end stall in about forty people's hearing and nine said no in nine words of their own and one did not say yes and did not say no and it is entered as what it was and not counted as either, and one man of about thirty-six a day up that coast had not answered that day and answered on the Monday in the coal, and in a town four days up that coast nobody answered at all and we did not write to that town and did not ask who put the page up on the wall. On Saturday the tenth a small tender of about ninety tons lay at the outer water with two lines out from half past two until dark with a young man of about twenty-three in her own wheelhouse with a telescope, and she came no closer and no officer came ashore and no paper came ashore and nobody hailed anybody. That is the water.**"

**The second was about the harbor, and the woman of no office said it from the bench, and she was not asked for it.**

"**Four things came into this harbor in ten days. A paper with a seal on it and two seals on it and one name on it, carried by a man who did not want to carry it. Eleven refusals, nine of them out loud and two of them down a coast road, on a sheet with a name and an hour on all thirteen of its lines and a price and a payer on eleven of them, and the price on all eleven is the word nothing. And one page on the crown of that mole with six names on it in a hand nobody corrected. And a boat of about ninety tons at the outer water for six days.**

"**And nothing came off any board. The cheapest thing in this town went up six shillings on the first day of June and has not come back down, and about eleven tons came in on ten boats at nobody's price in the last month of June and that did not bring it down either, and the mole-head light is out and its lens has been out since the seventh of May, and the fire party's hose is dead and has been dead since before the spring, and a page on the mole says a boat and says no figure, and a man of about fifty-one of the six had a day's wage on Monday the twelfth and forty pence of it went out in a shackle and a fathom of line and the other five of the six had nothing and were not told there was anything. And a man of about forty-four lost the top of two fingers of his left hand off a buoy's shackle off the foul at half past two on the Monday and paid five shillings and sixpence for it out of his own hand and is off the water nine days. And a bonesetter came on the Saturday and would not touch it. And a man of about twenty-six read a notice out to his wife in a kitchen on a Sunday and she asked him what a breach was and he said he did not know.**"

**The third was about this yard, and the holder of that writ said it, and he did not look at anybody while he said it.**

"**Two hundred and four pence came into that bag in ten days on two days' work, being three hundred and thirty-six pence in and a hundred and thirty-two pence out on new chain and on a shackle and a fathom of line, and that is a mark and three shillings and eightpence and it is about a day and a piece of a day of one boat with one man in her and it is the most money that has been in that bag since the eighteenth of May past. Nine refusals and one answer that was not a refusal were got out of a market at the end of an end stall and one answer out of a coal boat four days away, and not one of the eleven leaves came out of that shed, and the two blanks on the sheet of thirteen lines are two places where nobody was asked, and the reason is printed at the head of the sheet so that anybody with a pencil can see it. And on Sunday the eleventh I said out loud in that shed that if it came to it I would hand five leaves over and the woman of no office told me that a hand is not a thing and that I have not asked her one question in four days and that she has been waiting to be asked since the first of May, and I did not ask her, and that is on the page, and I am not going to put anything under it.**

"**And there is a boat at the outer water and I have no idea what it is for and I have not sent anybody and I will not, and there is a third channel on a borrowed instrument that nobody has measured and will not be measured by anybody in this harbor, and a page of forty lines in a town four days up that coast with a man's name against every line of it and nobody knows who copied it onto that wall. And I would like the two numbers on that leaf read out at the end of this month by somebody who was not me, and I would like the third one read out as well even though it has not moved.**"

And then at about ten past five in the evening he went up to the mole head, because that is where he goes, and there is nothing up there and nobody sent him.

The mole-head light is not lit. Its lens has been out since the seventh day of May past and it is out on the fourteenth of July as it was on the fourteenth of May, and a light costs oil, and the last figure anybody in this harbor has for a barrel of lamp oil is four hundred and fifty pence, being thirty-seven shillings and sixpence, quoted to a woman on the first day of June, which was three hundred and seventy-eight pence in February, being thirty-one shillings and sixpence, and on the February figure that light was out in October, and the notice of the seventeenth day of May past has been on a wall in a different street for fifty-eight days and has not been taken down and has not been compared with anything by anybody in that harbor.

The small steam tender of about ninety tons was still at the outer water at about ten past five, with two lines out and a lamp at her masthead, and nobody in that harbor had been aboard her and nobody had spoken to anybody in her, and the young man of about twenty-three was in her own wheelhouse and had come out onto the roof twice and had not hailed.

On the crown of that mole, about nine feet apart under two tarpaulins and about four feet from a page with six names on it in a hand nobody corrected, the two objects of nobody's are three hundred and ninety-eight days old, and one of them has had a corner off it since the seventeenth of June past and it has not been lashed back, and neither has moved, and nobody has asked whose either is.

Tobias Wren was on that crown at about ten past five with a boat's hook in his right hand and eleven pieces of a boat behind him, and he did not look up at the mole head and nobody went over to him. A man of sixty-one was on that crown from ten until four on every one of these ten days. **He is not that man.**

Across on the far bench a man of sixty-one was not there, because it was after four, and had been there from ten until four on every one of these ten days, and there was one shoe on that bench beside him and the shoe is not his, and it has been on that bench since the third of May past and nobody has moved it and nobody has asked.

The mark on the face of the second seam is fouled about a foot and seven inches on the weather side and nothing on the other, where it was on the nineteenth of May past, and it was not looked at on any of the ten days, and the mark four hundred yards off it is of a make nobody in this harbor makes, and the season of both is nine marks and neither has been paid and neither is going to be, and the use of the first was taken once on the eighteenth day of June past and closed, four figures and no fifth at any hour of any day, ladder in another man's book, nobody to explain it, not Fisk.

**AND THERE IS NOTHING ON THE PAGE OF THAT CROWN AND NOTHING ON THE LEDGER OF THAT SHED AND NOTHING IN THE MOUTH OF ANY PERSON IN THAT HARBOR THAT ANSWERS THE QUESTION THIS YARD HAS BEEN CARRYING SINCE THE THIRTY-FIRST DAY OF MAY PAST, AND IT WAS NOT ASKED AGAIN ON ANY OF THESE TEN DAYS AND IT IS NOT ASKED AT THE MOLE HEAD TONIGHT AND NOBODY IN THIS COUNTRY IS GOING TO BE ASKED IT BY THIS YARD, and eleven people said no to four prices and a sentence in ten days and what came back was eleven leaves in six hands in four places, and the word for a thing that would hold all four of them was not said in that market by anybody on any of these ten days, and this yard has not got one and is not going to say what it would be called if it had, and a kind is not a thing anybody here has got.**

*First column, entered on Wednesday the fourteenth day of July next at about half past nine in the evening in this shed: that the said leaf ruled on the twenty-seventh day of March past was read back in full at about four in the afternoon of the said day by the ship's hand in her own left hand, all four columns including the empty ones, in the presence of the holder of that writ and of a woman of no office, and that the two definitions of every figure in it were stated on the face of that reading, being that the second column's figure is that ledger's own and is not a count of paragraphs, and that the first column's figure and the fourth column's figure at the hour of the reading are counts of entries and of lines and are checkable by a stranger with a pencil; and that the said leaf holds ninety-three entries in its first column at the close of the said day, being eighty-three on the fourth day of July past and one entry against each of the ten days since, with no day in two of them, and ninety-two of them at the hour of the reading; three hundred and thirty-nine lines in its second; none in its third; and eighty-five in its fourth, of which the eighty-fifth is the last thing in that book, and the column is not closed and nobody has closed it.*

*And that the whole of what came into that yard in the ten days ended on the said day is three hundred and thirty-six pence, being two marks and one shilling and fourpence, on two days' work at one hundred and sixty-eight pence a day, and that the whole of what went out of it is a hundred and thirty-two pence, being eleven shillings, on nine feet twelve inches of new stud chain at ninety-two pence and on a shackle and a fathom of line at forty pence, and that three hundred and thirty-six and a hundred and thirty-two is two hundred and four pence, being one mark and three shillings and eightpence, and that a boat with a man in her for a day is one hundred and sixty-eight pence, so that bag is a day and a piece of a day, and that the figure that bag is short of the wage of four marks is four hundred and thirty-six pence, being two marks and nine shillings and eightpence, and it is not going to be paid; and that nothing has been in a tin in that shed since the twenty-eighth day of April past; and that the wage of four marks, being six hundred and forty pence, was not paid on either wage Tuesday of the ten days, so that the column stands at twenty-six lines and one hundred and four marks with twenty envelopes on a bench in a chest and thirty-eight weeks fallen due with no envelope against any of them.*

*And that on Wednesday the seventh day of July past a sealed instrument was put on the bench in that shed with a bulkhead brace against one corner of it and was read out in full in about four minutes, and that it is a single leaf with two seals and one signature and no schedule and no enclosure and nothing on the back of it, and that it wants four things, being five leaves of a public provenance record, the sheet of forty lines itself, four copies of that sheet, and a leaf carrying one correction and one date, being eleven leaves in six hands; and that its last paragraph wants a name under a sentence and does not say how many subscribing parties it means, and that the fourth thing it wanted was not a thing; and that on Friday the ninth eleven people were asked in five towns and one market and nine said no in nine words of their own, and one said neither yes nor no and is entered as what it was, and the man of about thirty-six a day up the coast said no in the coal on the Monday, and in the town four days up that coast nobody answered at all and this yard wrote to none of the five towns; and that the sheet of thirteen lines went up that coast in the coal of an ordinary boat on the Saturday at about two in the afternoon in the inside pocket of that boat's own boy of about seventeen, who was not paid and did not know what it was, and that it carries a name and an hour on all thirteen and a price and a payer on eleven and a place and neither on two, and that the reason for the two is printed at the head of it above the first line.*

*Second column, not checkable from a mole, entered on the said Wednesday at about half past nine in the evening in this shed, and no name is against it: that a page with six names on it has been on the crown of that mole since the eighth of July and was not altered and carries a boat as a price and no figure, and that the said page is on the harbor's stone and not on this yard's wall and that the holder of that writ said out loud that it is his own mistake and he is not going to move it; and that a tender of about ninety tons lay at that harbor's outer water from the tenth day of July and did not come inside the line and did not hail and no officer came ashore and no paper came ashore and nobody in that harbor went aboard her or spoke to anybody in her; and that a third channel was found on an adrift buoy off that foul with a borrowed instrument on the twelfth, that nothing was measured, that no instrument was left in that water, that the instrument went back up that coast on the Tuesday, and that the man it is borrowed from has not been asked about it and is not going to be; and that on Sunday the eleventh the holder of that writ said out loud that if it came to it he would hand five leaves over, and that the woman of no office told him that a hand is not a thing and cannot be handed over and that he has not asked her one question in four days and that she has been waiting to be asked since the first of May, and that he did not ask her, and that nothing is to be put under that; and that about eleven people of that harbor cannot read the notice, being a count of people and not a figure arrived at by two people counting separately and adding, that nobody in that yard is to tell any of them anything, that nobody was sent and nothing was written, and that the man of about thirty-two two days down that coast does not know there is an instrument and is not going to be told by anybody in that yard; and that nine communities hold the records one in each, and that the said practice is of six centuries standing, and that the office that answers it is of three hundred years standing, and that the office's three hundred years was not handed to the communities; and that the chandler's note is at thirty-one, not re-written, its three counts of weeks at twenty-two, thirteen and eleven; that the delayed note stands at eleven logged occasions and two of them with no call in them and nothing was measured; that four returns stand and there is no fifth and no finding of worked glass was made in these ten days; that the plate in the mole's own foundation stone is in eleven feet, seen and not measured and not touched, its note open and carried, and the only mechanism anybody has named for reaching it is a change of politics in an office and not a better letter out of a shed; that the case of five bells is not raised; that the lead plug and the chest and the undertaking of the second of July are not touched; and that five pages are in five drawers in five towns and that this yard has not looked in four of them.*

*And that a man of about forty-four lost the top of two fingers of his left hand off the shackle of an adrift mooring buoy off the foul at about half past two in the afternoon of Monday the twelfth, and that no bonesetter was on that coast on a Monday afternoon, and that a shipwright of about sixty set it at about four o'clock with a strip off a leech and a needle in about forty minutes, and that a bonesetter of about fifty came on the Saturday and said it was not a bonesetter's work and had not done it and would not touch it, and that the bill of five shillings and sixpence, being sixty-six pence, was paid out of the man's own hand and said twice and not a third time and came out of nothing in that yard; and that he is off the water nine days and is one of about eleven people of that harbor who cannot read the notice and was not added to that count because nobody asked.*

*And in the fourth column of the leaf ruled on the twenty-seventh day of March past, that night, in the ship's left hand, the eighty-fifth line:*

*What it is not: — that eleven leaves in six hands and four places is a refusal and not a position, and that a sheet of thirteen lines with a name and an hour on all thirteen and a price and a payer on eleven of them is not a counter-move and is not a document and is not an answer to anything, and that eleven people refusing in eleven words is not a position and is not a front and there is nobody standing behind the four sentences that were said in that market and nobody stood behind them but the people who said them; and that a boat of about ninety tons at the outer water for six days is not a blockade and is not the lifting of one, and the notice is on a wall in a different street on the fifty-eighth day of it and has not been touched, and the sixth clause has been the reason this harbor is open since the seventeenth of May past and that clause was written by an office that did not write it for this; and that a man of about fifty-one of the six having one day's wage is not a resolution, and a page on a mole with six names and a boat and no figure is not a claim and is not a document and has no jurisdiction whatever, and the harbor is poorer on the fourteenth day of July than it was on the fourteenth day of May and the cheapest thing in it went up six shillings on the first of June and has not come back down, and nobody in this country won anything this month.*
