# Chapter 142

*A Compressor of His Own, and Ten Marks We Do Not Have*

Saturday the twenty-ninth of July, at about half past eight in the morning, on the crown of the mole above the third seam's mark, in the open air, with a glass out of a locker that the yard owns and has not taken out since the fourteenth of July.

**The tide gang came past at about nine, four men, and there should be eight, and the four of them have been walking this mole since the first of May and past that boat off the outer marks every day of it.**

One of them stopped. He was about thirty and he had a line-marking pot under his arm and he had not been asked anything by anybody.

"**I am going to say a thing and then I am going to walk,**" he said. "**I have been asked about that boat twice this month and both times by men of other boats and both times I said I did not know whose she was, and I did not, and I was not going to be the man who put a name to a thing because a man of a boat asked me to.

"**And this morning I am going to say it anyway, and I am going to say it to the two of you, and then I am going to walk my four men down the length and I am not going to say it again.

"**Her pump is going. I can hear it from the crown at half past eight in a flat morning, and I have heard it at half past eight for nine days, and on Tuesday the twenty-first of July she was upright and on the twenty-second she had a list to starboard of about a foot, and on Saturday the twenty-fourth the list was two foot, and this morning the list is about four foot and she is about seven fathoms and she is on ground that is not this mole and I do not know whose ground it is and I have not sounded her.

"**I have known that since the twenty-second of July and I have said nothing to nine people in this office, and there are not eight of us, and the man who writes our page is in a building on the west basin and I have not gone up to him, and that is the whole of what I have done wrong and I would like it wrote down that I did it and not that I did not notice.**"

"**Your name,**" said Nessa Pike, "**and the hour, and it goes in the first column in my hand, and any man in this harbor can come up this hill and ask you whether you said it.**"

"**Tam Rundle.**"

---

**And then they stood at the crown and looked, and the shipwright counted with her mouth.**

"**There is a thing about where she is that nobody has said out loud and I am going to say it because it is the whole of the argument, and it is checkable, and it is in a chart in an office in this harbor and in a paper on this wall.**

"**The outer marks are the two stones you can see there, one on the mole head and one on the point, and the bar of four hundred yards is a line off them and it is on the chart in the officer's own hand. The *Hearthwake* is inside that line. She is lying between the marks and the four hundred yards, and not three hundred yards inside it, and she is inside the imperial line by about three miles, and no boat of ours and no lead of ours is going to be anywhere near the imperial line this season or any season while this yard has what it has.

"**So there are three lines and she is past one of them and inside two. And every one of those three lines is a line about paper, and not one of them is a line about water, and I have been up here since seven in the morning on nine days this month watching this yard say out loud that it is not going past the outer marks and I have not once heard anybody say what a paper does when you go past it.**"

"**It does nothing at all,**" said Calder Marr. "**That is the answer and it took me four days to get to it and I would like it on the page. It does nothing at all. The writ is bounded by the outer marks. The clause is on the writ. So there is no writ out there and no clause out there and no instrument of this harbor authority out there and no crown out there and no office out there, and if we go, we go on our own account, as a man goes off a mole, and there is nobody up there who can say we were not allowed to, and nobody up there who can say we were.

"**And the second half, and this is the one I have not been able to put down all week: if there is no instrument out there, then there is no return out there either, and there is no clause to report anything to, and no keeper, and no place, and no hour, and no limits, and no copy into anybody's ledger. We would be doing a day's work past the outer marks with a party's own hand and nothing else at all, and if it went wrong there would be no form in it and nobody to hand it to.

"**I have been sitting in that shed since Friday and I have been trying to make that sentence into a reason not to go, and it is not one, and I would like everybody to know that I tried.**"

---

**And on Sunday the thirtieth of July, at about two in the afternoon, Perry Ollett came up the hill with a slate under his arm, and he came up it in daylight on a Sunday, and nobody asked him to.**

"**I have read the notice,**" he said, "**and I have read the one underneath it, which is mine, and I put mine up on Monday the twenty-seventh in front of about thirty people and I did not know the navy was going to do that four days later and I am not going to stand here and pretend I did.

"**And I am going to say a figure before you ask me for one, and I am going to say it in the doorway, and then I am going to leave it there and you can do what you like with it.**

"**Six marks. The *Sallow Reach*, the day, and two of my men, and my compressor goes in my own boat and fills your pots at my compressor and not at that office's, and I do not charge you for the air because the air is mine and it is in her already and the cost of it is a fire I have stoked. Six marks and that is the whole of it and I have not put a victuals on it and if you want victuals you can buy them.

"**And here is the other half and you are going to want it in your book. I have a writ to go and see about the road and a compressor and forty fathom of my own hose. I have not got a Crown paper with four days in it and a name on it and I have not got a market wall. I have got gear. That is all I have ever had and it has been enough to be a nuisance to you for a summer.

"**And I am not doing this because I am a good man, and if anybody in this harbor says that Perry Ollett went to a boat off the outer marks for nothing, he has got the figure wrong and the figure is six, and I will say the figure myself.**"

Nessa Pike wrote it in the first column in her own hand and read it back, and the reading-back is the only thing this yard has ever given anybody and it is the only thing it has left to give.

"**Then what happens when the plan fails,**" she said, "**which is the only question I ask about a plan, and I am asking it of you and not of him: what do you do if there is air in her and the four of them are on her and you cannot get to her?**"

"**Then I stand off and I keep pumping her with my own pump over the side from my own boat for as long as my own boat has fuel, and I do that for nothing, and I have never told anybody that and I am telling you now because you have written my figure down and a man who has had his figure written down is entitled to put the rest of it on the page.**"

---

**And then the shipwright put the slate down on the bench and asked the question she had been carrying down the hill, and she asked it in the shed and not on the mole, because the mole is where this yard says things out loud and the shed is where it says them to itself.**

"**I am going to ask you the thing I have asked you every Friday since the first of May and I am going to ask it once and I am not going to ask it again this month, and I would like you to notice that I have said the date of the last one out loud, because you notice dates and I have noticed that you notice dates.

"**What happens when the plan fails. That is the whole of what I ask and I have never asked you for anything else. And on Tuesday morning of next week we are going to take two pots of air off that office's compressor at that office's own rate and we are going to put ten marks on a board and we are going to be past the outer marks, and I want the two of us to say now, out loud, in this shed, what happens if it fails. Not what we do. What fails.

"**And the answers I have are these and I have had them since the Saturday and I have not said them because you have been at a mole in a westerly for nine days.

"**One. It fails if the air is not there when we get there, and there is no air in this harbor and two pots is two pots, and a man of fifty-one in this harbor is better on a lead than anybody in this yard and is not on a lead of ours and is not to be asked.

"**Two. It fails if the hatch is not under four feet of water at the top of the flood, and nobody in this harbor has sounded that boat, and a man of the tide gang who has heard her pump for nine days has not been in her and is not going to be asked to be.

"**Three. It fails if the four hundred yards is not where the chart says it is, and the chart went out of this office's hands on the twenty-second of June and went into a vessel's keeping, and this yard cannot produce it and is not going to say so twice.

"**Four. It fails if the sentence of the fifth of July is what it says it is, and it is, and breaking it costs us the only thing we have that nobody can take off us, which is a sentence anybody can go up a hill and check.

"**And I am not going to say the fifth one because I have said the reader thing three times and I said on the eleventh of July that the fourth time was going to be in a different sentence and I am not going to say it a fifth time in any form at all, and I have not said it, and I would like it noticed that I have not said it.**"

Nobody said anything to that, and the shed was shut at about five because the light in this shed is one lamp and the lamp is oil.

---

**And on Sunday evening the two of them did the arithmetic on the back of a slate in the shed, and it is on the page in the first column, and it is checkable by anybody who can count.**

*That the cost of one day at the outer marks is, at this yard's own figures: the office's pot of air at this yard's own two-mark rate, two pots, four marks; the *Sallow Reach* at the figure its master named out loud in the doorway on Sunday the thirtieth of July and which this party has written down, six marks, of which the yard would pay at the mole as it pays every other man at the mole; and a day of the yard's own at no figure, which is a day of two people and a boat and no pot; and that is ten marks and no more and there is no victuals on it and no second pot on it and no allowance.

*And that this yard has three marks in a bag and four marks of a wage falling on Tuesday the first of August, and thirty-one on a note it has not re-written, and five marks of a compressor's gland on that note that it will not get rid of and cannot use, and a tin that has been empty since the twenty-eighth of April; and that ten marks is not in this yard and cannot be made out of the difference; and that the only way this yard has ever made ten marks is four days of work at about three marks a day, and that a day of work is a day it is not doing that.*

**And the note on the market wall says a vessel with people in her, inside the four hundred yards, has been pumping for nine days and had a list of four foot this morning, and this yard has three marks, and the tide gang is four men short, and there is no office in this harbor with a boat of its own since the evening of the twenty-eighth of April.**

**And nobody in that shed said out loud what the four days were for, and everybody in that shed had the same four days in their heads, and the four days are the second, the third, the fourth and the fifth of August, when the moorings of the third lay are shut for the examination of this mole's footing which the officer of this harbor authority entered in his own book on Wednesday the twenty-sixth of July at the cost of his own office with no money behind it and the price falling on the third lay and not on this party.**

**And nobody said it, and that is entered too, and the reason it is entered is that a yard of two and a half men who has been told a thing out loud in a market and has gone quiet about it for a whole day is doing arithmetic, and the arithmetic was not about air.**
