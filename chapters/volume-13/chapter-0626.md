# Chapter 626

*Thursday the Seventh Day of April, a Rota Day, the Hundred and Fortieth Time That Ladder Went Up With a Bucket on It at About Eight in the Morning, Two Men and Eleven Trips and Nothing Walked on, the Covers Back on It Before a Quarter Past Nine and That Butt Full to the Rim of the Second Hoop Until About Four in the Afternoon, the Name at the Head of That Rota Board Still Off It, a Man of About Fifty-Three With a Boat of About Fourteen Tons Saying One Thing Out Loud at About Two in the Afternoon at the Flat of That Bench Stone in About Nine People in About Three Hundred and Fifty-Eight Words About a Sheet With Three Columns on It and a Day Against a Name, and Nobody Agreeing With Him and Nobody Answering Him and Nobody Thanking Him and Nobody Saying He Was Wrong and His Eleven Words Still on a Page and Unanswered and No Figure Set Against Them, Three in the Held Column and Two in the Restricted and Four in the Dormant and Three Plus Two Plus Four Being Nine, Nothing Read Out of a Gap and No Figure Moved by One, the Gap Put Out Loud at About Half Past Five, the Bag at Twenty-Eight Pence, Being Two Shillings and Fourpence, the First Column at Three Hundred and Fifty-Nine and the Second at One Thousand Six Hundred and Sixty-Nine*

Thursday the seventh day of April, with that cistern at the back of the market charged before that market opened, and about nine people at the flat of that bench stone at about two in the afternoon and about nine in that shed at half past five.

**A Thursday rota with no wage Tuesday in it and no wipe in it. That cistern went up for the hundred and fortieth time in eleven trips by hand and was walked on by none of it, the covers were back on the butt before a quarter past nine, the water stood at the rim of its second hoop until about four in the afternoon, and the name at the head of that rota board is still off it. At about two this afternoon a man of about fifty-three with a boat of about fourteen tons said one thing out loud in about nine people about a sheet with three columns on it and a day written against a name on it, and nobody agreed with him and nobody answered him and nobody thanked him and nobody said he was wrong, and nobody in this yard is going to. His eleven words have been on a page in this harbor since the winter and have not been answered and no figure is set against them this morning or this afternoon. Nothing went onto the money sheet and nothing came off it, and a man with a book in one hand saying a thing about a page is not a line on that sheet. The day rate, a hundred and sixty-eight pence, arrived in that yard at ten and left it at four at that same figure, and twenty-eight pence, being two shillings and fourpence, was on that slate when the books of that day were shut. Behind a wage of four marks, being six hundred and forty pence, that leaves a hole of six hundred and twelve pence, being three marks eleven shillings. The sheet in question carries one hundred and eight thousand two hundred and seventy-six pence of outgo, being six hundred and seventy-six marks, nine shillings and eightpence, and ten thousand six hundred and forty pence of income, being sixty-six marks, six shillings and eightpence, and does not carry ninety-seven thousand six hundred and thirty-six pence, being six hundred and ten marks and three shillings. Nobody in this harbor has opened a tin in that shed since the twenty-ninth day of April past, and not one penny of this afternoon came out of that bag.**

**The nine went on again a day each and none of them is improved and none of them is in any room. That notice of the seventeenth of May past, three hundred and twenty-four days. The bellwright of this harbor, not asked for, three hundred and twenty-six. Two objects of nobody's on that crown, six hundred and sixty-four days. The bench under that pump, bare for the four hundred and thirtieth night. The two lights at about a mile off that point, two hundred and forty-eight days. The six-line sheet at the back of that end stall, its two hundred and third day. The paper of four lines in Fish Street, its two hundred and eighteenth. The nine-line sheet with a seal on it in that front street, its two hundred and thirty-fifth. A man of about fifty-one, two hundred and sixty-six days without a word to anybody about a page of six names. There are five papers on that board at the head of that mole and two of them have never been opened by anybody here. The four lines are behind a door in an upper room over that market with nothing written under the fourth of them, and the answer to them is no on four out of six with the fifth not asked. That basket in that boathouse stands with the paper inside it and nothing else inside it. About two sea miles off the head of that mole lie nine hulls this morning. A man of about twenty-nine can hear out of the left side of his head and it is one hundred and twenty-four days, one more than yesterday, and no figure is set against it and he is not on the water.**

---

That cistern went up the ladder at about eight in the morning, eleven trips by two men, and not one bucket of it was walked on and nothing went up that ladder on any other errand. The covers were back on the butt before a quarter past nine and it stood to the rim of the second hoop until about four in the afternoon with nothing drawn out of it, and that is a hundred and forty times.

The name at the head of that rota board is still off it and nobody in that market has offered to put it there and nobody has asked either of those two men about it, and that is not a thing this yard is going to change this month.

---

At about two in the afternoon a man of about fifty-three with a boat of about fourteen tons stood up at the flat of that bench stone in about nine people and said one thing out loud, and nobody had asked him for it and nobody has been asked anything by this yard in eleven weeks.

"**I keep a crossing column in one book in one hand and every hour in that book is in my hand and nobody else's, and I have said that in this yard once and it was not written down, and I am not asking anybody to write this one down either. I am saying it because nine names went onto a board in that shed in three columns this month and one of them changed its word yesterday in front of nine people, and I want to say the one thing I have got about it.**

A sheet with three columns on it and a day written against a name on it is the most checkable object this harbor has ever made and I am not going to stand here and take that away from anybody. **The trouble is not the three columns. The trouble is what a day against a name is for.** On my page an hour against a row means a man was asked and a man answered. On that sheet yesterday's word against that run means eleven men stopped going out. One of those two is a thing that was said and one of those two is a thing that happened, and this yard has spent eleven weeks teaching people the difference between a thing that was said and a thing that happened, and that board has it going the other way round.

**And here is what I have got and it is about this yard and not about her.** Whoever keeps that board has not asked a soul on this coast what state their run is in. She told him. He wrote it down. That is not the same act as her telling him, and one day this harbor is going to want to put a figure on a route and it is going to be able to do it in about four minutes without anybody in this yard going down that coast road at all.

**I am not saying she was wrong. I did not know one thing about that run and I would have said restricted too if it were mine.**"

That is about three hundred and fifty-eight words and it went to about nine people and about four of that market heard the whole of it and nobody agreed with him out loud and nobody answered him and nobody argued with him and nobody thanked him and nobody said he was wrong.

His eleven words are on a page in this harbor and have been since the winter and nobody has answered them and no figure is set against them.

---

A man of about thirty-three who hires hands was in that nine and had a thing in his mouth about four columns and got about six words out of it before about nine people looked at him, and he stopped where he stood and did not finish the sentence and did not start it again that afternoon.

About four people in that yard thought the man of about fifty-three had made a fair point and about four thought he had not and about one went and stood at the end of the boards on his own.

---

And at about half past five on that Thursday the man who keeps the leaf said the shortfall out loud at that bench stone, in about six hundred and four words, and nobody in that shed was asked which of those two readings of a day against a name was the right one.

"**A hundred and forty charges have gone past that cistern and the name at the head of that board is still off it, and eleven trips by two men before that market opened and nothing walked on and the covers back on before a quarter past nine and that butt up to the rim of its second hoop until about four this afternoon, and I am opening a slip with it because I said last Monday that I would stop and a man who stops on Monday is not a man who can be right about Monday.**

What I answer for on this slip stands at ninety-seven thousand six hundred and thirty-six pence, being six hundred and ten marks and three shillings. It is where the twenty-sixth of last month left it and it is not made to balance by asking this harbor for the difference. Twenty-eight pence lies under this slate, being two shillings and fourpence, and a hundred and sixty-eight pence came in at ten and a hundred and sixty-eight went out at four, and twenty-eight plus one hundred and sixty-eight less one hundred and sixty-eight is twenty-eight.

**A man with a boat of about fourteen tons said three hundred and fifty-eight words at that stone this afternoon about my own sheet and nobody agreed with him and nobody answered him and nobody thanked him and I am not going to answer him tonight.** That is the second time in three weeks that a thing has been said in that yard about a page in nine people and got no answer, and the first time it was a woman of about thirty-nine and the second time it was him, and both of those times the person who said it was somebody who had been here longer than me and was not here on somebody's errand this week.

**Here is the part of it that is mine and I will put it in my own mouth.** He says that a day against a name on that sheet means a thing that happened and not a thing that was said, and he is right about that, and I have known it since Wednesday afternoon and I have not written it down, and the reason I have not written it down is that the moment this yard starts putting words on that board about what a row means, this yard has begun keeping that board instead of carrying it.

And I will say the other half of it because it is my sheet and nobody else is going to say it. **If that board gets a meaning on it out of my own mouth, then in a year a stranger reads it and cannot tell whether I wrote the word or heard it.** That is the whole trouble with every page in this harbor and I have been writing it down for eleven weeks.

The fourteen at the end of that stall were not read out of and no figure on that sheet went up or down by one. The count of positions is not being taken again and the fifth word on the back of the other page is not a column.

One line went in on that first column at about six this evening and five went in on the second and I did not read that leaf out loud. Three hundred and fifty-nine stands against the first of those two figures and one thousand six hundred and sixty-nine against the second, my third is empty, and my fourth is at one hundred and twenty-five with no hundred and twenty-sixth line in it."

Nobody was thanked for the arithmetic and nobody was thanked for three hundred and fifty-eight words about a sheet. That shed was clear of everybody by about six with its door standing open on a slip with nothing on it, and the butt behind that market was still at the rim of its second hoop when it shut and the name was still off that board.

A MAN OF ABOUT FIFTY-THREE WITH A BOAT OF ABOUT FOURTEEN TONS SAID ONE THING OUT LOUD AT ABOUT TWO IN THE AFTERNOON AT THE FLAT OF THAT BENCH STONE IN ABOUT NINE PEOPLE IN ABOUT 358 WORDS, BEING THAT A SHEET WITH THREE COLUMNS ON IT AND A DAY WRITTEN AGAINST A NAME ON IT IS THE MOST CHECKABLE OBJECT THIS HARBOR HAS EVER MADE, AND THAT THE TROUBLE IS NOT THE THREE COLUMNS BUT WHAT A DAY AGAINST A NAME IS FOR, AND THAT ON HIS OWN PAGE AN HOUR AGAINST A ROW MEANS A MAN WAS ASKED AND A MAN ANSWERED WHILE A WORD AGAINST A RUN MEANS ELEVEN MEN STOPPED GOING OUT, AND THAT ONE OF THOSE IS A THING THAT WAS SAID AND ONE IS A THING THAT HAPPENED. NOBODY AGREED WITH HIM AND NOBODY ANSWERED HIM AND NOBODY THANKED HIM AND NOBODY SAID HE WAS WRONG, AND HIS ELEVEN WORDS ARE STILL ON A PAGE AND ARE UNANSWERED AND NO FIGURE IS SET AGAINST THEM. THE THREE COLUMNS OF THAT SHEET OF DAYS CARRY THREE, TWO AND FOUR, AND THREE PLUS TWO PLUS FOUR IS NINE. THE FOURTEEN SHEET WAS NOT READ OUT OF TODAY AND NOT ONE FIGURE ON IT MOVED BY ONE IN EITHER DIRECTION. THE FIGURE THAT MONEY SHEET IS SHORT BY STANDS AT NINETY-SEVEN THOUSAND SIX HUNDRED AND THIRTY-SIX PENCE, BEING SIX HUNDRED AND TEN MARKS AND THREE SHILLINGS; WHAT HAS EVER COME INTO IT IS TEN THOUSAND SIX HUNDRED AND FORTY PENCE, BEING SIXTY-SIX MARKS, SIX SHILLINGS AND EIGHTPENCE; AND WHAT IT HAS COST IS ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHT THOUSAND TWO HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-SIX PENCE, BEING SIX HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-SIX MARKS, NINE SHILLINGS AND EIGHTPENCE. WHAT IS UNDER THAT SLATE IS TWENTY-EIGHT PENCE, BEING TWO SHILLINGS AND FOURPENCE.**

*Second column, not checkable from a mole, entered on the said Thursday at about six in the evening in that shed, and no name is against it: that at about two in the afternoon the reason I have written down what a man said and not what I think of it is that he said it in about four and a half minutes and this slip is six hundred and four words and there is a kind of man in this harbor who can say a whole thing in a morning and a kind who cannot and I have been the second kind every day for eleven weeks;
