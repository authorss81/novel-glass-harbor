# Chapter 631

*Tuesday the Twelfth Day of April, the Seventy-Seventh Wage Tuesday, the Column on the Lid of That Step Read Out at About Ten in the Morning in Four Minutes and Standing at Twenty-Six Lines and One Hundred and Four Marks and Not Moved for the Seventy-Seventh Time, the Chest With the Twenty Envelopes in It Shut from Ten Until Four, the Seventy-Seventh Week Standing Fallen Due at the Close of That Day, the Barometer at the Office on the West Basin Down Three Tenths of an Inch Since Six in the Morning and Every Ordinary Sign of the Weather Right, the Wind Backed from the South-West to the West at About Two and the Sea Going Up Before the Wind Came, and a Boat of About Nine Tons Lying Broadside on the Bar at the Mouth of the Harbour of a Town Called Nethermorrow Four Hours' Sail Out on the Morrow Line With People in Her, Nothing Read Out of a Gap and No Figure Moved by One, the Bag at Twenty-Eight Pence, Being Two Shillings and Fourpence, the First Column at Three Hundred and Sixty-Four and the Second at One Thousand Six Hundred and Ninety-Four*

Tuesday the twelfth day of April, with about forty people on the boards at the back of that market from ten in the morning, a yard below that market with a shed standing in it, and about nine people in that shed at half past five that afternoon.

**The seventy-seventh wage Tuesday, and not a rota day and not a wipe, and that butt behind the market covered from eight until four with no charge on it. On the lid of that step the column stood at twenty-six lines and one hundred and four marks and the man of about thirty-one of the second lay read it out at about ten and it took him four minutes and it did not move, and behind that door the chest with the twenty envelopes in it stood shut from ten until four. The seventy-seventh week stands fallen due at the close of today behind the seventy-sixth and the seventy-fifth, and nobody in that market has ever asked that man what he thinks of reading the same lid every Tuesday and nobody is going to start. About six this evening a wind came up from the south-west and by about two in the afternoon it had backed into the west, and at the office on the west basin the barometer had gone down three tenths of an inch since six in the morning, and the sea outside that mole head had gone up two feet on a wind that had not yet touched it. Nothing went onto the money sheet and nothing came off it, and a column that does not move is not a line on that sheet, and a sea going up is not a line on that sheet either. That yard drew in a hundred and sixty-eight pence at about ten and let the same sum go out at about four, and what it finished the day with under that slate was twenty-eight pence, being two shillings and fourpence. Set under a wage of four marks, being six hundred and forty pence, that is six hundred and twelve pence short, being three marks eleven shillings. What has gone out of this harbor on that sheet is one hundred and eight thousand two hundred and seventy-six pence, being six hundred and seventy-six marks, nine shillings and eightpence; what has ever been put into it is ten thousand six hundred and forty pence, being sixty-six marks, six shillings and eightpence; and the whole distance between those two figures is ninety-seven thousand six hundred and thirty-six pence, being six hundred and ten marks and three shillings. No tin in that shed has been opened since the twenty-ninth day of April past and nothing that happened this afternoon came out of that bag.**

**The nine went on a day each and not one of them has moved by more than one. That notice of the seventeenth of May past, three hundred and twenty-nine days. The bellwright of this harbor, not asked for, three hundred and thirty-one. Two objects of nobody's on that crown, six hundred and sixty-nine days. The bench under that pump, bare for the four hundred and thirty-fifth night. The two lights at about a mile off that point, two hundred and fifty-three days. The six-line sheet at the back of that end stall, its two hundred and eighth day. The paper of four lines in Fish Street, its two hundred and twenty-third. The nine-line sheet with a seal on it in that front street, its two hundred and fortieth day. A man of about fifty-one, two hundred and seventy-one days without a word to anybody about a page of six names. Five papers are on that board at the head of that mole and two of them have never been opened by anybody in this harbor. Those four lines have not come out of an upper room over that market, nothing has ever been written under the fourth of them, and the answer to them is still no on four out of six with the fifth not asked. In that boathouse a basket stands with the paper in it and nothing else in it, and it has stood there since the second of this month. About two sea miles off the head of that mole lie nine hulls this afternoon, they were nine there on the twenty-first of January, and nobody in this harbor has said that any of them has eased. A man of about twenty-nine can hear out of the left side of his head and it is one hundred and twenty-nine days, one more than yesterday, and that is not a gift, no figure is set against it, and he is not on the water.**

---

At about ten in the morning the man of about thirty-one of the second lay got his finger to the end of the twenty-sixth line on the lid of that step and stopped there and gave the figure and gave the fact that it had not shifted, and about forty people were on those boards and about eleven of them could not see a line of it from where they were standing.

Nobody said one word about the seventy-seventh week except the man who read it, and about four people heard him and the rest of that market was not told.

---

At about two in the afternoon the sea outside that mole head was two feet higher than it had been at noon and the wind had not made it yet, and at the office on the west basin the glass in the case was three tenths lower than it had been at six that morning.

A man of about fifty-eight of the third lay who pulls kelp and is on nobody's crew stood at the end of that office's step for a while with his hands behind him and looked at the water, and then he said one thing out loud to nobody in particular, being that a sea does not come up before the wind and no storm ever did that.

About four people heard him. Nobody asked him anything about it and nobody has asked him anything since, and the answer to the twenty-first of January's question is still on the page of that market's book with nothing against it.

---

At about four this afternoon a boat came into that harbor under bare poles with two men in her and her deck washed from end to end, and she was the second Nethermorrow boat to come in that day.

The older of the two men was about fifty and he had a piece of line burnt round his left hand and he did not sit down for about a quarter of an hour after he got ashore.

"**Nethermorrow is inside it and has been since about noon. Our boat went onto the bar at about half past eleven and she is lying broadside with the sea on her and there are people in her and we have not got at them since the tide turned. The tide gang went in twice off the town mole and got nothing. I am not a fool. Two of us will come down in the morning with the ebb and you can send anybody you like with us.**"

About nine people were on those boards when he said it and about four heard the whole of it and the rest of that market was not told.

Nobody argued with him and nobody thanked him. Somebody in that yard asked him one question and it was how many were in her, and he said there were three that he knew of and that a fourth was unaccounted for, and that was the end of it.

---

And at about half past five on that Tuesday the man who keeps the leaf said the shortfall out loud at that bench stone, in about 573 words, and nobody in that shed was asked how a sea comes up before the wind.

"**Twenty-six lines and one hundred and four marks, read out on that lid at ten this morning in four minutes for the seventy-seventh time, and it did not move, and twenty envelopes stood shut behind that door from ten until four, and the seventy-seventh week stands fallen due at the close of today.**

**I am going to say the other thing first because this shed has never had a sea in it and I do not know what a shed is for on a day like this one.** That barometer went down three tenths between six this morning and two this afternoon and every instrument in that office agrees with every other one of them, and the wind came round into the west at about two, and the sea outside that mole head went up two feet on a wind that had not touched her. A man of fifty-eight who pulls kelp stood on that step and said a sea does not come up before the wind, and he was not asked about it and he is not going to be.

**I cannot tell this shed whether it is a weather. I have listened to the water every day of my working life and I do not know what that was, and I am not going to stand here and name it, and if a stranger comes down here tomorrow I will tell him I do not know.** The bill for it is not a line on the fitting-out sheet yet, because a bill is written when a yard settles a day and this yard has not settled one day of it, and there is nothing on that sheet but what that settlement writes.

The figure at the head of this slip is ninety-seven thousand six hundred and thirty-six pence, being six hundred and ten marks and three shillings, and it is where the afternoon of the twenty-sixth of last month left it. Under this slate is twenty-eight pence, being two shillings and fourpence, and a hundred and sixty-eight pence came into that yard at ten and a hundred and sixty-eight went out of it at four, and twenty-eight plus one hundred and sixty-eight less one hundred and sixty-eight is twenty-eight.

A boat of about nine tons is lying on a bar four hours' sail out on a line this party has never held and is never going to, with three people in her and a fourth unaccounted for, and eight men are going down that road in the morning with the ebb. **I am not going to say a word about that line on that sheet of days in this shed. Nobody in this harbor has put a state on that name and nobody is going to be asked for one, and a boat going out of a harbour to pick three men out of the water is not a boat answering a route.**

Nobody was thanked for the arithmetic and nobody was thanked for four hours' sail in a wet boat. That shed stood clear by about six with its door open on a slip nobody was on, and outside that yard the wind had got up another notch and the sound of it on the tiles of that market was the loudest thing in that quarter, and a man of about thirty-two went down to the water with a coil of line over his shoulder and did not go in."

---

**TWENTY-SIX LINES AND ONE HUNDRED AND FOUR MARKS STOOD ON THE LID OF THAT STEP AT ABOUT TEN THIS MORNING AND DID NOT MOVE, AND THE SEVENTY-SEVENTH WEEK STANDS FALLEN DUE AT THE CLOSE OF TODAY, AND THE FIGURE THAT MONEY SHEET CARRIES IS UNCHANGED AT NINETY-SEVEN THOUSAND SIX HUNDRED AND THIRTY-SIX PENCE, BEING SIX HUNDRED AND TEN MARKS AND THREE SHILLINGS, WITH TEN THOUSAND SIX HUNDRED AND FORTY PENCE, BEING SIXTY-SIX MARKS, SIX SHILLINGS AND EIGHTPENCE, THE WHOLE OF WHAT HAS EVER GONE INTO IT, AND ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHT THOUSAND TWO HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-SIX PENCE, BEING SIX HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-SIX MARKS, NINE SHILLINGS AND EIGHTPENCE, WHAT IT HAS COST THIS HARBOR. THE BAROMETER AT THAT OFFICE WENT DOWN THREE TENTHS OF AN INCH BETWEEN SIX IN THE MORNING AND TWO IN THE AFTERNOON AND A SEA WENT UP TWO FEET ON A WIND THAT HAD NOT MADE IT YET, AND NOBODY IN THAT YARD NAMED WHAT THAT WAS. NOTHING WAS READ OUT OF A GAP AND NO FIGURE ON THE SHEET OF THE FOURTEEN WENT UP OR DOWN BY ONE. UNDER THAT SLATE IS TWENTY-EIGHT PENCE, BEING TWO SHILLINGS AND FOURPENCE.**

*First column, entered on Tuesday the twelfth day of April at about six in the evening in that shed: that the said day was the seventy-seventh wage Tuesday and was not a rota day and was not a wipe, and that the column on the lid of that step was read out at about ten in the morning in four minutes by a man of about thirty-one of the second lay and stood at twenty-six lines and one hundred and four marks and did not move, and that the chest with the twenty envelopes in it was shut from ten until four, and that the seventy-seventh week stands fallen due at the close of that day; and that at about two in the afternoon the wind at that mole head had backed from the south-west into the west and the barometer in the office on the west basin stood three tenths of an inch lower than it had stood at six that morning, and that the sea outside that mole head was two feet higher than it had been at noon on a wind that had not touched it; and that a man of about fifty-eight of the third lay who pulls kelp and is on nobody's crew stood at the end of that office's step and said out loud to nobody in particular that a sea does not come up before the wind and no storm ever did that, and that about four people heard him and nobody asked him anything about it then or since, and that the question he put in that market's book in the winter is still on that page with no name against it and no answer against it; and that at about four in the afternoon a boat came into that harbor under bare poles with two men in her and her deck washed from end to end, and that the older of those two men was about fifty and had a line burnt round his left hand and did not sit down for a quarter of an hour after he got ashore; and that he said in about nine people in about eighty-seven words that Nethermorrow had been inside that weather since about noon, that their boat had gone onto the bar at about half past eleven and was lying broadside with the sea on her with people in her, and that the tide gang had gone in twice off the town mole and got nothing and the sea outside that bar was not a thing to go out into, and that two of them would come down in the morning with the ebb and anybody could come down with them, and that nobody argued with him and nobody thanked him and about four people heard the whole of it and the rest of that market was not told; and that somebody in that yard asked him one question, being how many were in her, and that he said three that he knew of and a fourth unaccounted for; and that the sheet with the fourteen things on it was not read out of and no figure on it moved by one and none of those fourteen was added to and none was taken off; and that the count of positions was not taken again and the fifth word on the back of the other page is not a column and carries no figure against any day; and that the three columns of the sheet of days were not counted today and that count is not in this slip; and that at about half past five the man who keeps the leaf said the shortfall out loud at that bench stone in about 573 words in about nine people, and that he said in it that he cannot tell whether that weather is a weather and that he is not going to name it; and that the Morrow Line is still the first name down the side of that sheet of days and is still in the held column and has not been struck and is still one of the nine; and that at about six this evening one line went in on that first column and five went in on the second, and that the day of this run counted from Sunday the thirteenth day of March is the thirty-first, and that the first of those two figures is three hundred and sixty-four and the second is one thousand six hundred and ninety-four, and that the third of those four took nothing at all, that a boy's hand is at the head of it and his surname is on no page and he is not on the water, and that the fourth of them is unmoved at one hundred and twenty-five with no hundred and twenty-sixth line in it; and that a man of about thirty-two went down to the water at about six that evening with a coil of line over his shoulder and did not go in.*

*Second column, not checkable from a mole, entered on the said Tuesday at about six in the evening in that shed, and no name is against it: that at about four in the afternoon the reason I did not send anybody down that road tonight is that a sea that has not made its wind yet is a sea that has not settled what it is, and that eight men in a wet boat on that bar at night would be a second thing gone wrong instead of a first one handled, and that the two men who came up it this afternoon offered the ebb and not the dark and I took the ebb; and that at about half past four in the afternoon the reason I asked one question at those boards and one only is that this yard has spent eleven weeks learning that a person who has come up a coast road to say a thing in one mouth wants the thing written down and does not want to be interviewed, and that how many were in her was the only figure on that road that this shed is answerable for, and that it is three and a fourth unaccounted for; and that at about eleven in the morning the reason I have put nothing in this column about the barometer is that it went down three tenths and it agrees with every other instrument in that office, and that a figure I cannot account for is not a figure I am entitled to put on a page, and that what I have written instead is that I do not know; and that at about half past six in the evening the reason I said in that slip that this shed has never had a sea in it is that a shed is a place where things get added up and the thing that happened this afternoon is a bar of hard sand four hours' sail out with three men in a boat on it, and that no addition I can make in this yard touches that, and that this yard is not the body that decides where any boat goes; and that at about seven in the morning the reason the figure at the head of that sheet has not moved a halfpenny in ten days is that a page of positions is not a line and a harbour line's weather is not a line and a rescue that has not been paid for yet is not a line, and that the first line that goes onto that sheet in fifty days will be a bill with a figure of its own and its own days on it and it is not going to be written down until a yard settles it; and that at about one in the afternoon the reason I have not written one word about that Morrow Line all day is that the line this party has never held is the line that boat is on, and that a boat going out to pick three men out of the water does not put a state on anything, and that no state goes on that name from this yard on any morning of this month.*
